# MK163 — Book Orange: Author-established macroscopic outline

Status: AUTHOR-SELECTED WORKING MACRO OUTLINE; detailed chronology, scenes and chapters OPEN.
Scope: Book Orange, seven arcs named Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Violet, White (no numeric arc names in narrative presentation).
Source: Author decisions in conversation, 2026-11-20. This package has not been pushed to GitHub.

## Inherited context and conventions
- Book Red ends with Kaira's King's Tournament team placing fourth / Green and her receiving the Dimensional Ring.
- Kaira is a Support Specialist, a Cardsmith and chainmail robe enterprise owner. The Accord Registry supplies dungeon contracts and already manages robe wholesale/retail logistics.
- Political domains Green, Blue and Violet are territories of the kingdom in the White/Material Realm, not the separate chromatic Green and Orange Realms.
- Princess illi (lowercase) is Kaira's close personal friend, does not govern a domain, and can represent the Crown diplomatically. Princess aina (lowercase) governs the Blue Domain.
- Spellings: Kaira, illi, aina, Vaelith, vaelum, Accord Registry, Black Sprite.

## Red Arc
- Following the King's Tournament, Kaira's tournament vaelum disbands. It was formed for the tournament, not intended as a permanent team; some members are recruited by more prominent vaelum. Individual destinations OPEN.
- Kaira, Princess illi and the Black Sprite travel to the native Green Realm. illi acts as a Crown liaison. They interact with Sprites and the realm's ruling body. Green Realm has non-ordinary geometry and vast scale; specifics OPEN.
- During the journey illi convinces Kaira to relocate her primary residence to the Violet Domain capital to be closer to her.
- Kaira relocates and expands her chainmail robe enterprise into the Violet capital: a small rented Yellow District assembly shop with eight trained blacksmiths. Existing kingdom-wide central banking and established enterprise/Registry arrangements remain in effect. No new rent, wages, sales or net profit fixed.
- After return, Red Arc ends with Kaira participating in the King's fifth-day raeon card game. The King and attendees discuss a rising cartel targeting nobility for kidnapping and ransom. The cartel is focused on ransom, NOT slave trafficking.
- Example case: a Green Domain noble's daughter was kidnapped; her ransom was PAID and she was returned. This is one of multiple kidnappings. No rescue mission for this victim; older rescue/off-continent trafficking variants are SUPERSEDED.

## Orange Arc
- Vaelith dignitaries from the Orange Realm visit the kingdom's Violet Domain capital because they have heard of Kaira and her Black Sprite and recognize the beginnings of the Black Prophecy.
- They meet the King, who confirms knowing Kaira and the Black Sprite. The Vaelith already have a Black Vaelith and are assessing whether they agree to a bond with Kaira. Kaira learns of the Black Vaelith and meets the dignitaries. They depart. No bond yet.
- Kaira also participates in several dungeon delves as a Support Specialist and performs well. Contracts/teams/dungeons/results beyond this are OPEN.
- The earlier placement of illi's Green Domain kidnapping investigation in Orange Arc is SUPERSEDED: it belongs in Green Arc.

## Yellow Arc
- Kaira travels to the Orange Realm, bonds with the Black Vaelith, and returns to the kingdom accompanied by the Black Vaelith as a member of her traveling party.
- Exact bond mechanism, companions on journey and duration OPEN.

## Green Arc
- Princess illi is assigned Crown duties investigating the repeated kidnapping/ransom cases in the Green Domain.
- Kaira accompanies her as a close friend and member of her royal entourage. The noble's daughter previously kidnapped has already been returned after ransom payment.
- Investigation findings and individual encounters OPEN.

## Blue Arc
- illi's Crown investigation continues throughout the kingdom; the entourage may stay in Green or Blue Domain cities, exact itinerary OPEN.
- Kaira travels with illi and has free time during official work. She returns more fully to dungeon delving, taking local Support Specialist jobs through the Accord Registry wherever they are staying, without joining a permanent vaelum.
- The dungeon work need not be connected to the investigation. Contracts, destinations and results OPEN.

## Violet Arc
- illi's investigation traces the flow of intelligence to a still-unnamed city in the Green Domain.
- A Yellow-ranked member of the lower nobility is identified as an informant collaborating with the ransom cartel. Their access to noble circles allows them to gather information about nobles and identify suitable kidnapping/ransom targets for the cartel.
- The noble's identity, city, evidence, and precise investigative methods OPEN. Do not assume the noble controls the cartel.
- The discovery provides the lead for the subsequent operation against the cartel.

## White Arc
- An all-out operation against this specific ransom cartel is launched and the cartel is dismantled. Specific forces, tactics, leadership, timing and outcomes beyond dismantling OPEN.
- Its removal creates a power vacuum that competing cartels begin filling. Kidnapping and ransom do not end. This does NOT mean the entire criminal underworld is defeated.
- Book Yellow's separate broader trafficking/slavery/piracy developments remain distinct; do not retroactively make Book Orange's ransom cartel a slave-trafficking organization.
