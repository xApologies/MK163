# MK163 — Book White (Book Seven) — Author-selected macro outline

**Status:** CUMULATIVE AUTHOR-SELECTED MACRO, 2026-11-21. The seven arcs below have established narrative directions, but details remain open. Do not invent dates, scenes, characters, government structures, or the mechanism of planetary stabilization.

## Book-level premise
Book White closes the seven-book series. Book Violet was the international tour dismantling Cabal footholds; Book White **must not repeat a kingdom-by-kingdom itinerary**. The multinational war against the **Cabal of Maegi** and slave-trading states continues in parallel while Kaira follows a more focused investigation, then explores naturally occurring **velis** in the six chromatic realms. Kingdoms **25 and 29** are the Cabal's two historically collaborating principal operating kingdoms; both permit slavery. Kingdom 29 contains a Cabal mountain stronghold. Cabal influence on the continent surrounding Kingdom 27 is longstanding (more than a millennium), but Kingdom 27 itself has resisted a successful foothold.

## Red Arc — arrival at the Great Tree and attempted infiltration
Kaira, Nyx and her Black-bonded companions travel from **Kingdom 1 through the Green Realm to Kingdom 27**, using the Green Realm's unusual geometry as a long-distance bridge, **not a ship voyage**. Nyx is a native of Green Realm and can enter it; bonded Black companions have Kaira-specific cross-chromatic travel eligibility, but Nyx does not acquire universal travel eligibility. Other members of the traveling party, including illi if present, and logistics are not exhaustively assigned.

Kingdom 27's capital is organized around/within an enormous **approximately five-mile-tall Great Tree**. Kaira and local authorities investigate Cabal attempts to penetrate the kingdom. They establish that the Cabal **has no successful foothold there**, despite active infiltration attempts. Facing international pressure and failure to gain control of Kingdom 27, the Cabal **changes tactics** and kidnaps members of ruling/royal and noble families, including from **Kingdoms 27 and 28**, seeking to transport captives toward its stronghold. Formal governmental titles are provisional. The precise kidnapping scenes and victims remain open.

A Great Tree aerial racing festival using six chromatic checkpoints and bracelet activation was **brainstormed only**: event, name, rules, mechanism, and Kaira's participation are **NOT CANONIZED**.

## Orange Arc — pursuit to Kingdom 29
Kaira's group investigates and tracks the Cabal kidnappers and captives from Kingdoms 27 and 28 toward the **mountainous Cabal stronghold in Kingdom 29**. Intelligence, pursuit route, allies, encounters, and captives' fates remain OPEN. This is a focused pursuit, not another broad country-by-country Cabal campaign.

## Yellow Arc — Cabal defeat, lethal extraction sigil, ancient research
The **Cabal of Maegi is defeated**. The pursuit culminates in confrontation at its mountain stronghold in Kingdom 29; exact military/political resolution and fate of individual Cabal members remain OPEN. The coalition/Kaira's group discovers a **large constructed sigil** used to forcibly extract Genesis energy from a living person placed inside it, **killing the person**, and storing that extracted energy in Genesis crystals, producing **overcharged Genesis crystals**. The Cabal's use of slavery and captive sacrifice is linked to this technology. Golems were mentioned as a **possible** application of overcharged crystals, not a selected use. No specific glyph geometry, activation procedure, extraction rate, energy capacity or other implementation is established.

A trove of Cabal research accumulated over centuries is recovered: old scrolls, books, ordinary historical paper, later maege paper, instruments and other records. Some of the research hints at a possible **Genesis Field structural approach** relevant to planetary stabilization. The Cabal is not assumed to have already completed that solution. Its archives supply clues Kaira must investigate.

## Green Arc — natural velis: Green and Yellow Realms
Kaira visits the **Green and Yellow Realms** to investigate **naturally occurring velis** and their Genesis Field structuring, drawing on recovered Cabal research. Natural velis are distinct from Architect-created, maintained velis and from physically inscribed ru’ne/sigils. Specific observations and discoveries remain OPEN.

## Blue Arc — natural velis: Violet and Orange Realms
Kaira visits the **Violet and Orange Realms** to continue observing naturally occurring velis. **Orange** is the selected second realm, **not Yellow**; earlier duplicate Yellow reference is superseded. Exact findings OPEN.

## Violet Arc — natural velis: Blue and Red Realms
Kaira visits the **Blue and Red Realms**, completing the intended six-realm natural-velis investigation. Exact observations and final preparation remain OPEN.

## White Arc — local planetary Genesis stabilization
Kaira applies the understanding developed through Cabal records and natural velis investigations to perform an extraordinary **planetary-scale Transduction**, synchronizing/stabilizing the **local Genesis Field associated with her planet**, **not the starscape**. The intended final outcome: no further Genesis storms, Genesis collapses, Domai formation, or monster threat. How preexisting ELDRIS/monsters disappear or are otherwise resolved, the mathematical/magical structure, energy budget, execution, and scene realization remain OPEN. **No seventh Black bond** is introduced.

## Terminology and authority
- **velis** (V-E-L-I-S): localized Genesis Field topology/anchor. An Architect deliberately creates/maintains one; **naturally occurring velis** are distinct and historically informed development of ru’ne/sigils (author-selected worldbuilding; reconcile with AERA rather than silently editing upstream).
- **vaelum**: adventuring group/organization, NOT velis.
- **Maegi** (M-A-E-G-I): Cabal of Maegi, NOT 'Magi'.
- **maege** (M-A-E-G-E): protected technology/material spelling for maege glass/paper. Preserve exact established repository names; avoid conflating with Maegi.
- Kaira, illi, Nyx, Vaelith, ELDRIS, Domai, aen are protected spellings.
- Black bonds maintain independent agency. Nyx is not one of the six Black bonds.
