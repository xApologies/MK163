## Author interpretation (controls over guesses from artwork)
- **Northern continent above Eastern Whirlpool:** **11 aqua-colored city markers** total. **Exactly five white-X cities** indicate Cabal footholds. **Six others are not identified as Cabal-occupied**. Red arrows trace the five-city investigation, successive intelligence links and Kaira's return.
- **Yellow route:** Violet capital → Green capital by King's Highway → naval voyage to southwestern continent. White-circled purple-X capital is base; yellow arrows radiate to operations and return.
- **Green route:** follow-on southwestern Cabal location.
- **Blue route:** Leviathan-assisted flight to southern continent and campaign from its capital.
- **Purple route:** subsequent bottom-right Cabal location.
- **Pink route:** represents **White Arc**, colored pink only to distinguish it from white X marks.
- **Far-right pink X:** **independent allied-kingdom coalition** operation that dismantles a Cabal foothold; NOT Kaira's destination, NOT necessarily White Arc, and NOT the same as the pink White Arc route.
- Colored paths and map labels are author guidance; **do not infer exact mileage, dates, borders, governments, unnamed cities or transit speeds** from pixels.
