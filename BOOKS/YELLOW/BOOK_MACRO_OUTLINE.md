## Red Arc — Return and preparations
Kaira returns to the capital of the Violet Domain following Book Orange. She checks on her chainmail robe enterprise and undertakes some local dungeon delving at the capital-associated dungeon. Preparations for a five-year World Summit take place. Kaira's companions include Nyx (now fully grown, still her mount), the Black Sprite, and the Black Vaelith.

## Orange Arc — World Summit
Kaira accompanies Princess **illi** (lowercase), the King, Queen and royal entourage to the summit, hosted this cycle on the large World Tree continent identified on the author's world map (specific country/city not yet named). The summit recurs every five years and rotates host nations. Approximately six or seven major nations/kingdoms/republics of the White/Material Realm are represented, not every nation. Delegates from each of the six chromatic realms attend as dignitaries representing their own realms: Red Dragons, Orange Vaelith, Yellow Primordials, Green Sprites, Blue Leviathans, Violet Unicorns. They do not govern White Realm nations.

Agenda: international trade, tariffs, cartels, pirate networks, drug trafficking, slave trafficking and possible cooperation among kingdoms. Cartels are distributed across continents; no single world cartel is established. Pirates have an international transport/trafficking role. This summit introduces Kaira to the international scale of these issues, not a direct pirate confrontation. Kaira interacts with Dragon dignitaries, providing the link to Yellow Arc.

Interrealm travel rule selected by author: White Realm humanity can travel to each chromatic realm; chromatic natives can travel between their own realm and White, but cannot ordinarily enter other chromatic realms because of structural energy incompatibility. Native chromatic peoples may adopt humanoid form for interaction in White. Do not equate this with free chromatic-to-chromatic passage.
