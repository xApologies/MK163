# Green · Blackstone and the royal raeon table

**ID:** MK163-RED-ARC-004 · **Book:** Red · **Status:** outlined (author-selected arc beats; chapters and scene realization OPEN).

## Narrative objective

Establish Kaira as a trained human Cardsmith and develop her friendships with the Princess and King.

## Entry state

The Alchemist has sponsored admission and housing; Nyx is rideable and the independent Black Sprite accompanies Kaira.

## Principal conflict

Kaira must develop technique through years of study and navigate unfamiliar royal company; natural insight does not confer mastery.

## Major events

## Academic and professional development
- Kaira studies Cardsmithing at Blackstone; years pass and she graduates older, with high honors or an equivalent academic distinction. Exact official distinction title is OPEN.
- Her previous armorsmithing/Chainmail Robe success provides substantial wealth. She can afford a serious competitive raeon deck and accumulated Card collection.
- She plays raeon strategically and tactically with unusual natural aptitude, develops a completed deck, joins local tournaments and games at Blackstone, and repeatedly defeats the Princess, who is her close friend.
- The Black Sprite's native understanding of Genesis-field topology at the **Resolution** level offers Kaira unusual insight into the topology of Genesis Cards. She still must learn Cardsmith technique and is NOT automatically a Master Cardsmith.
- She leaves with many basic student-crafted Cards, exercises and models, analogous to a serious undergraduate's technical portfolio; her *commercial* Cardsmith reputation remains unestablished.



## First meeting with King
- The King hosts regular, relaxed raeon gatherings with generals, military advisers, strategists, nobles, counselors, financial advisers and other invited guests. The gatherings permit informal political and military conversation, drinks and friendly competition, not a formal council.
- The Princess is frustrated that she seldom defeats Kaira at raeon. Knowing her father's competitive nature, she brings Kaira to one gathering, greets her father, sits Kaira down, and mischievously leaves her there on the **first night**.
- Kaira is intensely anxious at being unexpectedly seated with the King and influential guests, but **wins four out of five games**. The exact game-by-game order is OPEN.
- The King extends a **standing invitation**. The Princess often joins subsequent games; she does not usually abandon Kaira.
- The King develops a friendly obsession with defeating Kaira, who wins more often than not and becomes a kind of ringer.
- On selected nights he **stacks the guest list**, not the literal card deck, inviting military tactical strategists to play and analyze Kaira. Do not imply cheating.
- Kaira's friendship with the King grows through raeon and through her friendship with his daughter.
- The King becomes increasingly aware of Kaira as an anomaly: Awakened Afflicted, Chainmail Robe originator, extraordinarily good raeon player, accompanied by Nyx and the Black Sprite. His exact knowledge of her deeper identity remains OPEN.
- Future military special-project significance of strategists observing her is **foreshadowing only**; no military service or later-book plot is authored here.

## Royal challenge coin
- The King awards Kaira a personal **challenge coin** representing the recurring raeon invitation and granting **tax-/duty-free use of the King's Highway** to reach the Crown's capital for games from her later city.
- The coin is NOT an appointment, noble title, general royal security clearance, or automatic access to military installations. Exact design and verification remain OPEN.
- Preserve the distinction between the Blue Domain capital (Blackstone's location) and the Crown's capital/King's residence; do not merge them without source evidence.

## Exit state

Graduates with high honors and student work, without an established commercial Cardsmith practice. The invitation and personal coin continue.
