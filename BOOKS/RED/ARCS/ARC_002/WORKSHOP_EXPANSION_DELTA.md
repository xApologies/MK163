Kaira is UNMISTAKABLY Awakened: healthy white skin/hair retaining formerly Afflicted pigmentation, steel-gray irises faintly luminous, subtly enlarged orbital sockets/eyes, exotic symmetrical angular face, lean dense musculature, prominent battle scars. She is healthy and no longer pathologically Afflicted. All social classes, including nobility/royalty, can biologically inherit Affliction. It is rare genetic condition, not a class trait. Urban people are generally educated and understand Affliction, less superstitious than frontier Village Hub; many are indifferent rather than hateful. Her phenotype still identifies her past condition.
Awakening Potion costs extreme wealth due to White Genesis Dust and Crown-influenced supply; nobility are primary purchasers, not exclusive. Noble circles eventually hear and ask which family, patron, Crown connection and political relationships; do NOT assume all nobles sinister or every commoner resentful.
