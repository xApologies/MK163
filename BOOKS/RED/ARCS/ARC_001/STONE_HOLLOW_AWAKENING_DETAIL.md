# Arc One Chapter Three — Stone Hollow: risk, healing, extraordinary Awakening
This is a detailed author-level event design, not final prose. It supersedes older chapter placement where the collapse and healing were in chapters three/four.

## Arrival and immediate care
At the end of chapter two Kaira has walked or stumbled from the Genesis collapse on the Oak Hollow→Stone Hollow road, in a traumatized and disoriented fugue. She is severely injured, bloodied, and carrying an overfull pack of crystals, a strange black crystalline object and carefully wrapped black mushrooms. She collapses within sight of the guards outside Stone Hollow. The local alchemist is a resident healer. She has access to ordinary healing potions in low grades, a smaller number of medium-grade ones, and perhaps one or two high-grade. Kaira is administered healing potion while unconscious. Healing potions rapidly accelerate the body's own biological repair, powered by Genesis energy, not by magically erasing history. Deep injuries close and form prominent scars; the process is painful and traumatic even if Kaira is unconscious for most of it. She awakens with residual aching, scar tightness and a battered appearance. Specific permanent scars: left temple above eye down cheek, right abdominal puncture, long left thigh gash, and three severe claw marks diagonally from right shoulder across back toward left hip, once torn nearly to spine. These are not optional cosmetic flourishes; they persist as part of her public identity.

## The alchemist's workbench and recovered materials
When Kaira wakes, the alchemist is at a workbench with the backpack contents arranged: Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue and Violet crystals organized by color; several White crystals; black crystalline object whose identity she cannot explain; cloth unwrapped to inspect unfamiliar black mushrooms with subtly glowing internal Genesis channels. The alchemist is intellectually curious, especially about the unusual mushrooms, but fear dominates. Kaira believes the black crystal is a vaen; author knows it is the stasis form of the Black Sprite lost from Green Realm. No one in Stone Hollow has reason to recognize the prophecy.

The guards tracked Kaira's blood trail backward and discovered dead expensive beast, mangled mass-reduced metal cart, ruined armor and crystal fragments. They may pocket valuable colored fragments and conceal what they saw, but the alchemist realizes tiny chips of White crystal may remain at the site. That residual evidence is terrifying: anyone who knows White crystals were produced there could attempt to trace them to Kaira and then the alchemist. White crystal is wealth so extreme that an opportunistic group might kill Kaira, the alchemist, or even erase the small village's witnesses. This fear is the central reason for the alchemist's decision. She does not rescue Kaira out of pure generosity. She is a moral enough person not to steal the whole bag, but survival is her primary motive. She cannot simply seize the White crystals, because possession itself makes her an obvious target. The goal is to eliminate dangerous White material as evidence by consuming it in an alchemical preparation.

## Potion construction and transaction
The alchemist knows a normal Awakening Potion is made from organized Red-through-Violet Genesis crystal energy and White superposition. The exact formulation and proportions remain open. Kaira possesses MORE White crystal than the normal recipe would use. To eliminate all evidence, the alchemist devises an unusually White-heavy preparation. The author proposes using black mushroom vaen as additional structuring components to allow more White dust to be incorporated and consumed. This is a story-specific exceptional formulation, not a universal standard recipe. The White crystals are processed to fine dust; a vessel contains fluid; chromatic crystal anchors and material/vaen anchors on a sigil table provide energy and structure. The alchemist controls the Transduction through staged activation. The final potion stores an organized Genesis transformation in ingestible liquid form.

Kaira is put into unconscious sleep because undergoing bodily and neural reconstruction awake would be traumatic. The White Awakening remakes her Genesis-channel biology and integrated brain/body physiology. Her Afflicted pathology is cured, but she retains white hair, pale skin and steel-gray eyes; they are healthy rather than brittle/chalky. Her eyes have faint internal luminescence; her orbital sockets/eyes slightly enlarge; features become angular/symmetric, body leaner and stronger, height may modestly increase. The scars from the immediately preceding ELDRIS fight remain. She awakens feeling extraordinarily healthy, despite scar tightness and the shock of change. AERA general biology owns the mechanism; MK157 old narrative transformation is NOT imported.

Seven mushrooms collected in chapter two. Three consumed in the extraordinary potion; one transferred to the alchemist as payment for treatment; three remain Kaira's. This is an explicit arithmetic inventory, not 'maybe a few'. Alchemist has legitimate interest in one unfamiliar vaen specimen. Kaira retains her black stasis crystal and other chromatic crystals. The alchemist burns Kaira's bloodstained garments as evidence and gives ill-fitting clean clothes. Some red crystal is exchanged at an unfavorable rate (about 60% of value was floated, not a locked kingdom rate) for coins (ahm), food/rations and basic necessities. Kaira has backpack, remaining mushrooms, crystals, Black crystal, and whatever maternal items she has continuously carried; do not conjure discarded clothing back into her inventory. She is frightened of returning to the smith because the beast, cart and armor were destroyed. The smith is a respected regional supplier with power over her; she fears he might kill her and face little more than a fine. This fear drives her flight.

## Scene tension and knowledge boundaries
The alchemist knows enough to fear White material and to recognize its alchemical value, but does not know Kaira caused the collapse or that the black object is a prophecy-linked Sprite. Kaira does not know she destabilized the field; the author does. The guards do not know how the White material was used. The reader should experience the alchemist's fear through behavior, the organized workbench and urgent questions, not a lecture about royal monopoly. The exact guard response, dialogue, alchemist's name and potion animation are not fixed. The point of the chapter is a transaction of mutual survival with a transformative outcome, not a sentimental rescue.
