## Region, mother, smithy, poverty
Kaira is 23, born Afflicted; typical afflicted life expectancy 11–17, oldest reported 37. Her mother moved to Crown-sanctioned developing Village Hub ~30 years ago as a Cardsmith seeking opportunity, one of a few regional Cardsmiths. Crown-contracted specialists have higher pay, tax benefits, and steady services; independent merchants fill growing demand. Father was passing aithren, short relationship, absent/unknown. Mother taught Kaira Cardsmith theory, mathematics, vaen harvesting. Mother died when Kaira 15; family savings for extremely expensive Restoration Potion depleted, tools/cards sold cheaply. Kaira kept mother's THREE mathematics/Cardsmithing books and low-quality Fireball Genesis Card. Smith takes her in around 16; she works to 23 as effectively indentured laborer. Sleeps near banked forge with treasured blanket, threadbare clothing, meager dinner (saves part for breakfast), no lunch unless self-funded, minimum wages and deductions for damaged work. Smith sees her as productive tool; several other smiths, at least two treat her as human. He has profitable Crown armor maintenance contract and dungeon repair trade. Shop organizational example: owner, five working smiths, two apprentices; three smiths allocated to Crown contract and two to independent delvers, not necessarily final staffing. Apprentices make chainmail rings/weave by hand; master smiths use Thermal/Metal Genesis Cards. Kaira exceptionally competent at repetitive chainmail work but no combat training.

Kaira knows local vaen and Genesis-expressing flora from mother's Cardsmithing needs. On armor-delivery route she works efficiently to free unsupervised hours for gathering moss, fungi, vaen and other requisition-board resources. Sells at apothecary/alchemist/vaelum-related local buyers, sometimes at unfair discount due to prejudice, secretly saves ahm toward hopeless Restoration Potion. Vaen are local field-topology-dependent recurrent manifestations, NOT newly invented by this arc; inherit upstream. She loves gathering living vaen and later collects living plant specimens for her apartment.

Village Hub region radius 20 miles, central half-mile-square civic hub to become White District of 7×7-mile small city after 60–80-year development. Hollowmere Green dungeon (#6) separate satellite staging area (roughly 1–2 miles square). Crown guards patrol roads, protect from roaming ELDRIS, deter bandits, supply armor repairs. Villages/sites are organized crown installations, not ramshackle camps. Exact mapped circuit:
Village Hub → Riverbend (#2) → Green Hollow (#5) → Oak Hollow (#3) → Stone Hollow (#8) → North Ridge (#7) → Green Hollow (#5) → Village Hub.
Earlier route that placed Hollowmere as direct delivery stop is superseded. Day 1 hub→2→5, Day 2 5→3→8 planned, Day 3 8→7→5, Day 4 5→hub return. Collapse interrupts Day 2 about two-thirds from #3 to #8. Route distances previously estimated: Hub→Riverbend 6.6 miles, Oak Hollow→Stone Hollow 9.4 miles, Hub→Riverbend 2h38m at loaded cart pace; 5→3 ~3h, 3→8 ~3h46m; other segment figures should be recovered from earlier map calculation, not invented. Injured, concussed/fugue-like, fearful of smith's destroyed beast/cart/armor, limps to Stone Hollow (#8) and collapses in view of guards. Chapter Two ends there.

## Chapter Three — Stone Hollow alchemist
Kaira wakes in residential alchemist's building after healing potion accelerated traumatic wound closure, leaving severe tight painful scars. Alchemist organized recovered bag: chromatic crystals, White crystals, black anomaly, mushrooms. Guards traced blood trail to destroyed cart; remaining White chips at site imply dangerous discovery. White crystals are generational-scale wealth and could motivate lethal silencing of anyone with knowledge. Alchemist is primarily protecting herself, not selflessly rescuing Kaira, but does not rob her. She devises White-heavy Awakening Potion to consume dangerous evidence, using Red→Violet crystals, ALL recovered White crystals processed to dust, and three black mushrooms as stabilizing vaen to accommodate unusual White quantity. Exact sigil/alchemical recipe remains open. Transduction through table/sigil into fluid; Kaira put to sleep during traumatic biological restructuring. Her pathological Affliction cured but white skin/hair and steel-gray eyes retained; hair healthier, lean athletic frame, enlarged orbital eyes, facial symmetry, faint internal eye glow; scars remain. No evidence of large White crystals except chips at collapse site. Guards may quietly pocket chromatic crystals; exact action not forced.
Mushroom inventory: seven gathered − three consumed − one given alchemist for healing/Awakening service = THREE Kaira retains.
Alchemist burns bloodstained clothing, provides ill-fitting clean clothes. Trades some red crystal at unfavorable (~60% value illustrative) for ahm, food/rations, clothes. Kaira leaves with backpack, crystals, three mushrooms, Black crystal, mother's card/books as retained possessions subject to physical inventory reconciliation. She fears smith's reaction.
