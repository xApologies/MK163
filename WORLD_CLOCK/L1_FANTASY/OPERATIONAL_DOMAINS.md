# Six operational domains — not colors, professions, or lifecycle stages

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L1_FANTASY/OPERATIONAL_DOMAINS.md`

The inherited operation categories are:

1. Persistent Topology / Coherence–Decoherence: stabilizing, repairing and sustaining organization versus degrading or destabilizing it. Healing is a major application but is not the whole category.
2. Dimensional Geometry: geometrical/dimensional relations and their constraints.
3. Genesis Field Topology: direct organization within the Genesis Field.
4. Genesis Expression: organized manifested Genesis capability.
5. Resonance: coupling, oscillatory organization, reinforcement and sustained interactions.
6. Chirality: organizational grammar, orientation, admissibility and related structural interaction.

No Red=Coherence, Orange=Dimensional, etc. one-to-one assignment exists. A class may use several operation categories. An Augmenter can combine coherence and resonance; a helkir focuses coherence; an Architect may use field topology and dimensional geometry. Ordinary Decoherence is generally less practical/efficient than Coherence in the source; exceptional Black story applications elsewhere are not universal.

Seven prophetic concepts also are not an exhaustive list of professions. Warden and Cartographer were proposed by the assistant and rejected as redundant professions. Their defensive and spatial capabilities remain inside support maegi and Architects respectively.
