The first seven describe foundational aspects of magic. The eighth is a separate foretelling of a singular manifestation. The denizen associations are selected interpretive correspondences, not identity equations between basins, professions, lifecycle stages and prophecies. Secondary associations can remain. Framework documents use plural Covenant Gardens while the manuscript is singular The Covenant Garden; preserve this naming difference until the author selects a uniform form.

MK163 is the inaugural seven-book realization of the AERA framework selected by the author. All eight prophecies will be realized in the narrative; that is a series-level commitment, not permission to import MK231's old Phoenix Egg, Kitsune companion, Black Sprite replacement scene or prior character chronology. The Black Prophecy belongs to Kaira's narrative identity, while her Afflicted biology remains the ordinary condition.

Black is not a separate eighth inhabited realm. White is the seventh material realm resulting from the sixfold superposition. The six realms can also be energy domains; neither use invalidates the other. Book colors are independent narrative ordering and must not be assigned to denizens or prophecies solely for diagram symmetry.
