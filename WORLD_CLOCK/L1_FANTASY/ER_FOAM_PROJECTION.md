# ER Foam — spatially locked tactile topology

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L1_FANTASY/ER_FOAM_PROJECTION.md`

L0 retains MK Ultra/API 4.3 ER Foam mathematics. L1 introduces the authored fantasy use: a Genesis-field projection whose geometry is spatially locked/stabilized in place and physically interactive. It feels like firm/hard rubber: some elasticity, not highly elastic or a soft spongy material.

The surface can be flat, a button-like region, a continuous sheet or a raised topographic distribution. It can be positioned above a material substrate rather than being identical to the substrate. Touching it is a real tactile interaction, not merely looking at projected light.

The associated construct/sigil network performs the programmed effect. ER Foam need not itself be the whole light or water system; it supplies the interface. A table's projected relief map can have palpable contour and depth. This does not make the display omniscient or automatically live-updating without information sources.

The road example extends tactile support to a traversable/load-bearing surface. A stone road is fully carved with a sigil network; activation projects the ER Foam a couple of inches above it. Traffic contacts the projection, not the stone. This is a permitted inherited application, not a declaration that every MK163 road uses ER Foam. Exact load capacity, maintenance cost, friction, elasticity curve and projection thickness are unassigned.
