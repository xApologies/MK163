# Shared fatigue, basin access and cross-interface constraints

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L1_FANTASY/FATIGUE_AND_CROSS_INTERFACE_CONCURRENCY.md`

Fatigue is an endurance-like physiological decline in access and sustained output, not a replenishable numerical mana pool. It accumulates by basin across native work, Bindings, cards, and aen maintenance/expansion/throughput. Losing access through exhaustion prevents further compatible use until recovery. Exact recovery times and equations remain unset.

The dumbbell analogy: holding a heavy curl becomes increasingly difficult; the arm lowers; eventually the hand cannot hold the weight. Likewise a practitioner first loses sustainable performance, then access. This does not assign one fixed endurance duration to a color. Single-basin chip charging times are production benchmarks, not universal fatigue limits.

Raw Red energy is compared to holding a raindrop; raw Violet to holding a storm. Higher-basin direct control is generally more demanding. A Genesis Diamond organizes incoming energy into a manageable distribution, so drawing stored energy can reduce the control burden regardless of charge color. This is access to a reserve, not an automatic reset of existing fatigue.

Installed Binding bandwidth, card-exclusive basin occupation, aen throughput, native control skill, and physiological endurance are distinct constraints. Do not collapse them into slots or a shared invented point meter.

Examples: native Red plus native Red can be valid if capacity permits; active Red+Orange Card blocks a Red+Green Card and native/Binding Red use; it does not by itself block Blue+Violet native use or Yellow+Green cards. Activating a Red Card after a native Red Shaping has already started remains a procedural open question.
