# Genesis Cards — operational manifolds, basin occupation, and skill

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L1_FANTASY/GENESIS_CARDS.md`

An operational Genesis Card encodes a manifold in maege glass. The user puts personal Genesis energy into it to activate/couple, then draws the required basin energy **through the card** and directs the Shaping. The author's current rule is prepared lifecycle automation, not zero operator skill.

Every required basin is exclusively anchored for that practitioner while the card is active, across every interface. A card requiring Red, Orange and Green leaves Yellow, Blue and Violet available but blocks other Red/Orange/Green-dependent cards, native Shapings, Bindings and aen. A manifold needing any unavailable required basin cannot be completed through those basins. Six simultaneous ordinary cards is the theoretical maximum only when each is single-basin and all six use different basins; it is not a guarantee of anyone's endurance.

Inactive cards do not reserve basins. Native concurrent same-basin Shapings are not prohibited merely because card concurrency is restrictive. The order of activating a card against an already-running conflicting Shaping is not resolved: no automatic cancellation or priority rule is invented.

Card operation causes physiological fatigue. Idle maintenance is a small trickle; burst and sustained effects have different draw patterns in the inherited source. A multibasin card fails when a required basin's access is exhausted. Cards can be carried, placed or suspended nearby; separation can weaken coupling, and cards can be damaged. Exact ranges and failure behavior were not fixed.

A novice can produce the encoded fireball and still miss. The golf-slice analogy preserves aim, trajectory, timing and practice as operator competencies. Better aiming is not an invented card power upgrade. aen cannot boost the card's conduits and cannot simultaneously use its occupied basins.

These are also collectible/tradeable commodities: ownership, collection and activation are separate. MK163 selects operational cards that can also be collected, not a false exclusive choice between casting and collecting. Collectible-only AERA variants may exist but do not acquire casting ability by being traded. raeon is a separate field card game, not a spellcasting-card catalogue. The referenced 1,245-Shaping catalogue has not been fully instantiated into MK163; no individual spell list is fabricated.
