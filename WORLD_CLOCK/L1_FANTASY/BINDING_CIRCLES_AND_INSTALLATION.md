# Binding Circles — physical sigils, resource anchors, and installed architecture

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L1_FANTASY/BINDING_CIRCLES_AND_INSTALLATION.md`

A physical Binding Circle is an engineered sigil and identity-resolution scaffold. It can select suitable identities from compatible inputs and resolve them into a person or object. vaen are identity-bearing resources, not the sole possible input. An input may carry more than one identity.

The author diagram establishes designated resource anchors, a target anchor, and geometric ru’ne pathways. The central violet mark is the target position in the example, not a mandatory Violet energy requirement. A person stands there or an object is placed there. Other points take vaen, Genesis crystals or compatible inputs. Multiple crystals may occupy a designated point, with one crystal color per point in the described arrangement. Different points can carry different colors.

Genesis Dust traces/fills the required pathways; not every marking must be filled. Exact requirements depend on the intended Binding. The three resource categories are vaen, Genesis crystals and Genesis Dust, combined as the operation requires. Activation consumes designated inputs and resolves the effect into the target. Neither mandatory six-anchor symmetry nor a fixed circle boundary was specified. Squares, rectangles and other engineered boundaries are possible.

The physical circle performs installation. The installed reusable Binding is a distinct object of personal architecture. Its active PSSP manifold is another representation again. Subsequent casting does not require reconstructing the original large physical circle. Object enchantment uses related fabrication but retains its Aineth/Ain/Aenith terminology and object identity.
