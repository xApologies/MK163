# Neon configuration and material Starscape realization

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L1_FANTASY/NEON_AND_STARSCAPE_EQUILIBRIUM.md`

Each colored basin has a reference equilibrium marker k/7: Red 1/7, Orange 2/7, Yellow 3/7, Green 4/7, Blue 5/7, Violet 6/7. Their repeating decimals are approximately .142857, .285714, .428571, .571429, .714286 and .857143. These reference markers are not a complete state equation.

Neon describes bounded departures toward 0 or 1 from a basin's reference. Direction matters: Red has more upward span toward 1, while Violet has more downward span toward 0. An Orange coordinate near .3 is the author's small-departure example. Do not identify larger numerical coordinate with universally greater Neon intensity across all colors.

A White singularity forms from a compatible superposed Neon configuration. Starscapes generally do not have all six basins exactly at their fractions. The author described the perfectly aligned case as unusually inert, with an energy effect deliberately deferred. Ordinary nonzero Neon is not a universal catastrophe. Global Starscape coherence can persist while local conditions are prone to collapse.

Kaira's Starscape has an unusually high Yellow coordinate, about .87 versus its 3/7 reference. This is the chosen environmental pressure permitting episodic runaway Genesis collapse, storms, domai, eldris, and altered flora/fauna. Do not claim the White singularity has failed or that its whole universe is continuously collapsing. The specific genetic relationship to Affliction is separate and remains proposed in AERA.

Different White-superpose configurations can support different internal physical makeups. The narrative concerns Kaira's own Starscape, not an obligation to enumerate every alternative universe. No formula for collapse rates, storm probability, or full six-coordinate configuration was established.
