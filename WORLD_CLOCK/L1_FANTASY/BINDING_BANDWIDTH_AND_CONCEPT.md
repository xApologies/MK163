# Binding bandwidth, normalized output, and conceptual efficiency

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L1_FANTASY/BINDING_BANDWIDTH_AND_CONCEPT.md`

Installed Bindings occupy persistent portions of finite personal Genesis bandwidth, even when idle. People differ in available capacity: one may accommodate six particular Bindings, another those same six plus another. There is no universal slot count. The same percentage-cost illustration is not automatically a common absolute capacity across people.

CSR means **Continuous Spatial Resolution** in MK163. It is locally bound spatial updating/control supporting flight and is an example of a bandwidth-heavy Binding. AERA's roughly 60% case is illustrative, not a locked universal cost. Earlier “Controlled” wording is superseded for this novel, not an open naming question.

The Binding provides prepared manifold construction and holding. The practitioner's concept controls how efficiently and reliably that geometry is realized. With a weak concept, injected energy may fail to stabilize the Shaping, escape into the environment, and produce no fireball. The attempt still expends effort and generates fatigue. A highly realized concept can approach ideal use of the prepared structure.

Mastery does not automatically make a fixed Fireball Binding more powerful. It improves energy economy, reliability, control and the number of successful castings the practitioner can sustain. The author's two limiting examples are total failed manifestation and ideal efficiency; no measured percentage curve was established. Source artwork/assistant graph percentages are not canon.

Installation/removal mechanics, universal bandwidth equations and the cost of individual Bindings remain unspecified. They must be recovered or authored, not filled in by multiplying rank numbers.
