# Six foundational basins and the seventh White superposition

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L1_FANTASY/CHROMATIC_DOMAIN_STRUCTURE.md`

The foundational colored energy structures are Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, and Violet. In MK163 the generalized AERA domains are also realized as inhabited structural realms. They can therefore be encountered as an energy source and as a realm without those two descriptions being identical.

White is recognized as Domain Seven and the Material Realm. It is the coherent superpose of all six colored domains, not a seventh independent energy source of the same type. Calling White the seventh realm or seventh chromatic position is correct within the author's classification. The warning against a seventh independent basin must never erase White from the model.

The Black Prophecy is about Kaira. Black is not an Eighth Realm. The seven-book order Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Violet, White is an ordering convention for books; it does not constrain each volume to its namesake basin or denizen.

The seven foundational prophecies and Black foretelling are inherited. The Seven Realms prophecy concerns the entire six-plus-White domain structure. Other prophetic correspondences express chosen relationships, not equations identifying a race, basin, operation category and lifecycle stage with each other.
