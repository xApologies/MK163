The carbon remains diamond and the energy remains Genesis energy; a color/optical change is not literal transmutation into ruby, emerald or amethyst. Energy introduced from one or more basins is reorganized rather than merely remaining an undifferentiated mass of the charging basin.

The author uses quicksort-like sorting and chirality occupancy as the analogy. Adding raw Violet does not instantly make the diamond a Violet charge-state. The diamond organizes lower-to-higher average energy density while conserving stored energy under its source model. The eight-position/four-occupied-site illustrative average is preserved in the L0 scope record; it is not the complete microscopic algorithm.

Nine source states are retained: Empty clear; Infrared/sub-Red frosted/clouded; Red clouded red/frosted-ruby appearance; Orange warmer and clearer/citrine-like; Yellow topaz-like; Green emerald-like; Blue sapphire/near-clear; Violet very clear glass/amethyst-like; Ultraviolet nearly invisible except refraction/distortion/lensing. “Predator camouflage” is the visual analogy for the UV state, not a stealth spell or an extra basin. The source describes increasing translucency/clarity at higher charge; simple gemstone names do not replace those details.

The common personal diamond chip is 1/4 inch on each edge. The source includes 1–4-inch Binding-circle-scale diamonds, about one-foot infrastructure blocks, and larger strategic units. Size affects application; no universal volume/capacity equation is invented.

Stored organized energy is comparatively easy to draw/control, like holding a raindrop regardless of the diamond's color, unlike raw high-basin “storm” handling. It can supplement fatigue-limited direct basin access and support sustained use, personal reserves, Binding energy and infrastructure. It does not itself erase physiological fatigue or automatically grant a missing manifold.

Diamonds are valuable energy commodities that may serve as payment or reserves, but are not ahm coinage. A house can be sold for Violet diamonds; a particular buyer/seller may accept only chips. The energy utility and transactional market value are both retained.
