# Aineth, Ain and Aenith — enchanted equipment engineering

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/TECHNOLOGY/ENCHANTING_AINETH.md`

**Aineth** is the enchanting process/discipline. **Ain** is an individual persistent enchantment or identity/property modification. **Aenith** is the object possessing one or more Ain. Through Aineth an Ain is installed, making the object an Aenith. These terms are not aen cutaneous channels or vaen resources.

Prepared fabrication puts an object into a specialized sigil/Binding scaffold with proper materials/resource anchors and activates the operation. Designated resources are consumed for the coherent system. Direct Architect fabrication is a second inherited route through consciously engineered topology and labor, not necessarily the same consumable recipe. Both obey the Genesis lifecycle and admissibility.

Four combinable configurations are retained: intrinsic persistent property without continuing activation; permanently installed activation-dependent capability; finite energy reservoir using either a suitable Red–Violet crystal or charged diamond; and chromatic conduit using crystals as basin anchors. A device may combine several configurations. Turning off an effect or emptying a reserve does not erase its persistent Ain. No fixed four-features=four-activations pricing law exists.

Armor examples include thermal negation/fire resistance so a fireball does not heat the metal until it harms its wearer. These are proposed equipment applications, not a universal armor immunity or a full combat-stat catalogue. The chainmail robe's low effective weight is selected, but exact mass/inertia mechanics and all future enchantments remain unspecified. Optional material stress/cooldowns are not mandatory identity erasure.

The author's AE/AI lexical inheritance is retained. Do not globally derive every word's meaning from one prefix or replace Aineth with Aenith as the process, which was earlier provisional wording.
