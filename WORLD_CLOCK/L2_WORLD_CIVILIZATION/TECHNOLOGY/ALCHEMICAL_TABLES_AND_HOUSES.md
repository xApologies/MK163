# Alchemical tables, proprietary research and manufacturing
Status: author-approved working design; AERA L1 technical synchronization pending.

Table is durable stone, carved as one integrated permanent sigil, configurable into modes for healing, restoration, Awakening, material infusion, etc. No one-table-per-potion requirement. Alchemist can own multiple complementary tables. Seven primary crystal anchor positions R/O/Y/G/B/V/White (White superposition, not independent chromatic basin), supplementary vaen anchors and separate vessel/target anchors. Larger professional tables have larger crystal seats and more vaen capacity; small ones have fewer. Exact sizes/counts/modes open.

A mode selectively establishes energy corridors, anchor relationships and stabilizing field topology, while projecting tactile ER foam controls. ER foam is phase-locked sigil projection, physically touchable, firm-medium rubber feel with slight elasticity; examples include 2×2-inch household switch, hot/cold faucet interface, tactile map. On table, switches and faders control activation and energy transfer rate. Crystal fixes available energy budget; fader changes timing/throughput, not intrinsic total energy. Music-studio analogy: red 50% and green ~two-thirds may be desired, full/full can be wrong. Percentages are illustrative, not proven linear Genesis laws. Complex alchemy may take ~one hour of active adjustments; table does not replace operator expertise. Sigil geometry imposes active topology. No arbitrary unconstrained transformations.

Correct Transduction does not automatically erode carved sigils. Excess throughput, incorrect activation sequence or destabilization can damage anchor/corridor inscriptions locally; not necessarily explosion. Architect/runesmith repairs material geometry, alchemist diagnoses process. A century of service was floated as example, NOT fixed expiry.

Ordinary independent alchemist makes general recipes; alchemical houses may employ 10–100 alchemists and internal architects/runesmiths. Proprietary assets include ingredient ratios, vaen, sigil topology, control timing, efficient consumption, table design and execution knowledge. Houses can guard recipes in vaults, develop custom tables and conduct competitive research. Industrial espionage/trade-secret theft plausible, NOT universally criminal or mandatory plot. In-house architects protect secrets that external table fabricators might learn.

Standard table manufacturing exists as a general kingdom-scale Transduction equipment industry; two or three large broad-line producers proposed, count/name open. Stone fabrication→architect/runesmith inscription→manufacturing→professional customer. Some produce maege glass or other apparatus. Genesis crystals are mined/harvested energy materials, may be reduced to dust for rune channels; DO NOT invent separate crystal-specialist profession. Table price, mode catalog and manufacturer identities OPEN. Do not copy AERA mechanics into MK163.
