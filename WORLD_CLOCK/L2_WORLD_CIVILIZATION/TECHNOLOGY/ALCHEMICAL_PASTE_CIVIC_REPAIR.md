# Alchemical paste and civic maintenance
Status: author-approved MK163 working material, AERA synchronization needed.
Alchemical paste bonds to matter and assumes substrate's material properties/identity. Smear into cracks, broken stone wall, damaged carved sigil channels or damaged alchemical table; cured substrate can be recarved. Paste restores MATERIAL, not missing mathematical sigil information. Qualified architect/runesmith restores original geometry and validates operation. Controlled activation required for permanent bonding; exact activation procedure OPEN.

Made by skilled alchemist on Transduction table using expensive Genesis crystal recipe. All-six chromatic and R/O/G were speculative examples, NOT fixed formula. Resource cost prohibits casual production; ordinary children cannot manufacture buckets without costly ingredients, expertise and table. Restricted possession/distribution to certified professionals and authorized civic maintenance. Unauthorized possession serious offense, fines and possible imprisonment; 'one month' illustrative, not law. White civic noble may regulate production/distribution; actual licensing structure open. A few licensed producers possible; may overlap equipment manufacturers, undecided.

Durable stone sigils rarely need routine repair but accidents, overdriven alchemical channels, physical vandalism and deliberate paste filling justify civic crews. Stolen warehouse buckets could be misused by pranksters to disable some inscriptions. Larger arrays may tolerate local damage; exact fault-tolerance/anti-vandalism mechanism requires recovery. Civic fountain vandalism was hypothetical illustrative scene, NOT canon Arc Two event. No claim of invulnerable or self-aware material.


## Delta 005 detailed case qualifications

### Theft and realistic civic incidents
Control is not perfect. A few adolescents could steal three buckets from a warehouse and use the paste to fill grooves on civic stonework. They could disrupt a fountain or other public installation. This is a plausible background event in a large living city, not an approved Arc Two plot point. Its value is that it introduces ordinary vandalism, guards, complaints, repairs and municipal budgets. It makes a clean city feel inhabited by imperfect people rather than immune to human behavior.

Some sigil arrays may tolerate local scratches or partial channel obstruction because the overall topology is redundant or resilient. The author stated that a detailed anti-vandalism architecture had been worked out in earlier material, but it was not recovered in this workshop. Therefore do not claim every scratched ru’ne disables the entire network or that every array is invulnerable. The exact threshold of failure remains an AERA engineering question.

### Case: damaged alchemical table
An alchemist overdrives an anchor by setting the Red and Green energy controls too high or activating them in the wrong order. The Transduction becomes locally unstable and damages a ru’ne channel or anchor seat. The table ceases to perform its intended mode reliably. The alchemist identifies the failed process. An authorized runesmith applies alchemical paste to restore the substrate, re-carves the damaged portion of the sigil, and tests the table. This is a repair cycle, not an automatic total table replacement. Proper operation need not cause chronic erosion; accidental overdrive explains repairs despite durable stone.

### Case: wall damage
A vandal smashes an exposed stone block carrying part of a sigil. A maintenance crew assesses whether the active array remains stable, isolates the damaged segment if needed, fills the missing stone with paste, restores the carved geometry, and validates the utility. The surrounding wall can remain in place. This explains how a city made of standardized phase-grown blocks remains maintainable over decades or centuries.

### Industrial consequences
A small number of authorized alchemical producers may manufacture paste in bulk. They may be specialized alchemical houses or broader Transduction-equipment manufacturers; this is not yet decided. Cost, storage and professional access generate a market for licensing, supply contracts, repairs, auditing and insurance-like risk arrangements if later developed. No new company name, patent law, price list, or mandatory separate 'paste guild' should be invented. Civic maintenance is one of the three approved new city-map functions and should be linked to White civic authority and distributed service crews.
