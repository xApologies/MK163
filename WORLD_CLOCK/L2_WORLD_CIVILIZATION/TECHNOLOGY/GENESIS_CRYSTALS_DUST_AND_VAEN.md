# Genesis crystals, Genesis Dust and vaen — distinct engineering resources

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/TECHNOLOGY/GENESIS_CRYSTALS_DUST_AND_VAEN.md`

Genesis crystals retain chromatic identities Red through Violet. They can grow in suitable mines/dungeons and appear in storm/domai environments. Special superposed White crystals can serve domai structural cores; this does not establish White mines or a White dungeon stratum.

Crystals have engineering roles as selected energy/material inputs, finite reserves, or compatible basin anchors/conduits. Do not universally equate every crystal with a diamond battery: a conduit and a finite reservoir are different configurations. The author describes crystals as more costly than diamonds, with substantial importance for high-value fabrication such as dimensional devices.

Genesis Dust is recovered when eldris are destroyed and can be produced by alchemists breaking down crystals. It is used selectively in sigil pathways, Binding and enchantment fabrication, materials and other processes. White Genesis Dust can arise in the domai resource system, but an old potion recipe is not automatically defined by its presence.

vaen supply unusual Genesis-relevant identities/properties, often through biological/environmental field interaction. They may be combined with crystals and dust. A resource carrying multiple identities needs admissible identity selection rather than assigning every trait indiscriminately to a Binding.

These commodities have separate exchange value in relation to eln, ahm and diamonds. No complete prices, mandatory conversion ratio or yield formula was established. Their actual source formations and intended uses must remain visible in records rather than collapsing them to “magic resources”.
