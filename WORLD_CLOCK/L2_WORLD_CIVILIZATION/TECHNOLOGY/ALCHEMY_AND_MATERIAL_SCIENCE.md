# Alchemy and Type 1–5 material science

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/TECHNOLOGY/ALCHEMY_AND_MATERIAL_SCIENCE.md`

Alchemists are material scientists guiding Transduction with sigils and phase-field projections, physical apparatus and suitable resources. Their work includes potions, Genesis-infused metallurgy, crystal-to-dust processing, agricultural media and other industrial materials; potion making is not their entire discipline.

The inherited five-type material hierarchy distinguishes ordinary periodic-table chemistry (Type 1) from higher Genesis-mediated material organizations (Types 2–5). The workshop's recovered approximate discovery estimates included Type 2 about 80%, Type 3 about 40%, Type 4 about 10–20%; no exact Type 5 discovery percentage is established. These numbers are fictional research-status estimates, not exhaustive catalogs. The assistant's 15% midpoint graph is not a new exact Type 4 rule.

Genesis-infused Type 1 iron may retain its underlying material identity while acquiring enhanced durability, hardness, strength and resistance to brittleness/corrosion. Infusion does not automatically make it Type 2. The recovered Type 2 illustration combines helium-4, iron and Green Genesis Dust through a sigil to produce a stable liquid-iron-like material; preserve it as the source's fictional example, not ordinary chemistry or a universal alchemical formula.

The author's water/iron/calcium beaker example expressly used made-up ingredients to explain apparatus placement, not a locked recipe for a red healing potion. Resources are consumed as appropriate while materials undergo controlled transformation. Not every ru’ne path needs dust. Staged activation allows guidance rather than a single unexplained all-purpose mixing action.

Alchemists supply enhanced stock to blacksmiths, nutrient-rich media to agriculture, and Genesis Dust processed from crystals to other engineers. vaen is a resource-identity designation, not identical to a Type 2–5 material classification. The old MK147 exclusion of Awakening Potions does not control MK163, which explicitly inherits the AERA Awakening mechanism.
