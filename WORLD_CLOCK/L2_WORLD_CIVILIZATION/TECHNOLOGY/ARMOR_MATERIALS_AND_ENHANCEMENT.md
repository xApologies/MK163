## Live Model 007 integration

### Supplied rings and enchantment boundary

Kaira's purchased rings are **Genesis-infused iron**, elastic and self-closing, with durable/rust-resistant material characteristics, shape recovery and demanding tolerances. Those properties belong to material/design selection. Their **only selected enchantment is mass reduction**: no added fire, magic or impact resistance, free protective multiplier or immunity follows. Customers may seek separately chosen enchanters. Bulk remains; lower mass alone does not guarantee flexibility, stiffness, opacity or drape. No precise ring dimensions, wire gauge, total weight or enchantment implementation is selected.

### Offset-Lattice demonstration-shirt concept

**Offset-Lattice Weave** is Kaira's author-approved proprietary design concept, mentally developed during years of smithy labor without drawings. Intended benefits are dense coverage, controlled flexibility, alternative load paths/distributed tension and close fit, compared conceptually with European 4-in-1/6-in-1. Tradeoffs include tighter tolerances, complexity, more labor/material and difficult repair. These are design intent, not demonstrated physical superiority or universal protection.

The supplied abstract connectivity graph has primary **P(i,j)=(i,j)** and secondary **Q(i,j)=(i+1/2,j+1/2)**; each interior Q connects to **P(i,j), P(i+1,j), P(i,j+1), P(i+1,j+1)**. This graph does not solve physical threading: crossings, interference, over-under order, diameters and elasticity remain **UNVALIDATED**. It is not a proof or a new L0 physical law.

### Separate five-layer robe development

The robe is a separate genuinely three-dimensional interlayer weave, with about 250,000 rings as a planning estimate. Its exact interconnected topology remains **OPEN**. The five layers are not fixed independent sheets and no five layer-specific functions have been approved. Kaira must develop workable construction during Arc 2. [The robe event owner](../../L3_CHARACTERS_EVENTS/EVENTS/CHAINMAIL_ROBE_AND_AGE_OF_KNOWLEDGE.md) governs the opaque gray-black fitted athletic silhouette, substantial hood, controlled drape/no flare and garment-first intent. The concept image cannot establish physical topology, a European weave, hardened steel, protective effects or movement restrictions.

[Arc 2](../../../BOOKS/RED/ARCS/ARC_002/ARC.md) owns scrap practice, hand-assisted opening/orienting/threading/releasing rings and gradual individual-ring proficiency. [The economy owner](../ECONOMY/ARMOR_AND_CHAINMAIL_PRICES.md) owns order allocation and price. Existing ordinary mycelium armor and general optional enchantment configurations remain unchanged.
