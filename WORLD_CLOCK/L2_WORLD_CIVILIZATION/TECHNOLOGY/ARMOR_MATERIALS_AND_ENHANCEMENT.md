# Armor materials and optional enhancement

**Status:** AUTHOR-ESTABLISHED MK163 working reference; qualifications below apply.

Ordinary leather armor is animal-free cultivated fungal mycelium rather than hide. Velis-supported agricultural growth fields accelerate cultivation; growth rates, processing yields, manufacturing steps and resource costs remain unassigned. This material selection does not remove fauna such as Nyx, prohibit all animal husbandry, or select every textile's composition.

Ordinary and premium armor can receive compatible sigil engineering, persistent Ain, advanced alchemical materials and Genesis-resource inputs. AERA retains the Type 1–5 material model: Type 1 ordinary chemistry, Types 2–5 Genesis-mediated materials. Vaen are separate identity-bearing resources. These are available ingredients rather than mandatory components of every suit or Card. No invented all-six-basin Restoration formula follows from their availability.

The four inherited, combinable enchantment configurations are intrinsic, activated, finite reservoir and chromatic conduit. They are not rarity tiers. Enchantment costs remain separate and no universal enhancement price, recipe or defense multiplier is established. Layering ordinary mail under full leather is allowed; it does not confer immunity. Premium artistry may increase price without automatically increasing defensive performance.
