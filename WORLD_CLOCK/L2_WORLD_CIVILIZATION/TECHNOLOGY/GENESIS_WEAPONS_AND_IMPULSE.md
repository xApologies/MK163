# Genesis-engineered heavy weapons and impulse geometry

**Status:** AUTHOR-APPROVED conceptual architecture and representations; field implementation and illustrative values retain their explicit limits.

## Selected conceptual architecture and open realization

Massive war mauls, oversized swords (including six-to-seven-foot examples) and large war axes can use reduced experienced handling inertia and gravitational load, with a distinct impact-recoupling function. Personal augmentation may complement the equipment. Inertial decoupling during handling with impact recoupling and optional amplification is the accepted conceptual design. Reaction and recoil management are necessary.

Attachment or anchoring to the Genesis Field or Shield as the means of apparent mass displacement remains a **working hypothesis**, not an established topology or proven mechanism. No universal reduction percentage, recipe, activation latency, reservoir arrangement, energy cost or performance limit is fixed. Gravity/load reduction and inertial reduction are separate controls. Simply declaring a larger impact mass does not supply energy or momentum. “Impulse transduction” is working vocabulary. This is an L2 realization record, not a new L0 law or rewrite of AERA/_bricked.

## Preferred geometric representation

Impulse is the vector displacement in momentum space:

`J = p_final - p_initial = Δp`.

For a sufficiently isolated combined system:

`Δp_weapon + Δp_target + Δp_GenesisField + Δp_wielder/surroundings (if applicable) = 0`.

A closed vector polygon is the preferred geometric visual language. A three-term closure is valid only where omitted external interactions are negligible or already included in the field term. Target impulse is its own momentum change, not automatically the maul's initial momentum. Field-supplied energy/momentum must balance the full weapon–target–field–wielder system.

## Classical calculus cross-reference

`J = ∫_(t_initial)^(t_final) F_net(t) dt = Δp`; `F_avg = J/Δt`.

For constant mass and ordinary mechanics: `p = mv`, `E_kinetic = (1/2)mv²`. Geometry and calculus are parallel descriptions. Geometry does not remove conservation requirements, and these formulas alone do not solve the hypothetical field coupling.

## Illustrative 300-pound maul — not universal parameters

Physical mass ≈ 136 kg; experienced handling inertia ≈ 13.6 kg; speed 10 m/s. Under the simplified comparison, the reduced-state momentum and energy are 136 kg·m/s and 680 J; full-mass equivalents are 1360 kg·m/s and 6800 J. Differences are 1224 kg·m/s and 6120 J. A field-mediated equivalent must account for these differences rather than obtain them for free. Actual outcomes also depend on contact geometry, duration and recoil; no damage multiplier is locked.

The author approved retaining both geometric and calculus descriptions. Exact field coupling, reaction management and energy/momentum budgets remain unresolved. No new combat scene, Kaira equipment acquisition, physical proof or upstream amendment follows.
