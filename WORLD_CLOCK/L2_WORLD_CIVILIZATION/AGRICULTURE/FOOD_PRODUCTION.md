# Genesis-assisted agriculture and the predominantly vegetarian food economy

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/AGRICULTURE/FOOD_PRODUCTION.md`

Architect growth-field velis and Alchemist-enriched soil operate together: faster development raises nutrient demand, and the enriched growing medium supports rapid uptake. Suitable crops can yield multiple harvests per season. The author's three/five/seven examples contrast with an ordinary single seasonal harvest; they are not identical growth laws for every plant.

This supports plentiful plant-based food, clean cities and a predominantly vegetarian diet. Roving eldris and exposure outside protected settlements make extensive livestock acreage difficult and expensive to secure. Livestock can be lost before reaching slaughter age. The author compares the price of an ordinary meat meal to very expensive specialty beef in Earth terms; no exact price or real-world economic conversion is specified.

Beasts of burden for caravans and other transport remain useful. Specialty livestock and meat consumption can be associated with wealth and noble households. Predominantly vegetarian does not mean all people are biologically unable to eat meat, nor that there are no domesticated animals.

The inherited agricultural design uses **seventeen agricultural slices with thirteen crops each**, preserving redundancy and staples. The named source categories are greens, grains, roots, fungi proteins, legumes, aromatics, herbs, orchard fruit, berries, nightshades, dense calories, salads, plant fats/oils, preservation/fermentation, sweeteners, spices and emergency crops. The full 221-item crop catalogue was referenced but not transcribed in this thread; do not invent the missing crop names. Fantasy trade names are prose-facing while real-world parentheticals in sources are author translation aids.

Natural Genesis-expressing flora, vaen, ordinary crops, and Alchemical Type 1–5 materials are different classifications. A crop accelerated by a velis is not automatically a novel Type 3 material or a vaen resource. Local farming can support developing Village Hubs and permanent cities; no new footprint or universal yield is assigned.
