# Agricultural royal legacy — workshop addition
A past unnamed king funded large-scale agricultural research as a defining civilizational contribution. Eldris and unstable Genesis collapses constrain remote farms, extensive grazing and herds; people consolidate in stabilized cities and Village Hubs. Architects establish growth-field velis that accelerate crops; alchemists create enriched nutrient-dense soil to support the increased uptake. Both needed, not nutrients from nothing. Illustrative 3/5/7 harvests per season depending on field/crop, rather than one; no universal fixed yield. Combined with sigil water, this produces cheap abundant predominantly plant-based food and reduces famine risk. Livestock remains rare/costly but exists (working animals, noble protected herds, specialty meat). Name/reign of king OPEN. The 17-slice/13-crop preexisting agricultural framework remains inherited, not duplicated.

City map keeps explicit Crops, Livestock, Trees, Groceries, Restaurants, Open Market, Industry (Mass Production) and newly approved Localized Food Storage and Distribution. Processing may occur in Industry, then food distributed to local stores and markets. Do not introduce unnecessary new farming mechanism.
