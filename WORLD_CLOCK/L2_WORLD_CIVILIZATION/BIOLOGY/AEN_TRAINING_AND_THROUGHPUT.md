# aen — dot, arc, ring and band

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/BIOLOGY/AEN_TRAINING_AND_THROUGHPUT.md`

aen is lowercase and distinct from Ain (enchantment) and vaen (resource). There are six cutaneous/subdermal chromatic access channels, one per Red–Violet basin. They can be expressed on suitable body regions; wrist practice is traditional, not an absolute anatomical location restriction.

The current AERA training illustration begins with six approximately 1 cm dots on either wrist, developed Red through Violet. A manifestation ordinarily begins as a dot, extends into an open arc around the wrist, closes as a ring, then broadens into a band. Direct dot-to-wide-patch expansion is impractical. Advanced branching/body coverage is trained; full sleeves are exceptional.

The author specifically fixes useful extra draw at the dot, closed ring and wider band stages. An unfinished arc faces elastic-like resistance to closure. Trying to fight that resistance and draw through the incomplete arc is prohibitively fatiguing; it is a training state, not another useful throughput tier. Closure reduces maintenance resistance compared with an equally extended unfinished arc, but does not remove fatigue. Wider bands offer greater throughput and higher maintenance demand.

The system chiefly affects energy-loading throughput, not automatic Binding output power. A more capable practitioner may load an admissible native/Binding manifold much faster, but no linear area-to-power law is established. Operational cards use their own basin conduits: no aen boost and no simultaneous aen access to a card-occupied basin. All methods share cumulative basin fatigue.

Afflicted aen appear dark/black branching or expanded recursive anchor structures with subtle basin-specific leakage when expressed. They are neither tattoo pigment nor flames, and need not be visible at rest. Geometry must accommodate bodily movement. No fixed full-body map, maximum band width or numerical fatigue equation was established.
