# White Awakening — whole-body biological reconstruction

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/BIOLOGY/WHITE_AWAKENING.md`

The Awakening Potion is the selected distinct White potion; its recipient is Awakened. Ordinary chromatic channel potions and direct-casting education are not equivalent to Awakening.

White reconstruction integrates the existing brain tissue and Genesis crystalline architecture into a unified functional neural system, rather than merely widening separate filaments. Whole-body Genesis coupling develops. Natural pigmentation, eye color and recognizable individual identity remain; healthy hair, multilayered optical skin depth and faint internally contained original-color eye luminescence are general features. Source examples include leaner/denser athletic musculature, modest height increase, more angular/symmetric features and slightly larger orbital proportions. The 8–11% body-fat source example is illustrative fiction, not a universal outcome or health instruction.

Four provisional skin functions are biological surface, chromatic anchors, chromatic separation/organization, and body/neural integration. Cephalopod multilayering is a functional analogy, not literal pigment camouflage. Exact histology remains unassigned.

This makes a body-wide controllable access surface available through six aen. It does not automatically grant skill, a full sleeve, a closed ring, combat rank or a unique destiny. Kaira takes an Awakening Potion to survive her Affliction; her old MK157 acquisition/blackout/armor-selection scene is not imported.

The generalized Awakened appearance must not be overwritten with a prior character's Black spectral eyes or armor traits. The source's dramatic older phenotype is preserved as source history until explicitly adopted for MK163. Current AERA Afflicted biology retains individual pigmentation and recursive channel identity.
