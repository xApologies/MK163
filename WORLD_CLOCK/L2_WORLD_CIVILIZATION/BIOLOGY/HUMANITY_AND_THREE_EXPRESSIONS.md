**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/BIOLOGY/HUMANITY_AND_THREE_EXPRESSIONS.md`

The author uses three species under the human race: dwarf, human and elf. Those are provisional descriptive names; final in-world names remain unselected. AERA calls them physiological expressions/subgroups of a common humanity. Preserve both vocabulary scopes without creating three unrelated chromatic races or declaring the author’s terminology invalid.

Their typical innate neural Genesis-channel development differs: dwarf-like relatively low, human-like intermediate/general, elf-like relatively high. This affects the ease/capacity of natural Genesis access and expression. The author also described lower/average/higher broad intellectual-development tendencies. These are fictional population generalizations, not measured IQ values, individual destiny or immutable professions.

All share the six-strand channel principle. A skilled, knowledgeable dwarf-like practitioner may exceed an untrained elf-like practitioner. None is barred categorically from Bindings, cards, potions or Awakening. Do not equate channel development with learned manifold expertise, noble status, wealth or professional rank. No skull sizes, exact heights, hard channel diameters or universal lifespan ordering were specified.

Kaira's Affliction is an inherited condition of humanity, not a fourth human species, an eldris identity or an automatic Black power package. Her special Black Prophecy role is a character fact, separate from ordinary Afflicted biology.
