# Neurogenesis and the six chromatic channel strands

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/BIOLOGY/NEUROGENESIS_AND_CHROMATIC_POTIONS.md`

AERA's fictional Neurogenesis is microscopic Genesis crystallization and branching in the brain, not a claim about real-world neuronal neurogenesis. Each continuous filament is woven from six distinguishable chromatic strands, like threads twisted into yarn. Native access can be developed through training; competent direct Shaping additionally requires knowledge, geometry and sustained control.

A chromatic potion stimulates the corresponding strand's growth. The inherited newer model uses irregular porous radial crystalline shells linked by outward branches, not detached dots or perfectly sealed ring shells. Neural space competition and changing chromatic surface exposure matter. The selected default is one dose per basin in Red→Orange→Yellow→Green→Blue→Violet order, with recovery, adaptation and conscious acclimatization between doses. Older three-dose and Type One homogenization accounts are historical and not silently combined.

Potions are expensive. Severe overdose may cause neural injury or death; no universal safe dose number is established. Six prior chromatic doses are not automatically a required prerequisite for the White Awakening Potion; that dependency remains undecided in AERA. Modest peripheral coupling can accompany chromatic enhancement, distinct from White's integrated whole-body reconstruction.
