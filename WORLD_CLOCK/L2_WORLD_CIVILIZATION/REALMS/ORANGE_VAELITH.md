The name is **Vaelith**, V-A-E-L-I-T-H. Daelith, Veilith and Vaylith in dictation are not new species. Vaelith replace Phoenix as the selected Orange-native default here.

A Vaelith is a single living Genesis system with four mutually dependent components: living filaments carrying internal communication and energy distribution; distributed coherence nodes coordinating integrity/energy/repair; branching and regeneration that reroute and repair structures; and a persistent identity sustained through the distributed network and a physical living energy shell. It does not depend on a lone indispensable core.

The body is a continuous charcoal-gray, somewhat translucent living Genesis energy shell, not armor, an exosuit, clothing or artificial plates. Faint internal filaments, nodes and branching can be visible. Emission can be subdued; Orange is dominant internal structuring, not an instruction to make the whole body saturated orange. The illustrative 80/20 energy profile is not a taxonomic color-variant lineup.

Accepted anatomy is unisex/androgynous with no male/female secondary sexual differentiation, extremely slender elongated proportions, minimal musculature, flat chest, narrow hips/waist, long thin limbs. A high-fashion mannequin is a shape analogy only. No crests, ribbons, tendrils, voluptuous curves or exaggerated muscles. Energetic reproduction does not require imposing human sexual anatomy.

Coherence, complementary Decoherence and Rebirth define the native focus. Healing, stabilization and reconstructive persistence are important; no automatic immortality, universal rebirth threshold or Kaira-level Black amplification is granted. Phoenix fire/light/avian traits do not silently transfer.

The four-component system may serve as a structural biological blueprint for material humanity in White, not proven literal ancestry. Primary prophecy V, Covenant Garden(s); secondary VI, Living Art. Exact chemistry, opacity, offspring form and contributors, identity-retention limits of rebirth remain open.
