# The seven inhabited realms and their natural denizens

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/REALMS/SEVEN_REALMS.md`

MK163 adopts AERA's inhabited-realm realization. The six chromatic structures are both energy basins and structural realms; White is the material-world realization of their coherent superposition. The selected natural denizens are Red Dragons, Orange Vaelith, Yellow Primordials, Green Sprites, Blue Leviathans and Violet Unicorns. Humanity inhabits White; dwarf-like, human-like and elf-like peoples are three working species/expressions under that underlying human race, not three extra chromatic realms.

AERA's current biological sources use “optional” because the general framework serves other stories. MK163's explicit adoption makes these selections operative here. Older Phoenix/Kitsune defaults and MK231 histories remain source history, not replacement populations or imported companion arcs.

Chromatic identity is not a paint instruction: a creature need not be uniformly its home basin's color. Source illustrations allowing dominant and subsidiary energy contributions do not create extra subspecies. Populations and realm geography do not turn six operational Transduction categories into a color-to-spell chart.

All eight prophecies are intended to be realized through the saga. The Seven Realms concerns the entire domain structure. Black concerns Kaira, not an eighth realm. No fixed itinerary, companion order, visit schedule, or one-denizen-per-book structure has been established. Book colors only order the seven volumes.
