# Green — Sprites

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/REALMS/GREEN_SPRITES.md`

Sprites are intelligent naturally occurring bounded, resolved, persistent Genesis recursions. They share a recursion/containment mechanism with eldris but are not automatically collapse monsters, eldris, or hostile beings.

An individual has a candle-flame-sized visual form, not literal combustion. It does not require fuel or oxygen and need not burn, smoke or be hot. A changing dark-gray recursive shadow contains most internal luminosity. Green dominates; Green light and occasional tiny Red/Orange/Yellow/Blue/Violet leakage may appear. This is not a rule creating six Sprite subspecies.

Sprites can merge as one hive mind/collective entity. Three or four can make a small collective, while additional members can form a larger moving recursion-flame, even bonfire-like in visual scale. Population examples are not fixed energy multipliers.

A sufficiently supported collective may instead become an actual functioning biological tree, human, dragon or Leviathan. These are not green shadow copies, holograms or illusions: they look and function as genuine organisms. Their underlying identity remains the Sprite collective. Taking a dragon's morphology does not automatically confer every native Red Dragon specialization. A Leviathan generally needs a much larger collective than a dragon; small forms may need fewer members. Energy, Resolution, Bandwidth, admissibility and coherence constrain this.

The Green-native focus is realization of admissible perceived potential. Primary prophecy II, The Unwritten; secondary V/VI. Partitioning collective cognition, reproduction of assumed forms, duration, reversion and identity continuity are not numerically defined.

The exceptional Black Sprite has full neon-rainbow internal expression inside Black containment, not ordinary Green leakage. A Black Sprite crystal is available AERA material, but replacing Kaira's awakening or assigning her a first companion through it was never selected in this workshop.
