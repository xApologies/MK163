# Green Realm — world, biology and time model

## Spatial and environmental structure
- Green Realm is distinct from White Realm Green political Domain. P11 is a naturally occurring **always-active** crossing, analogous to a bridge in the fabric of reality; no sigil/key/activation required.
- Non-ordinary geometry: inhabited land above and below; traversable orientation changes; floating and inverted islands; local up/down can reverse. Some apparent upward waterfalls flow into upper lakes. The realm is vast and may wrap back on itself; **Riemann-sphere analogy is illustrative**, not a fixed global topology or claim of literal infinity.
- Dense enchanted-forest-like environment dominated by huge botanical structures, layered growth and water. No visible sun, moon or stars. Illumination can change across spatial thresholds from daylight-like to twilight-like without conventional diurnal cycles.
- Distinct realm temporal behavior: 11 **White Realm days** elapse between Day 1663 entry and Day 1674 exit. Local experienced duration, event sequence, and time-conversion function remain OPEN. Do not infer a fixed 21:11 ratio from an earlier illustrative possibility. The Green Realm event ledger uses G-000 for entry and G-001, G-002 etc **only when actual narrative events are selected**, not advance plot placeholders.

## Genesis ecology
- Native AERA biology is not restricted to Earth species. Distinctive anatomy and adaptations arise from local environmental conditions and non-ordinary geometry.
- Some species originated elsewhere and migrated/crossed into Green Realm, where they adapted; others may be indigenous. Do not assign unverified ancestry to each species.
- Natural state: native organism; first Genesis evolution: field-mediated Genesis expression with integrated luminous channels, altered biological functions, etc.; second **separate** evolution: specialized identity-bearing `vaen` realization in whole organism or component. Neither stage is automatic. A glowing plant is not necessarily `vaen`.
- Genesis luminance is restrained and functional: dermal striping, internal veins, selective membranes, not universal rainbow glow. Nyx male striping green-blue-violet, female red-orange-yellow, and functions as camouflage amid Genesis-expressive environment.
- Sprites are intelligent Green-native communities with a ruling body, **not wildlife**. Black Sprite remains bonded to Kaira, reconnects with its native community and returns with Kaira.
