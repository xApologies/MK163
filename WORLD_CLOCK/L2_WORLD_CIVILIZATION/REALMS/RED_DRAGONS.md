# Red — Dragons

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/REALMS/RED_DRAGONS.md`

Dragons are the selected Red-native natural population. The current inherited outline describes large, physically powerful, flight-capable beings. Six operational specializations exist across the population; an individual dragon has one native specialization in the source, not automatic mastery of all six. A Red identity does not mean “only fire” or equate Red with an operational school.

Chromatic-native reproduction can involve compatible energetic contributions from several individuals. Dragons can produce an egg through pooled energy; the three-or-four-contributor example is not a mandatory count. Physical redesign, exact development and social organization remain author-defined. The chosen primary prophetic association is IV, The Thousand Threads, concerning relations/Transduction organization; this is not identity with the prophecy itself.
