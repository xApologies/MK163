# eln, ahm, diamond chips and resource exchange

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/ECONOMY/CURRENCY_AND_COMMODITY_EXCHANGE.md`

eln are banknotes; ahm are coins. Genesis Diamond chips, Genesis crystals, Genesis Dust and vaen are separate commodities with their own functional and exchange value. A diamond integrated into a coin is not automatically a charged chip. Coin color/denomination is not a diamond's energy grade or a professional rank.

The supplied note/coin artwork shows Red 1, Orange 6, Yellow 12, Green 18, Blue 24, Violet 30 denominations. Coin material pairings are Red iron+diamond, Orange copper+diamond, Yellow tin+diamond, Green nickel+diamond, Blue silver+diamond, Violet gold+diamond. Preserve them as the supplied currency design reference. The banknote artwork prints Aellin Crown; it is not automatic authority for the main kingdom's final name.

Coins are phase-grown metal and diamond together in a sigil into a coin form. Their chromatic glow and the grafted material structure contribute to counterfeiting resistance. Ordinary elemental metal can be grown/shaped with Genesis engineering, so precious metal alone is not an adequate authentication basis. Notes have their own measures, but detailed authentication protocols were deferred.

The tentative “30 gold/diamond coins = one Red eln” recollection was explicitly left unverified and then accepted as unknown. No ahm:eln conversion is locked. The denomination numbers do not settle that conversion. Do not promote an art caption into an exchange law.

The diamond chip density/energy equivalence ladder is separately supplied: 13 Red = 1 Orange; 11 Orange = 1 Yellow; 7 Yellow = 1 Green; 5 Green = 1 Blue; 3 Blue = 1 Violet; 2 Violet = 1 Ultraviolet. It is a retained source exchange reference, not a universal derived quantum law or the coin ladder. Pricing, transaction fees, dust/crystal conversions and regulation remain unassigned.

Mathematical designs can be owned, licensed and generate royalties. Currency, commodities and intellectual property all support the civilization's economy without forcing modern industrial banking details into the book.
