# Genesis Card class retail benchmarks

**Status:** AUTHOR-ESTABLISHED MK163 working reference; qualifications below apply.

Class-level reference retail prices in eln do not set every individual Card's price or specification.

| Classification | Reference eln | Increase from previous |
|---|---:|---:|
| FOUNDATIONAL | 100 | — |
| COMMON_SPECIALIZATION | 230 | 130 |
| ADVANCED | 540 | 310 |
| POWERFUL | 1300 | 760 |
| EXCEPTIONAL | 3100 | 1800 |

The author locked the entire schedule. Foundational 75, Powerful 830 and Exceptional 1000/2300/2700 are earlier proposals, superseded by the current table. MK727 credit values are historical ratios rather than current eln exchange rules. The referenced 1,245-card catalogue remains owned by MK727 (live-model/04_CATALOGUE.md); its individual Exceptional entries, names, class counts and recipes were not verified or imported here.

Foundational Cards are attainable but require deliberate savings for Red-income workers. Specialization commands a nonlinear premium. Crystal input mass, Cardsmith labor and actual savings time remain distinct parameters. A custom job can modify an existing Card or commission a new one. Distinguish the class benchmark from genuinely additional resources and labor; do not charge a second time for materials already included in the retail benchmark or already supplied. Advanced 540 eln plus 1300–1800 eln in extra resources was an illustration, not a universal surcharge, equation or fixed finished-card quote. The general economic index's older statement that Card prices remain open is superseded only by these class benchmarks, not by invented entry-level catalogue prices.
