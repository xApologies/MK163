# Genesis crystal market reference prices

**Status:** AUTHOR-ESTABLISHED MK163 working reference; qualifications below apply.

These are material prices per kilogram of Genesis crystal, denominated in eln. Grams are appropriate for small crafting quantities. They are not finished-card, Genesis Dust, White Dust, vaen or charged-diamond-chip prices.

| Basin / superposition | eln/kg | eln/100 g | eln/g |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Red | 200 | 20 | 0.20 |
| Orange | 350 | 35 | 0.35 |
| Yellow | 600 | 60 | 0.60 |
| Green | 1100 | 110 | 1.10 |
| Blue | 2300 | 230 | 2.30 |
| Violet | 5000 | 500 | 5.00 |
| White | 31000 | 3100 | 31.00 |

The author proposed Red 200, Orange 350 and Yellow 600. Green 1100 was accepted; assistant proposals Blue 2000 and Violet 3600 were explicitly replaced by 2300 and 5000. White was initially deferred and then explicitly set to 31,000, replacing both its OPEN row and the unselected sevenfold 35,000 alternative. The preference was for the numeral 31, which is prime; 31,000 is composite. White remains the six-basin superposition, not a seventh source basin.

Derived ratios: Green→Blue 2300/1100 ≈ 2.09; Blue→Violet 5000/2300 ≈ 2.17; White/Violet = 6.2. Prices do not imply a kilogram per Card. Small quantities can be bought; resource specifications, regional fluctuations, manufacturing costs, margins and yields remain open. White-crystal pricing does not define White Genesis Dust processing yield or price. No Restoration Potion recipe or universal three-basin ingredient formula is established.
