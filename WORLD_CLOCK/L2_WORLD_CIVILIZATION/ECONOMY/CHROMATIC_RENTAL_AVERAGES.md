# Chromatic residential rental averages

**Status:** AUTHOR-ESTABLISHED MK163 working reference; qualifications below apply.

These are author-approved average monthly residential rents in eln, not mandatory unit prices, minima or approved broader range bounds. The income comparison is to individual reference earnings, not household income or professional rank.

| District | Average rent (eln/month) | Reference individual income | Rent / reference income |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Red | 50 | 100 | 50% |
| Orange | 90 | 200 | 45% |
| Yellow | 175 | 400 | 43.75% |
| Green | 600 | 1200 | 50% |
| Blue | 1400 | 3000 | 46.67% (rounded) |
| Violet | 3750 | 5000 | 75% |

Yellow→Green is the major socioeconomic threshold; Blue→Violet has a deliberate luxury premium. White is civic, not another residential price tier. Every district retains clean, maintained infrastructure. Price differences concern space, service, amenities, luxury, quality and location, not automatic decay of lower districts. Multiple earners may share a household's costs.

Kaira's actual Orange lease price remains OPEN: 90 eln is not her agreed rent. The previously withdrawn universal 50-eln Red rent returns only as the explicitly approved average. The historical eleven-month/351-day versus 360-day discrepancy is not repaired by this price table.
