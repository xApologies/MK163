# White Genesis Dust — Crown supply dominance, commercial circulation and social privilege

## Physical origin and rarity
A domai is a temporary bounded Genesis-event environment, with a non-conscious superposed White Genesis Crystal structural core. The six domai classes run Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue and Violet; no White domai class exists. A formed domai has a substantial White core whose structural disruption forces progressive unraveling. Expedition members seek the core because it represents extraordinarily valuable recoverable White Genesis material. A localized Genesis collapse can occasionally begin to form a domai but fail to resolve into a complete environment. Such an aborted formation can leave small White crystals instead of the large coherent anchor mass. Kaira's incident is this rare second route: several roughly six-to-seven-inch White crystal growths near a black central crystal, rather than a complete mature core. She gathers about three to five. The exact mass/price remains unassigned. The author compared the value to extremely rare laboratory materials such as californium, not to specify an actual Earth dollar exchange rate but to convey how violently valuable the material is.

## Military escalation and the competitive race
Red domai permit Red ELDRIS; Orange permits Red and Orange. These are dangerous, but their threats can be handled by local noble groups, specialized aithren and vaelum without routine Crown army deployment. Yellow is the qualitative jump because Yellow ELDRIS express ranged Genesis force as well as lower-class physical and contact threats. The Crown's military response begins at Yellow and continues through Green, Blue and Violet. Military expedition objectives include security, resource recovery and especially the core. Elite noble houses can train specialist strike teams to penetrate directly to a core; exceptional aithren teams can also compete. Groups racing for a core may encounter one another or act under contracts, but no exact contest law has yet been defined. The practical principle is that whoever reaches and successfully breaks the core first can harvest it, though the kingdom's property and contract law can qualify later disposition. It is not the same as saying the Crown owns every core by magical fiat.

The Crown's trained military can sustain repeated high-class domai operations, while independent teams may be sporadic and less well resourced. As a result, the Crown generally recovers most White material, giving it a de facto monopoly in reliable supply. That is a supply dominance, not a universal physical monopoly, a total ban on private ownership, or a claim that every Crown mission succeeds. Other harvesters can collect chromatic crystals, Genesis Dust left when ELDRIS recursion destabilizes, and identity-bearing vaen along the route. Dimensional rings or bracelets help store heavy valuable materials; these devices are expensive and not standard issue to every commoner.

## Distribution and banking
The king is aware that White material is strategically scarce. The Crown may offer no White Dust at a given time, reserve it for royal priorities, use it to support political agreements, allocate it to allied nobles, sell it, or condition access on service and contract obligations. Noble houses may harvest independently, receive royal allocation, or purchase at exchanges. Aithren groups may harvest and sell. Rich merchants can buy on the open market through Genesis Dust exchanges when stock is available. Banks may purchase and hold Genesis Dust reserves, including scarce White material, and thus become another potential route of acquisition. The precise regulatory regime, reserve ratios, prices, banking instruments and royal monopoly enforcement remain unassigned. This is an economic model of scarcity and influence, not a modern securities exchange transplanted wholesale.

## Alchemical and other demand
White superposition provides structural opportunities that ordinary single-basin crystal energy cannot. White material has uses in advanced alchemy, Binding Circles, and occasional Cardsmithing. Awakening Potions require White material and are therefore extraordinary in cost. Other ingredients and skilled alchemy matter, but White material dominates economic accessibility. Some wealthy merchants can obtain Awakening, and nobles can be Afflicted just as commoners can; neither class nor social rank changes the inherited biological mutation. However, noble households are the predominant consumers because they can marshal wealth, political relationships and direct Crown access. Even ordinary affluent households may not be able to purchase an Awakening Potion. Availability and affordability are distinct: possessing money does not guarantee that the Crown or market is offering White Dust.

## Social interpretation
People in an educated city recognize the visible Awakened phenotype: subtly enlarged orbital sockets and eyes, refined angular and symmetric face, healthy multilayered skin, healthy hair, dense lean musculature, and faint internally luminous eyes that retain their original iris color. They also recognize the phenotype of Affliction, a rare genetic mutation producing extremely pale skin, white hair and steel-gray eyes in Kaira. Awakening cures Kaira's pathological condition without erasing pigmentation. She therefore looks unmistakably Awakened while retaining the coloration that identifies her as formerly Afflicted. This is unusual but not biologically impossible: noble families have historically had Afflicted children, and those families sometimes can afford treatment.

When Kaira rents in the Orange District, people know she received something usually associated with nobility, yet she behaves like a financially careful commoner. A rumor forms that she is a rejected, estranged or disowned daughter of an unknown noble house. This is a plausible social guess, not evidence of noble lineage. Some residents are jealous, some indifferent, some curious, some kind. Noble networks eventually learn of her and ask which family, who paid, what patron or Crown connection she has, and why she resides in Orange. Her striking Chainmail Robe and emerging fashion prominence later amplify a mystery that began at her arrival. Across the seven books, strangers repeatedly assume she is noble. Kaira has no established noble ancestry or hidden benefactor; the rumor is wrong, and the public does not know her actual genesis-collapse/Stone Hollow alchemist history.
