# Melee weapon physical-construction benchmarks

**Status:** AUTHOR-ESTABLISHED MK163 working reference; qualifications below apply.

| Physical weapon | Average retail eln before Genesis engineering |
|---|---:|
| Standard sword | 35 |
| Standard large war axe | 30 |
| Standard war maul | 20 |
| Massive sword | 165 |
| Massive war axe | 165 |
| Massive maul | 165 |

The shared massive-weapon average replaces the provisional 130–200 range as the central reference without prohibiting variation by size, quality and commission. These are working author labels, not formal weapon ranks or rarity tiers. Historical English shilling prices were inspiration, not a literal currency conversion or documented MK163 market survey.

All six benchmarks cover physical construction only. Intrinsic mass/handling changes, impact recoupling/amplification, sigil work, Ain, Genesis crystals, diamonds, vaen and other enhancements are priced separately where applicable. No universal enhancement fee or formula is fixed.
