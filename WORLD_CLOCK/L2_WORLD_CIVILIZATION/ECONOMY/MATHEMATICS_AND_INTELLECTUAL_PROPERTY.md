# Mathematics as practical intellectual property

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/ECONOMY/MATHEMATICS_AND_INTELLECTUAL_PROPERTY.md`

Mathematics describes the Genesis Field and its engineered sigils, Transduction, Alchemy, projections and constructs. In this civilization it is not only scholarship; a specific useful mathematical equation/construction can function as patented intellectual property and generate royalties.

The author's example is the equation that produces an ER Foam projection for a light-switch surface. It might be owned by the Crown, a noble family or the mathematician. Ownership and licensing can interact with manufacturing. This is the selected fictional legal-economic premise, not a claim about real-world patent eligibility or a replacement of the author's premise with Earth law.

Precise patent duration, public-domain exceptions, enforcement, filing processes, infringement tests and royalty rates are unassigned. The current decision is ownership/licensing of useful mathematical constructions. Do not invent universal ownership of every abstract mathematical truth. Nor reduce this to an irrelevant optional aside: the author explicitly adopted it for MK163.

Kaira's garment business, devices and learned engineering can later use this economic structure, but no patent has yet been assigned to her or awarded revenue. Source definitions and provenance remain separate from in-world legal ownership.
