# Chromatic individual income reference scale

**Status:** AUTHOR-ESTABLISHED MK163 working reference; qualifications below apply.

The accounting unit is individual income, not household income, business revenue, accumulated wealth, district residency eligibility, nobility or professional rank. Red reference income is 100 eln per accounting month; the approved relative multipliers are ×1, ×2, ×4, ×12, ×30 and ×50.

| Tier | Multiple | eln/month | eln/year (11 accounting months) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Red | 1 | 100 | 1100 |
| Orange | 2 | 200 | 2200 |
| Yellow | 4 | 400 | 4400 |
| Green | 12 | 1200 | 13200 |
| Blue | 30 | 3000 | 33000 |
| Violet | 50 | 5000 | 55000 |

The Yellow→Green transition and Blue→Violet elite jump are deliberate. Households may pool several incomes; this schedule does not require one earner per home or equal income for every resident. The author selected eleven-month annualization even though inherited month lengths total 351 days against the canonical 360-day year. The missing nine days and their wage treatment remain unresolved; do not manufacture twelve 30-day months or intercalation.

Earlier Red weekly five-eln modeling is superseded. The source income document's withdrawal of a 50-eln Red rent was an earlier modeling stage: the subsequent explicit rental decision reintroduces 50 eln only as a district average. Income alone does not determine rents, equipment prices, or savings time.

[Approved rental averages](CHROMATIC_RENTAL_AVERAGES.md) and [currency](CURRENCY_AND_COMMODITY_EXCHANGE.md) own those separate facts.
