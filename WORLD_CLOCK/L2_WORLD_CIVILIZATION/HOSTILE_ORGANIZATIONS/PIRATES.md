# Pirates — international criminal maritime infrastructure

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/HOSTILE_ORGANIZATIONS/PIRATES.md`

Pirates form a major seafaring global criminal power, not merely isolated raiders. A Pirate King, subordinate Pirate Lords, fleets, captains and crews organize the collective network; independent pirates can also exist. The author's desired distinction is the coordinated infrastructure that lets them move illegal goods and people internationally.

Drug trafficking and the slave trade are central crimes. They are also suppliers of prohibited luxury resources and other contraband. A wealthy noble might pay a cartel to obtain a true-black timber whose originating country forbids export and other governments will not knowingly import. Pirates can acquire/transport it at an enormous premium, and cartel intermediaries and suppliers each take a share. The timber example illustrates demand and price escalation without locking a biological species or specific law.

The original source names pirates as primary global slavers and describes maritime hubs and logistics. Its detailed fictional drug and historical violence remain source evidence; this package does not turn its old pharmacology into a practical real-world recipe or add new methods. Victim exploitation is an authored evil, not an endorsement of the organization.

**Geographic source reconciliation:** the original PDF describes three to five large mobile floating hubs and a Pirate King hierarchy. MK147's accepted later summary also describes continent-scale fragmented volcanic Pirate Islands, tectonically unstable and cyclically regenerative: growth → destruction → regrowth. Both source implementations are preserved without silently declaring them identical, replacing current geography with the older planetary circumference, or introducing a permanent Domai Heart/Chimera system. The final hub/island combination and exact number of lords remain open.

Pirate consequences are present socially before their direct story focus. Kaira becomes aware around Yellow, engages more directly in Green and Blue, and the organized collective hierarchy/infrastructure is dismantled in Blue. That does not kill every independent pirate or instantly cure addiction and erase local black markets. Once the union breaks, remaining pirates lose coordinated protection and become more vulnerable on open water.
