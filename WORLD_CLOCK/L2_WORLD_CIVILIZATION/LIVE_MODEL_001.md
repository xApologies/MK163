# L2 World and Civilization — MK163 checkpoint 001

**Inheritance:** AERA in full; MK147 as historical worldbuilding reference only.

## World and settlements
- Circumbinary starscape; 31-hour day, 360-day year, four 90-day seasons, six-day week. Kira age 23 local years. Historical month-length discrepancy unresolved.
- Wilds beyond protected settlements. Storms, Domai, localized collapse, Eldris, Genesis-expressing flora/fauna, vaen. Dungeons prismatically isolate contiguous chromatic strata (e.g. Orange-Yellow-Green, not Red-Orange-Blue); Red oscillates slowest, Violet fastest. Oscillations grow crystals, vaen and Eldris. Eldris confined to their own strata. Deep dungeons may extend many miles (17-mile example). Genesis Mines isolate basin-specific crystal-growth oscillation until equilibrium.
- Four capital domains: Violet King and Queen, Orange and Green princes, Blue princess. King's Highway: six bidirectional permanent gate links; three monumental sigil-carved portals in each capital oriented inward around plaza, traffic exits outward.
- Square capitals ~13x13 miles, large cities ~10x10, small cities ~7x7. Concentric outer Red to inner chromatic ranks and White civic center. Small cities Red/Orange/Yellow/White; large and capitals Red through Violet plus White. Economic affluence increases inward; outer rings are not automatically dilapidated.
- Village hubs ~0.5x0.5 mile for future small cities or 1x1 for large; regional development ~20–30-mile radius for 60–80 years then ~11–13 years city construction. Kira's starting region includes dungeon staging area formerly labeled Hollowmere, name not locked.
- Sigil-grown orthogonal stone blocks, spatial locking, fresh water and dissolution fields, velis growth fields, alchemically enriched soil and abundant vegetarian crops. Meat costly due to livestock vulnerability/space. Late 17th/early 18th-century preindustrial aesthetic; metals exist, no Earth-style metal-intensive industrial revolution; wooden ships.

## Institutions and professions
- `maege` singular / `maegi` plural: active offensive/support/utility Shaping specialists. `helkir`: healing by coherence and topological stabilization. Summoner: channel/sustain manifested entities. Augmenter: finite-duration locally anchored biological reinforcement, weapon/armor melee specialist, not typically projectile caster. Architect: directly engineers Genesis field topology, velis, bulwarks, agriculture, spatial corridors. Alchemist: material science using sigil-guided phase-field Transduction, potions, material infusion and dust production. Warden and Cartographer NOT adopted as distinct professions.
- Professional chromatic qualification Red→White; proposed 13 test domains, White overall only with all 13 White. Mixed non-White aggregate method undecided. White-ranked practitioners exceptionally prestigious. Noble titles still to recover.
- Blackstone flagship Institution of Knowledge: mathematics, science, Genesis Field Theory and concept training. Better concept improves Shaping efficiency, not necessarily Binding-normalized power.
- Vaelum guild-like organizations, aithren adventurers, Accord Registry maintains records. Dungeon delvers are relaxed in staging settlements, serious inside mapped dungeons; storm chasers are high-risk operators in Wilds. Raeon card game; Prism national sport.

## Economy, technology, threats
- ELN banknotes, AHM coins, exchange rate not yet fixed. Diamond chips are separate energy-storage commodities. Diamonds organize deposited basin energy into lower-to-higher average occupancy states; frosted low energy, then Red through Violet, then nearly transparent ultraviolet. Easier draw/control than raw basin energy. Multi-basin charging can be faster; personal charging causes fatigue. Sigil charging operates continuously, often on large diamond blocks. Crystals, dust and vaen have independent markets. Sigil-grown diamond/metal coins resist counterfeiting; ordinary metal phase-growth possible.
- Dimensional Rings and Bracelets expensive due to Genesis crystal arrangement/manufacture, common among affluent people. Bracelets typically hold ~2–3x ring capacity, no fixed cubic-foot values. Both hold substantial equipment. Kira receives exceptional ring at end of Red, uses it Orange–White.
- Genesis constructs include simple glowing sigil cube, explosive cube, alchemy table, maege glass interfaces and larger engineering. ER Foam can provide tactile surfaces and optional load-bearing road projection. Mathematical/sigil engineering may be patented/licensed.
- Bandits threaten roads throughout series; cartels operate black markets, pirates maritime smuggling/drug/slave trade, cabal of evil maegi later threat. Exact book-by-book escalation not fully locked.
- King's Tournament every FOUR years. Eligible dungeons reach Green/Blue/Violet. Local qualifiers; six-event regional points circuit; top seven teams in each of four domains (28 finalists) race Red→Violet dungeon, activate marker and return. Scoring White=7, Violet=6, Blue=5, Green=4, Yellow=3, Orange=2, Red=1; six-event maximum 42. Violet hosts final. Multi-day festival at dungeon staging area with entertainment and possible jousting/melee events. Exact calendar, prizes, team composition remain open.
