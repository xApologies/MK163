# helkir — Coherence and topological biological stabilization

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/PROFESSIONS/HELKIR.md`

The Healer/helkir profession is specific to this Genesis realization. Healing operates through **Coherence and topological stabilization** of biological organization, rather than being defined merely as a generic green healing spell or an added life-point quantity.

The practitioner needs understanding of the biological structures they are stabilizing. Blackstone's Biology, Anatomy, Chemistry, Topology, Differential Geometry, Genesis Field Theory and clinical work are relevant because conceptual precision informs the intended restoration. That establishes an educational relationship, not a complete medical spell catalogue or guaranteed regeneration of every injury.

The author describes this profession as substantially established but with more detailed material still to be added to AERA. Do not replace that deferred technical corpus with invented diagnoses, anatomy, numeric healing thresholds, resurrection, automatic immunity or guarantees that every Afflicted person survives Awakening. Kaira's lifesaving Awakening is a selected personal story event, not a universal helkir outcome.

A dungeon party can recruit a helkir for healing and stabilization. Which interface that individual uses remains independent of the profession. An Augmenter may know a few healing Shapings without changing their primary martial identity.
