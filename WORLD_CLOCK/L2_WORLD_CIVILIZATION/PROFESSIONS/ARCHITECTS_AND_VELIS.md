# Architects and velis — local field engineering

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/PROFESSIONS/ARCHITECTS_AND_VELIS.md`

An Architect directly interacts with the Genesis Field and organizes its local topology, manifolds, geometry and boundaries into functional structures. The author compares this with knitting yarn into a scarf, hat, shirt, gloves or socks: the field is the yarn, the practitioner organizes the relationships, and a closed/coherent structure performs a function.

A **velis** is a localized Genesis-field topology/anchor, not an inscribed ru’ne and not necessarily a conventional physical sigil. Its function can be a persistent projected field or an interactive structure. Examples include a defensive hemisphere around an area, a prepared defensive bulwark, an encampment perimeter, an interactive facility enabling a Shaping, an agricultural growth field, and a semi-permanent spatial corridor. These are functional possibilities, not complete formulas for all such effects.

The field topology is temporarily persistent, not necessarily momentary. Source low-load examples can last decades or centuries; heavy use can shorten effective life. Coherence, maintenance and reinforcement matter. No single universal duration or depletion rate has been selected.

Architects are usually preparation and infrastructure specialists. Establishing field geometry takes work, so a stationary setup specialist is not normally the first choice for immediate dungeon monster combat. That is a characteristic professional deployment, not a prohibition against an Architect knowing active combat Transduction.

The rejected Cartographer proposal is subsumed here: managing spatial adjacency, anchors and corridors is Architect work. It does not grant every Architect the ability to build a King's Highway gate or require the prophecy called The Cartographers to be a professional qualification.

Agriculture is especially important: a locally anchored growth field accelerates suitable plant growth, while an Alchemist supplies enriched soil for increased nutrient uptake. The field is not a replacement for nutrients. Examples of three, five or seven harvests in a season indicate civilizational abundance, not a universal growth multiplier.
