# Professional badges and the chainmail qualification

## 3. Professional rank and scope
Seven chromatic ranks, **Red → Orange → Yellow → Green → Blue → Violet → White**. Rank means demonstrated **mastery within the credential's stated profession/specialization**, not innate Genesis capacity, social class, combat rank or wealth. White is the highest recognized tier. A professional may have different ranks in different specialties. One White Chainmail Armorsmith is not thereby White in general armorsmithing, all metalwork, Transduction, aen, or combat.

The Crown may maintain credential records for many occupations, but classification of **civilization professions** (chefs, bakers, tailors, shipwrights, innkeepers and many others) is **deferred**. The earlier proposal for a sixth universal 'Civilization' badge and later 'Culinary Arts' family is **RETIRED / NOT SELECTED**. Do not claim every business owner is professionally rated.

## 4. Five APPROVED primary badge families
1. **Transduction** — four specific roles: Guardian, Helkir, Offensive Specialist, Support Specialist. Family badge shared; specific role in credential record. Do not conflate these with every other craft that happens to use a Genesis Card.
2. **Craftsmanship** — material workmanship and making; includes Kaira's specialized Chainmail Armorsmith credential. Other specialties require their own rank/standards.
3. **Alchemy** — alchemical professional practice; not equivalent to Helkir.
4. **Architect** — **AERA-native prime role working directly with the Genesis Field**, NOT construction/building architect, building designer or structural engineering. Precise operational definition inherited from AERA; do not extrapolate mechanics.
5. **Sigilcraft** — physical carving/inscription of sigils. Do not automatically enlarge it into all sigil research, architecture or Transduction engineering. **Sigilcraft** is accepted family label; 'Sigilwright' was an unselected naming suggestion.

Each family has its own **three-dimensional metal-and-gem structural geometry**, **not** a pictogram stamped onto a pin. The five physical geometries in [exact five-badge reference](../../../PROVENANCE/WORKSHOP/ORANGE_CUMULATIVE_005_2026_11_09/VISUAL_REFERENCES/ACCORD_REGISTRY_FIVE_BADGES_APPROVED.jpeg) are **APPROVED** as the visual reference. The Alchemy family was revised from a vial-like silhouette into a nonrepresentational, symmetric orbital/petal geometry; do not revert to the vial. Badge form is similar in fabrication language to Crown ahm currency (integrated metal + faceted gem). These are distinct family structures, not literal icons. Dimensions, alloy and security details remain OPEN. The later-selected magnetic backing is described below.

## 7. Chainmail appraisal: what the assessor examines
A Crown on-staff qualified **chainmail craftsmanship appraiser/assessor** reviews the submitted complete shirt and tests the applicant's authorship/competence. Distinguish **(a) item craftsmanship appraisal, (b) maker's professional rank, (c) item mechanical performance, (d) item market value**. None automatically determines the others.

Seven appraisal domains, with IMPORTANT attribution distinction:
1. **Ring fabrication/input quality standard**: diameter, wire thickness, circularity, material consistency and defects. Rings were **fabricated by Kaira's independent master-mailwright supplier using sigil Transduction**, to a quality standard Kaira requires/accepts. Kaira did NOT manufacture those rings. She must recognize/reject defective supplied rings; this is supplier quality control, not her fabrication mastery.
2. **Ring closures**: Kaira personally manipulates, aligns and clamps rings tightly, using her beginner **Metal Manipulation Genesis Card** as a tool; checks secure closure, absence of sharp ends, repeatable symmetry and consistent alignment. Card use does not automatically grant expertise.
3. **Weave geometry**: correctness, pattern consistency, symmetry, coherent Offset-Lattice design and controlled junctions.
4. **Structural integrity**: connected-section stability, sound transitions and controlled resistance to separation under non-destructive checks; no unearned claims of verified combat protection.
5. **Garment construction**: panel transitions, underarms, neck, shoulders, fit, articulation and drape.
6. **Finishing quality**: clean uniform surfaces, correction of defects and completed workmanship.
7. **Craftsmanship sophistication**: difficulty and coherent execution of design, geometric ingenuity and successful integration of techniques; innovation alone cannot compensate for flawed closures or construction.

**Two through seven are the armorsmith's own work.** Number one is the required component quality standard and source-material judgment. No transduction rank is conferred for using the Card.

## 8. Standardized qualification procedure — Day 26
The earlier 'appraisal alone instantly grants rank' idea is **SUPERSEDED** by completed-work appraisal **PLUS supervised practical authorship verification**.
A. **Intake**: applicant presents completed garment, declares construction, supplier materials, Card/tool use, intended function; examiner records applicant and item.
B. **Finished-work appraisal**: expert visually and instrumentally checks representative sections, critical seams and transitions of ~23–25k-ring Offset-Lattice demonstration shirt; non-destructive handling/articulation; discusses design and construction. No need to inspect every ring.
C. **Practical test**: Registry supplies standardized rings, tools, panels and imperfect ring samples, purchased from local suppliers and covered by testing material allowance. Kaira may use permitted declared tools/Card. Examiner watches her work, not a second full shirt.
   - **Input quality control**: identify and reject faulty supplied rings (domain 1; not ring fabrication).
   - **Closure**: accurately align/close a series of rings (domain 2).
   - **Flat weave**: produce a standard panel (domain 3).
   - **Joining/integrity**: join sections and demonstrate sound structural connection (domain 4).
   - **Shaping**: demonstrate underarm, neckline, shoulder or equivalent shaped transition on samples (domain 5).
   - **Finishing**: correct deliberately introduced faults and finish a sample (domain 6).
   - **Advanced technique**: demonstrate complex/Offset-Lattice weave or chosen advanced construction and explain decisions (domain 7).
   The earlier suggested six supervised stations can group input quality screening with the workmanship stations; **the precise count of stations and testing duration are not locked**. Core competencies and practical demonstration are locked.
D. **Determination**: assessor combines submitted-work appraisal and supervised performance to verify authorship, competence and discipline-specific rank. Kaira is awarded **WHITE rank in CHAINMAIL ARMORSMITHING on Day 26** and becomes eligible for a professional Registry listing. This is not an automatic White ranking of the demonstration shirt's battlefield performance or sale price.

Kaira is **23**, mother died at **15**, began smith's exploitative work around **16**, worked to **23**: **~7 years** of intensive repetitive chainmail practice, not a canonically fixed eight years or a decade. She is already a master before the test; the Crown acknowledges it. Her Chainmail Robe remains unfinished, and any future robe quality requires its own assessment.
