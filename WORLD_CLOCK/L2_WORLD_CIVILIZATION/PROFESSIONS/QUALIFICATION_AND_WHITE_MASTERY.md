# Chromatic qualification and the White mastery condition

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/PROFESSIONS/QUALIFICATION_AND_WHITE_MASTERY.md`

Professional qualification can apply to maegi, helkir, blacksmiths, chefs/bakers and other selected trades, not only combat specialists. The assessment certifies proficiency within a profession; it does not describe nobility, species, energy-basin access or book position.

The author proposes **thirteen competency domains** for a profession. Each is assessed Red through Violet with White representing exceptional complete performance. Exact domain names and the list of trades using the system remain to be authored/recovered.

**White overall requires White in all thirteen domains.** Twelve White results and one Violet are not sufficient: the overall result is Violet. A White-ranked professional may be regarded as a paragon within their field. It is an exceptional and socially significant designation. A White-ranked maege inspires particular caution/respect because of that professional mastery.

The author describes mixed results as averaged but has not assigned numeric weights, rounding or aggregation rules below the White threshold. The assistant's earlier lowest-score interactive example is NOT adopted. Do not infer a universal minimum rule, or implement a plain rounded arithmetic mean that upgrades twelve White plus one Violet to White. Preserve the explicit special condition and leave the remaining formula open.

Chromatic maege robes communicate earned professional rank. The gray-black Chainmail Robe communicates shared Transductionist identity instead. It does not nullify qualifications or make every wearer a White-ranked master.
