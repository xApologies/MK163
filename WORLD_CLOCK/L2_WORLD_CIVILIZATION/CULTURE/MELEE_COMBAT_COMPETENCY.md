# Melee competency and Genesis weapon timing

**Status:** AUTHOR-ESTABLISHED MK163 working reference; qualifications below apply.

Weapon competency includes guards, transitions between guards and weapon forms, attacks, recovery and coordinated Genesis activation. Owning expensive equipment or an augmentation Binding does not supply these skills. The wielder may activate impact recoupling/amplification shortly before contact and release it for recovery. Sustaining the amplified state makes transitions and handling harder unless augmentation or engineering compensates; exact latency and limits remain open.

Personal augmentation, leather/mail/plate or Juggernaut armor, and engineered melee weapons can coexist. This permits martial specialization without proving superiority over other Transduction interfaces. The four AERA equipment configurations can coexist in one item and are not weapon rarity tiers. A larger stated mass alone does not generate impact energy.

**PROPOSAL, not a locked demographic:** 60% swords, 25% mace/maul, 15% axes among conventional melee weapon users. This is not the share of all aithren, all citizens, or all Transductionists. It remains a discussion proposal even though it totals 100%.

Massive sword/war axe/maul are working labels. Their construction benchmarks, geometric impulse, calculus correspondence and unresolved field conservation details have separate owners below; no equations or pricing model are independently redefined here.
