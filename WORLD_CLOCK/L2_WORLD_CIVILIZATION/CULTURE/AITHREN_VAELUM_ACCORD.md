# aithren, vaelum and Accord Registry

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/CULTURE/AITHREN_VAELUM_ACCORD.md`

The terms remain lowercase in ordinary prose: **aithren** for the adventuring participant/community identity, **vaelum** for the persistent organization, and **Accord Registry** for Crown registration/recognition. Dictation Aethryn/Aethrin/Velum does not establish new institutions.

A vaelum is an independent, recognized, contract-capable organization under which aithren may operate. It is not a Crown military unit, a noble house or a geographic area. The relationship Crown → Accord Registry → recognized vaelum → members/teams describes recognition and affiliation, not universal military command. The author's explanation includes keeping records of organizations and their members; detailed registration powers and legal code remain unassigned.

Aithren culture values participation, contribution, curiosity, discovery and competence, not only paid monster fighting. Vaelum can furnish temporary expertise to the Crown, nobles, merchants, caravans, researchers, Blackstone, physicians and private citizens. Work includes guards and escorts, bandit suppression, dungeon/storm/domai work, exploration, retrieval, recovery, sample harvesting, dangerous transport, medical assistance, agriculture and infrastructure support.

In a research expedition, the researcher controls the research question while the hired group supplies navigation, security, logistics, safe working space, sample recovery and extraction. This distinction explains how institutions hire capability without retaining every specialist permanently.

Recognized organizations can receive qualifying tax/property benefits. Source examples include reduced tax on recovered Genesis Dust and qualifying headquarters or housing. A roughly 200-member vaelum with a headquarters is illustrative, not a universal required size. No fixed tax rate, standard quest board, compulsory reputation score or complete arbitration system has been authored.
