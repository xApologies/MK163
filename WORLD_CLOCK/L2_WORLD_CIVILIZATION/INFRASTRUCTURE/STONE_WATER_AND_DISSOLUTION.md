# OR-03 — Civic architecture: stone, spatial locking, lighting, water, greenery, sigil flame

**Orange arc of Book Red · 2026-11-08.** ## Materials and architectural grammar

The Violet Dungeon staging settlement is **fully laid with constructed stonework in its streets, promenades and public spaces**, rather than a network of bare dirt paths. Phase fields and engineered sigils grow precise, orthogonal, interlocking stone blocks. Spatial-locking relationships distribute loads through the engineered system and permit broad open interior volumes with fewer conventional load-bearing pillars. The stones, roads, building shells and pavement have clean maintained surfaces. There can still be dust, everyday work, a little occasional dirt and material scuffing; **do not invent universal automatic cleaning** or make it a sterile world with no wear. Wood is relatively scarce due to danger in the Wilds; stone and metal are normal structural/furnishing choices. Some wooden shelves, furniture accents and equipment remain appropriate, but thick timber framing everywhere is wrong. No derelict, muddy, smoky "medieval tavern" clichés.

Architecture combines **orthogonal engineered structure with intentionally cultivated flora**: flowers, trees, gardens, planters, small courtyards, water features. Botanical support via **Architect-created velis** and appropriate physical sigils, watering and local Genesis conditions means buildings can sustain plants without adding fictitious electronic systems. Lower-price buildings are still maintained and beautiful, simply smaller and less elaborate; luxury indicates size and expressive design, not clean-versus-filthy.

## Maege lights and ambiance

Use protected singular **maege**, plural **maegi**, and **maege lights** (not "mage lights"). Engineered luminous sigils can illuminate spaces at different brightness and tonal colors, including warm ambient gold and subtle blue, green or violet. Lighting can be layered in ceilings, walls, floors, accents and water features. At night, sigils positioned within a water feature or behind flowing water illuminate the liquid from within, producing an atmospheric contrast with the bright naturally lit day. Specific colors in any individual shop are design choices, not a mandatory uniform global scheme. No computer HUD, advanced digital projection walls or holographic tablet screens merely because some old generated imagery displayed them.

## Water generation and dissolution

A wall may have an engineered continuous water cascade illuminated from behind/within, descending into a lower basin or shallow indoor pond. Small aquatic/terrestrial plants can inhabit the landscaped basin. Engineered water-generation sigils establish controlled input; an overflow pathway returns excess to an **appropriate dissolution field** below, preventing uncontrolled water spilling onto the floor. The author explicitly chooses balanced water generation and removal rates for the working feature. Existing MK163 ontology returns dissipated water/waste in appropriate conditions to low-intensity ambient Genesis energy; **do not invent perpetual energy creation or bypass source-system admissibility and maintenance**. This system is appealing in civic spaces, inns and some restaurants, but **not required in working smithies**, which should be modest, relatively unadorned and clean.

## Sigil fireplace

Fireplace flames can be **sustained Genesis-field Transduction expressions**, with warmth and illumination but **no burning wood, soot, ash, carbon dust or smoke**. The emitter may be integrated in a stone hearth and can be used aesthetically and thermally; precise thermal regulation, energy budget and failure protections remain OPEN. Fireplaces are not ordinary coal-fired forges. A controlled smithing/thermal sigil may appear at an apprentice station as part of material work, but main master craft is through constrained Metal/Thermal Transduction, not endless hammering at a roaring smoky forge.

## Building security and open entrances

Large commercial and civic buildings (Accord Registry, raeon shop, **abecca** shop, smithies, large inns where chosen) may use broad open frontages during operating hours rather than physical swinging/sliding doors. At closing an engineered sigil activates an invisible or subtly visible **projected barrier that prevents passage**. Houses/private residences can still have ordinary physical doors. Exact barrier operator, legal authorized access, alarms, emergency egress and maintenance rules are OPEN; do not automatically import the precise elevator barrier state machine into storefront security. Open-faced smithy bays can each possess independent threshold barriers; security is functional even without massive door hardware.

## Socially inhabited, not showroom sterile

Registry employees, independent aithren, vaelum teams, researchers, visiting citizens, traders and craftworkers move through the refined spaces; venues remain lively and functional. A beautiful inn accommodates tired expedition groups, discussions, meals, raeon play, repairs, and individual leisure. The visual atmosphere is "clean, utopian, engineered, bright, elegantly inhabited". An upscale Registry hall can be more spacious and feature-rich than an ordinary 11–20-room inn, and working smithies should be smaller than monumental cathedral-sized generated renders.

## Source ownership boundary

These are authored fictional architecture/Transduction rules, not real-world technologies or physical claims. AERA generic maths and the MK163 World Clock remain upstream dependencies, not something Codex should replace with new speculative ontology. Avoid treating sigils (physical inscriptions), natural ru’ne/sigil relationships and temporary Architect velis as identical objects; they are related but separately defined interfaces.

## Prior record retained in full

# Phase-grown stone architecture and Genesis municipal utilities

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/INFRASTRUCTURE/STONE_WATER_AND_DISSOLUTION.md`

Sigils project phase fields that grow uniform, orthogonal stone blocks. Spatial locking lets blocks click/interlock and makes Genesis engineering carry architectural constraints beyond conventional masonry. This is not a ban on craft, planning or upkeep; it is the selected technology through which buildings are made.

The strongly rectilinear/block-like stone is softened by natural green growth, trees, courtyards, gardens and abundant flowing water. The eight original architecture images are accepted aesthetic references, not eight separately measured city layouts. They apply across maintained districts, with luxury/space varying by ring.

Water-generation sigils supply clean water. Dissolution fields transform appropriate waste matter back into Genesis energy dispersed to ambient surroundings. A toilet's biological waste can be treated this way. Overflow water is routed into dissolution rather than accumulating into urban flooding. No conventional sewer network is assumed necessary for every site; no universal heat conversion/blast equation was supplied.

Agricultural velis with alchemically enriched soils support food production. This allows a settlement to be placed far from conventional supplies of every raw resource. Security, field stabilization, skilled maintenance and development time still matter. “A city in the wilderness without nearby resources” describes the capability, not a claim that capital investment/labor/energy have no cost.

Light controls, water controls and other equipment may use tactile ER Foam projections. These ordinary objects illustrate a society whose infrastructure developed through magic, not one with modern electronics silently imported.
