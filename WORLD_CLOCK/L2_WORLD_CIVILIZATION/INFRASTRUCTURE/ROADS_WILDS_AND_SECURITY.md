# Regional roads, stabilized territory and the Wilds

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/INFRASTRUCTURE/ROADS_WILDS_AND_SECURITY.md`

Regional trade and travel still require roads even though capitals link through the King's Highway. Caravans, supplies and travelers move between ordinary settlements and resource sites. Bandits prey upon this exposed traffic; guards and hired vaelum fill a practical security need.

The author describes the Wilds as uninhabited nature outside protected settled domains. The inherited technical usage is land outside reliable Genesis-field stabilization. These overlap but are not exact synonyms: a protected rural farm may be outside city walls without being unprotected Wilds. Preserve both conversational geography and technical hazard usage rather than silently asserting all countryside is the same.

Metropolitan sigil arrays can protect a region larger than a city's walls. MK147's large-city example uses five major sigils (four corners and center) as a coordinated array, with a possible roughly 200-mile stabilization radius. This is a source example, not every city's locked radius or proof that bandits/physical invaders cannot enter. Do not equate suppression of new Genesis collapse with elimination of all crime or monsters already moving through the area.

The multi-generation Village Hub scheme combines stabilization, settlement, patrols, surveys, roads and resource development. ER Foam projected roads are an available engineering use, not a universal replacement for stone/ordinary roads. No map distance should be computed from a newly invented schematic.
