# The King’s Highway — four capitals, twelve gateways, six paired links

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/INFRASTRUCTURE/KINGS_HIGHWAY.md`

The King's Highway is the permanent dimensional gateway network of the four main capitals, not the kingdom's ordinary roads. Every capital has direct passage to each of the other three. This makes six bidirectional capital-pair connections and twelve monumental endpoint structures, three per capital. The complete graph K4 describes connectivity, not their geographic arrangement.

Each gateway is a vast standing stone doorframe, hundreds of meters high and wide, beautiful/possibly carved and covered in sigils. Those sigils maintain the active dimensional geometry. It is always active under kingdom maintenance. A traveler looks through and directly sees the other capital, then walks across as through a doorway. No opaque shimmering screen, scheduled activation, personal summoning or waiting cooldown is imposed.

Each capital has a plaza with three gates arranged at the vertices of an imagined triangle around a central park/fountain. All face inward **relative to their local vertex**, not all the same rotation on the page. In the accepted Violet-capital plan: Blue north with horizontal span; Orange southwest with span slanting \; Green southeast with span slanting /. The triangle is a construction guide, not a physical wall or plaza boundary.

Arrival through Blue proceeds north into the city; through Orange southwest; through Green southeast. Gates remain bidirectional. Preserve the original approved image IMG_6988.jpeg and the author's orientation sketch; do not reinterpret them by adding mismatched arrows or rotating all gates horizontally. The same architectural principle applies to every capital; destination labels change, but exact placement of those other labels has not been specified.

Violet connects Orange/Green/Blue. Orange connects Violet/Green/Blue. Green connects Violet/Orange/Blue. Blue connects Violet/Orange/Green. No hub-and-spoke intermediate stop is required. The capitals concentrate regional commerce on this enormous planet and form administrative/trade hubs.

Roads still connect capitals to smaller cities, villages, farms, mines, dungeons and estates. Ordinary caravans through exposed terrain remain vulnerable to bandits and justify hired guards/aithren. Exact gate tolls, policing, cargo rules, energy budgets and engineering specifications are deferred; the book needs their existence and practical function, not an exposition of every equation.
