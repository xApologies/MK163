# Genesis storms and localized collapse events

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/ENVIRONMENT/GENESIS_STORMS_AND_COLLAPSE.md`

The selected Starscape's unusual Neon configuration creates episodic environmental Genesis instability. A local collapse can form roaming eldris without a dungeon, and may alter flora/fauna. The author's ten-to-fifteen-monster example is an illustration of a dangerous local outbreak, not a fixed spawn count.

Genesis storms are violent weather-like field disturbances with intense energy expression and dangerous collapse potential. The comparison to entering a high-energy lightning storm conveys severity, not ordinary atmospheric lightning as the full mechanism.

A storm can resolve into a bounded domai, but does not always do so. Local/runaway collapse and eldris formation can occur without a bounded landscape. Storm, collapse and domai are related environmental outcomes, not compulsory stages that every event must complete.

Threat depends on where stabilization and security are reliable. Wilds events explain settlement defenses, expedition professions, agriculture constraints and patrols. No universal incidence, storm radius, early-warning time or guaranteed safe route has been assigned.
