# eldris — persistent recursive collapse and six behavior families

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/ENVIRONMENT/ELDRIS.md`

“The collapse did not stop. It stabilized.” An eldris is a bounded continuing process that persists as an entity: local instability, runaway recursive chirality propagation, basin-dominant closure, persistent recursive topology. The basin alone is not the monster, nor is every collapse a monster. The closure-state is the entity.

The body is typically black/shadow-like, wispy, rootlike, fractured or branching. Basin expression is tightly contained and leaks in eyes, claws, wounds, breath, inner traces and impact effects. Family is identified by behavior and traces, not painting the entire body its color. Black containment is not Kaira's Black Prophecy identity.

Its substance is energy-mass closure rather than ordinary flesh; it can hit, claw, block routes, be struck and be dispersed/killed. It is not merely an illusion or demon. Destruction collapses it into recoverable Genesis Dust in the workshop's resource economy. Exact yield, anatomy or dispersal threshold is not fixed.

| Family | Inherited characteristic |
|---|---|
| Red | Contact/physicality: claws, teeth, body force; still lethal. “Fuzzy ball” is a low-coherence example, not mandatory spherical anatomy. |
| Orange | Contact plus energy intrusion: fatigue, destabilization, internal harm and additional healing burden. |
| Yellow | Force-bearing ranged projection: orbs knock down, beams dent armor, rings push as expanding walls. |
| Green | Major professional threshold: resonance-reinforced persistent juggernauts, harder to disperse. Nine feet and an hour-long team fight are examples. |
| Blue | Expressed projection with secondary effects: explosive orbs, burning-through beams, detonating/shearing/burning rings. Not every entity has every attack. |
| Violet | High Genesis-expression, maege-like effects, rare knowledge and legendary threat; not just a stronger Blue. |

Broad categories are A=Red/Orange/Yellow, B=Green/Blue, C=Violet. Qualitative transition shorthand is 1,1,2,2,3 jumps across adjacent colors, not damage multipliers. There is no ordinary White eldris in the supplemental inherited palette. The White domai core is not one.

Source S3/closed-manifold wording is structural intuition, not a newly proved closure operator. Distorted antlers/horns/limbs and half-formed silhouettes are possible while physical persistence remains real. Eldris are environmental pressure, not morally evil organizations. They form in dungeons **and** suitable Wilds/storm collapse; only dungeon-born compatible strata impose the discussed stratum confinement.
