# Genesis ecology, luminous flora and vaen

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/ENVIRONMENT/GENESIS_FLORA_FAUNA_AND_VAEN.md`

Ordinary organisms, Genesis-adapted organisms and Genesis-dependent organisms are distinct. Non-Genesis life does not require specialized Genesis-dependent biology despite inhabiting a Genesis Starscape. Adapted organisms benefit from additional field properties but can survive without that benefit. Dependent organisms require suitable ongoing Genesis interaction and eventually fail without it. Luminescence alone does not establish dependence; field-adapted life need not glow.

Local collapse can alter a region and interweave flora/fauna growth with the Genesis Field. Veins/channels and colored expression may form in tissues. The two supplied blue/pink luminous-forest images and Pandora comparison provide aesthetic/behavioral reference, not named species or a literal import of another fictional ecosystem. The original images are preserved.

vaen are specialty natural resources with Genesis-relevant identities/properties. They may form through local field/biological relationships, in dungeons, in storms/domai or following collapse. They are not all ordinary chemical elements and need not be merely energy-rich. A luminous fungus can be useful to an alchemist but qualifies as Binding vaen only when it bears the relevant identity. Inputs can carry multiple identities.

The term is not interchangeable with Genesis crystals, Genesis Dust, diamonds, or alchemical Type 2–5 material. Those categories interact in engineering while retaining distinct functions. No flora roster, universal chromatic pigment map, guaranteed per-cycle vaen yield or numerical rarity is invented.
