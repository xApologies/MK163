# Genesis Mines — one basin, continuing oscillation, finite growth equilibrium

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/ENVIRONMENT/GENESIS_MINES.md`

A Genesis Mine isolates exactly one ordinary Red–Violet basin through a persistent local environmental/prismatic configuration. Its normal crystal output is monochromatic. It is a renewable Genesis-field pocket rather than merely an ordinary mineral ore body. Do not turn it into a multi-stratum dungeon or infer ordinary White/Black mines.

Its local field oscillates and can nucleate/grow matching crystals. It has a finite preferred stable crystalline occupancy/capacity. At equilibrium the oscillations continue, but no additional **net** crystal growth occurs. Harvesting reduces occupancy, allowing later oscillations to replenish the missing crystals. Renewable does not mean unbounded accumulation. One oscillation is not guaranteed to restore all missing material.

Internal oscillation frequency, recurrence of hazardous release/growth events, event duration, and worker exposure time are four different quantities. Red is qualitatively slower and Violet faster; no fixed 17-day schedule, cycle equation, mine geometry or extraction law is adopted.

Current AERA qualitative exposure examples: Red tingling and often tolerable short exposure; Orange temporary numbness; Yellow headache and concern with repetition, evacuation customary; Green even one oscillation can cause lasting neurological harm; Blue severe lasting damage expected; Violet extreme and likely fatal. These are fictional Genesis neurological interactions, not real radiation-dose instructions. Lower colors are not universally harmless and equilibrium does not make ongoing oscillations safe.

Worker shifts, evacuation, safety methods, recovery, neurological treatment, mine lifetime, yields and economic regulation remain author-defined. Crystal bead/marble/honeycomb morphology is preserved only as an older source example, not a mandatory modern crystal shape.

AERA's newer mine publication replaces old Genesis Stones with crystals and rejects mandatory stone absorption for protection, will-based release/extraction, compulsory penal mines, universal stone-backed ELN, old stone-specific syndromes and dust rules. The workshop initially deferred mines while the update uploaded; the current AERA source is now available and inherited. Valuable mines can justify Crown Village Hub development even when their detailed workings stay in background narration.
