# domai — temporary bounded Genesis-event landscapes

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/ENVIRONMENT/DOMAI.md`

A domai is an environment, not a conscious organism or an eldris. Suitable bounded collapse creates an anomalous temporary landscape containing possible entities and resources. The inherited environmental classes are Red through Violet; there is no White domai class simply because its structural core is White.

A non-conscious **superposed White Genesis Crystal** is its structural core. It does not think or control monsters. Inhabitants are not a collective mind of that core. The White crystal is not an eighth or ninth domain.

Lifespan generally increases with basin, but no universal time/size is defined. Internal geometry is anomalous/non-isometric. Nearby depth remains usable; farther depth information degrades toward a blurred, nearly two-dimensional projection-like horizon. This is a perceptual/geometric effect, not literal flattening of the entire landscape or a fixed visibility radius.

Natural expiration or destruction of the core can trigger unraveling. Dissipation is progressive, not instant deletion or guaranteed automatic ejection of occupants. It creates a final dangerous harvest/evacuation interval whose exact duration is not defined. Possible contents include eldris, Genesis crystals, ordinary and White Genesis Dust, vaen and other scarce materials; no yield is guaranteed.

Storm chasers specialize in working this uncertain transient environment. The inherited MK147 response example allows local vaelle/specialists for lower Red/Orange incidents and kingdom military involvement at Yellow and above, with vaelum support. This is a response example, not a new fixed total military doctrine.

The older permanent Domai Heart/Class-0 Chimera source is not current. Do not use the preserved Genesis Point faction PDFs to reinstate that ontology, merge domai indefinitely, grant a conscious core or import old Sanctuary suppression architecture.
