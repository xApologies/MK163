# Planetary map — accepted visual reference and numerical cautions

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** VISUAL REFERENCE; SOURCE ANNOTATIONS RETAINED WITH CONFLICT NOTES  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/GEOGRAPHY/WORLD_MAP_REFERENCE.md`

The supplied AERA World Map is the planetary cartographic reference. Preserve its original bytes and recognizable geography rather than redrawing it. It shows Western, Central and Eastern Oceans; Northwestern/Northeastern/Southern Expanses; Northern and Southern Poles; extensive mountains, forests, deserts, plains, rivers and lakes; warm/cold current arrows; two major whirlpool systems; and a World Tree.

The image prints “Diameter: 80,000 miles (~2.5x Earth)”. These two labels are not arithmetically consistent under the ordinary Earth-diameter comparison. The workshop accepted the map as supplied but did not reconcile the mismatch. Preserve 80,000 miles as the stated source-map dimension with the discrepancy visible; do not silently replace it, reuse the erroneous comparison as verified, or recalculate unrelated orbital parameters. The legend's roughly five-mile World Tree and three-to-five-times-Earth mountain heights are image annotations, not independently surveyed MK163 dimensions.

The polar “Violet Realm” labels and “Six Realms. One World” caption do not override the selected seven-realm/White Material Realm structure. This is a planetary map, not proof that all chromatic realms are merely geographic biomes on that planet. Kaira's kingdom has not been placed on the world map. No kingdom borders, scale-derived voyage times or new geographic names should be invented from it.

The huge oceans provide context for wooden ships, maritime trade and pirate logistics. Detailed navigation and global cartography remain a future narrative task, not a requirement to re-engineer this reference now.
