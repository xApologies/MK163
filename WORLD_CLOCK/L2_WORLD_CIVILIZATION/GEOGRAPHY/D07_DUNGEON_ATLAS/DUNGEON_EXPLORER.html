Authority boundary

The 6 depth bands and 24-mile maximum depth are author-selected. Horizontal spans, node placement and passage geometry are D07 design proposals. This Phase 2 atlas leaves environmental regions and detailed traversal OPEN.

A line crossing is not a junction without a shared node ID. A connection is geological topology, not proof that the party can walk it. Equipment, fatigue, accessibility, ecology placement and travel times remain open.

How to explore

Drag to pan. Use the mouse wheel or +/- buttons to zoom. Click a node to see its coordinates, view-order index and all neighbors. Type R1, R001 or D07-R001 to find Red 1. Width aliases such as Y-W056 find the existing node without creating a second identity.
