# Kaira’s Village Hub and the northwest Hollowmere dungeon settlement

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/GEOGRAPHY/KAIRA_STARTING_REGION.md`

The starting regional map has a stated 20-mile radius. Kaira lives in its half-mile-square Village Hub, a Crown development nucleus on the small-city track. The nearby dungeon, not an automatically imported orange Genesis mine, is the workshop's stated development reason.

Hollowmere is the map's current working label for site #6, **northwest of the Village Hub**, at the dungeon. It is already a staging/service settlement supporting visiting delvers, merchants and equipment traffic while the broader region is being developed. Do not relocate it east or replace the supplied map with a contrary diagram.

The eventual 7×7-mile city grows around the Village Hub, which becomes its White civic center. Hollowmere remains a separate satellite extension connected to that city, roughly eleven miles from the center in the author's working estimate. Its staging/service footprint may be about 1×1 or 2×2 miles. These are working distances/scales, not measured from the image during this package.

The separation keeps dungeon visitors near the dungeon and reduces unnecessary congestion through the city center. Hollowmere is not a second Crown Village Hub, not the future central civic district and not automatically an independent Crown city. Satellite villages support roads, patrols, mapping, surveys and economic development.

The place label is retained as a working geographic identifier. No permanent final renaming has been made. The starting hub itself remains unnamed in the current plot. Arc One's blacksmith direction is a proposal for narrative development, not permission to populate family names, scenes or dates from MK147.
