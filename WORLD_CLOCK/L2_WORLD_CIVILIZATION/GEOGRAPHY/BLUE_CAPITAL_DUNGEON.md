# Blue Domain capital dungeon · depth and research sites

The entrance serves the Blue Domain capital and its 2 × 2 mile staging district. Depths measure vertical distance below the entrance. Mapped passage lengths can be substantially longer. The Green Domain city associated with the Violet Dungeon has its own geography.

| Stratum | Thickness | Depth below entrance | Local oscillation period |
|---|---|---|---|
| Red | 1.5 miles | 0–1.5 miles | 11 days |
| Orange | 2 miles | 1.5–3.5 miles | 9 days |

| Green | 5 miles | 6.5–11.5 miles | 6 days |
| Blue | 5.5 miles | 11.5–17 miles | 4 days |

Red through Blue account for 17 vertical miles. Violet’s depth, extent, access and the absolute bottom remain OPEN. The first research expedition reaches a site approximately 2.5 miles inside Green, about 9 miles below the entrance. Its Yellow research site is unspecified. A separate Yellow harvesting site is a permanent, naturally formed cavern several miles across. Its name, entrance, precise dimensions and coordinates are OPEN.

The research party studies natural ru’ne, sigils and velis in Yellow and Green. A natural velis is a locally stabilized Genesis Field anchor that forms without a practitioner. Its geometry can be studied without visible carvings. [Architects and velis](../PROFESSIONS/ARCHITECTS_AND_VELIS.md) explains the inherited term for engineered analogues.
