# Violet Dungeon City — new geographic decisions
10×10-mile square mature city. Concentric orthogonal square districts Red→Orange→Yellow→Green→Blue→Violet→White. Red/Orange/Yellow generally commoner and working households; greatest lifestyle jump Yellow→Green, then Blue affluent, Violet luxury. White civic center, limited governing noble residence. No slums by district definition: clean, maintained stone infrastructure, water, sanitation, greenery and civic services throughout. Red may have some nighttime petty crime, not a blanket stigma.

Three principal gates per side = 12; normally open, defensively closable. Violet dungeon NW of city ~5 miles away (distance reference point open), one of two Violet dungeons in territorial royal domain; exact jurisdiction open. Dungeon stratified R/O/Y/G/B/V. Independent 1×1 to 2×2-mile staging hub (size provisional) absorbs concentrated delver traffic, offers inns, shops, food, crafts, repairs at proximity premium. Northern gates handle significant traffic. City has full independent civilian economy.
