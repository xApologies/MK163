# Crown settlement hierarchy and generations of development

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/CIVILIZATION/SETTLEMENT_HIERARCHY.md`

The Crown hierarchy is Capital City → Large City → Small City → Village Hub. Capitals have approximately 13×13-mile square perimeters, large cities 10×10 miles, small cities 7×7 miles. These are city dimensions, not territorial-domain diameters. Ordinary towns and dungeon-support settlements are not an extra formal Crown hierarchy rung.

The Crown chooses a 20–30-mile-radius development region for a dungeon, Genesis mine, other resource, agricultural opportunity or strategic benefit. A small-city-track Village Hub begins about 0.5×0.5 mile; a large-city-track hub about 1×1 mile. The hub is the administrative/settlement nucleus. Satellite villages support surveying, exploration, guard posts, road patrols, resource work and farming.

Over approximately 60–80 years the region is tamed, surveyed, mapped, protected and economically developed. A subsequent roughly 11–13-year construction/redevelopment process uses/reorganizes the region and builds the city outward from the original hub. The hub becomes the White civic center. Earlier MK147's roughly ten-year reconstruction is the older source value; the workshop refines it to 11–13, not a rigid law every settlement must precisely satisfy.

Wilds conditions and roving eldris explain walled settlements and protected agricultural regions. Local Genesis collapse can produce dangerous groups even when no dungeon is nearby. Sigil stabilization suppresses environmental storm/collapse conditions; walls and patrols address physical threats. The two forms of protection are not interchangeable.
