# Concentric square city districts and economic occupancy

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/CIVILIZATION/CHROMATIC_DISTRICTS.md`

Every Crown city uses nested **square rings**, not wedge sectors, separate horizontal strips or arbitrary blocks. Red is outermost. In a small 7×7-mile city, moving inward gives Red → Orange → Yellow → White center. In a large or capital city it is Red → Orange → Yellow → Green → Blue → Violet → White center. Exact ring widths and district acreages are not assigned.

The rings express economic occupancy, property value, amenities and luxury. Moving inward increases typical residence size, restaurants/shops/entertainment quality and prices. Red has smaller residences and lower taxes/costs, including an illustrative 20×20-foot, 400-square-foot apartment. Do not use the dictation's self-corrected “400 by 400”.

Red and Orange are not slums. All districts have a maintained, clean city environment with Genesis utilities. Lower income means fewer luxuries and smaller housing, not required filth, homelessness or decaying buildings. A well-maintained affordable neighborhood is part of the intended romanticized civic setting.

The White center is the administrative/civic-management district, not the final luxury housing band. Ordinary residential occupancy is not its role. Color districts do not assign residents' energy-basin access, biological type, professional ability or automatic individual rank. Diagram colors label district identities; no incorrect earlier assistant layout is retained as a map.
