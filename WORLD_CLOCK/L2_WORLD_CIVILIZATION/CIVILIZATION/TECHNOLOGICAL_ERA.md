# Technological divergence: preindustrial aesthetics, advanced Genesis engineering

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/CIVILIZATION/TECHNOLOGICAL_ERA.md`

The selected aesthetic is romanticized late seventeenth/early eighteenth century, before an Earth-style Industrial Revolution. Technology develops through sigils, Genesis-field construction, Alchemy, dimensional engineering and mathematical knowledge.

“No metal” was explicitly corrected: metal production and shaping remain. Tools, weapons, coins, chainmail, jewelry, alchemical metallurgy and suitable components are compatible. What is absent is the iron-beam/metal-ship/steam-engine trajectory of large mechanized industrial development. There is no steam engine or ironclad industry implied by advanced material knowledge.

Ships are wooden sailing vessels with conventional craft and any separately established Genesis support. The premise is a divergent civilization, not one incapable of complex technology or a ban on every mechanism. Do not insert engines, cars, guns or modern electronic grids from an analogy to technology in the author's world. Food/drink and formal/luxury culture remain compatible with the selected setting.
