# Crown military, Special Projects and Crown Intelligence

**Status:** author-selected MK163 institutions; exact ranks, names and command limits OPEN.

## Regular military and the Book Yellow recruitment distinction

The kingdom’s military has three selected functional components: conventional kingdom-versus-kingdom forces; guards including royal, city and road protection; and forces fighting ELDRIS/domai. These descriptions do not supply official branch names or a complete hierarchy. At a regular royal raeon game during Book Yellow Green, Kaira meets an unnamed general associated with the ELDRIS/domai forces and is recruited into that regular branch. She completes the three Blackstone semesters before Blue service and military training. Blue ends with her first operational domai entry; Violet is a multiple-domai theater culminating in a merged engagement. Commendations and **Special Projects recruitment occur only in White**. No exact feat is selected.

## Independent Crown command

Special Projects is an independent command directly under the Crown. Its existence is generally known but activities are poorly understood. It recruits exceptionally capable people and has unusual magical tools/resources. Three functional branches cover conventional warfare, ELDRIS/domai operations, and intelligence/espionage. Only **Crown Intelligence** is an author-selected formal branch label here. No detailed roster, headquarters or rank hierarchy is supplied.

Kaira enters Book Green as a newly recruited high-authority officer of **Crown Intelligence**, the intelligence/espionage branch of **Special Projects**, an independent Crown command. Other Special Projects branches address conventional warfare and ELDRIS/Domai operations. The Crown Intelligence officer's authority is directly below Princess **illi** in the relevant established authority relationship and generally can supersede ordinary command structures; formal rank and limits are OPEN. The old operative label “Reaper” is **author-level legacy only**, not canonical; a replacement following ae/ai naming conventions is OPEN. Princess illi is a Crown diplomat, a gifted Transduction practitioner, and Kaira's close friend; she is **not** a Special Projects operative. She was not a domain governor.



## Service and deployment boundaries

Book Green Red supplies accelerated officer training in diplomatic conduct, intelligence gathering and espionage responsibilities. Green Arc combines formal Special Projects shaping instruction with independent Violet Dungeon practice, roughly splitting her time; no permanent escort or new Blackstone course is inferred. Book Blue Orange attaches Kaira to a coordinated division responding to three merged domai, not a reassignment to ordinary monster combat. In the multinational anti-piracy campaign she remains Crown Intelligence / Special Projects, **not transferred to the navy**. Her primary operational role in the central resource islands is land-based attacks; her precise contribution at floating fortresses remains OPEN. She is not responsible for every coalition battle.

No special command power, tactical plan, security procedure or new magic mechanism is inferred from these descriptive institutional facts.
