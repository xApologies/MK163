# Resolution, Bandwidth, Occupancy, and Chirality — scope-preserved source use

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L0_MATHEMATICS/RESOLUTION_BANDWIDTH_AND_CHIRALITY.md`

Resolution and Bandwidth are top-level organizational quantities in the inherited Genesis mathematics. They are not interchangeable labels for personal magical rank, an energy-basin identity, or a quantity of mana. Personal available bandwidth is finite; different individuals can have different capacities. Binding installation consumes a persistent share of this capacity under L1's selected rule.

The author used the chirality byte `0 1 1 0 1 0 0 1` as an illustrative occupancy pattern, where each 1 signifies an occupied energy state. The corresponding example `0, Red, Orange, 0, Green, 0, 0, Blue` assigns ordinal weights 1,2,4,5 to the four occupied entries. Their arithmetic average is 3. This is an illustrative average-over-occupied-sites calculation, not a claim that a diamond's physical microstate is exactly eight sites or that charge colors obey a proved universal ordinal-energy equation. In the example, the denominator is four occupied sites, not eight positions.

Do not replace the native source mathematics with a speculative capacity formula. Source equations and definitions are retained in the supplied native API and Tome. The author intentionally deferred complete mathematical transcription from _bricked. No exact percent cost has been assigned to every Binding. CSR's roughly 60% example is illustrative rather than a universal installation cost. No linear law converts an aen surface area, a practitioner's rank, or a chip's color directly into infinite throughput.
