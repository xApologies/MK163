# Kaira’s fourth Blue Arc expedition · Green cave-in rescue

On Day 1054, while Kaira collects her Yellow-harvest payment, exhausted members of another research expedition reach the Blue capital’s staging Registry. They had been heading for a known research objective in Blue. A physical cave-in split the party deep in Green, about 10–11 vertical miles below the entrance. The members on the surface-accessible side withdrew with a map of the known passage and the obstruction. Their escort and equipment could not safely clear it. Other researchers and escorts remained stranded on the deep side.

The site lies within Green (6.5–11.5 miles). Blue begins at 11.5 miles. The passage keeps its mapped position. The cave-in’s underlying cause and time, and the hour the survivors reached the Registry on 1054, remain OPEN. Green’s six-day local oscillation and the confinement of ELDRIS to compatible strata still apply. ## Volunteer call, passage clearance and return

The Registry calls for local dungeon-delving aithren. Kaira signs up as a Green-certified environmental Support. The large, unpaid, multi-vaelum rescue needs structural and excavation expertise, Guardians and combat specialists for ELDRIS, Helkir for medical care, environmental Supports for Genesis pressure, guides, provisions and logistical support. The stranded group is exhausted. Potential injuries and dwindling supplies inform the rescue plan. How the group sheltered, sustained shielding and provisioned itself during the wait remains OPEN.

| Story days | Days inclusive | Selected work |
|---|---:|---|
| 1055–1064 | 10 | Rapid descent along the mapped route, with necessary rest, security and environmental shielding. |
| 1065–1067 | 3 | Rotating crews stabilize and clear the obstruction, reopening the passage. |
| 1068–1080 | 13 | A slower ascent with stranded personnel, supplies, assistance, shielding and medical support as needed. |

On Day 1080 the force reaches the surface and the Registry closes the operation. Kaira receives a positive dossier commendation for completed unpaid service and retains Green certification. The rescue is complete. Numbers and individual fates of rescuers and stranded personnel, injuries, methods of excavation, precise encounters and personal acts remain OPEN.

## Day 1080 dinner and acceptance

That evening six members of an existing vaelum dine with Kaira in the Blue capital dungeon staging settlement. They served beside her as one group in the larger rescue. They have been training at dungeons for the King’s Tournament and seeking an environmental Support. Kaira gets along well with them at dinner and accepts their invitation as their seventh member. Her acceptance closes the Blue Arc.
