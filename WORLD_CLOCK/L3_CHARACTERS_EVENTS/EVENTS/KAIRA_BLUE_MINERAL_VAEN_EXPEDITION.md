# Kaira’s second Blue Dungeon contract · mineral vaen

On Day 952, after returning from the royal visit, Kaira applies at the staging Registry with illi present and is accepted for the expedition. Days 953–958 cover preparation and personal time. Day 959 begins the 41-day commercial expedition into Blue.

| Days | Assignment |
|---|---|
| 959–975 | Seventeen days of descent through Red, Orange, Yellow and Green to Blue. The mapped route includes difficult passages and subterranean rivers or lakes. The party travels, rests, fights and recovers resources along the way. |
| 976–982 | Seven additional days in Blue for exploration, oscillation monitoring, harvesting and ELDRIS combat. Rare internally luminous mineral vaen are a target. The four-day local cycle allows more than one opportunity, with phase and successful formation OPEN. |
| 983–999 | Seventeen days of ascent with opportunistic harvesting, rest, combat and traversal. On Day 999 the party surfaces, closes out at the Crown Accord Registry and Kaira collects her payment. |

The permanent vaelum fields three Guardians, four Offensive Specialists, two Helkir and two combat Support Specialists. Four contracted environmental Supports including Kaira bring the total to **15 registered aithren**. Nyx and the independent Black Sprite accompany her. Combat Supports protect against ELDRIS attacks while environmental Supports rotate shields against ambient pressure and fatigue. The Shield attaches to the recipient’s Genesis field. The Sprite uses their own protective Shaping. Kaira has no Dimensional Ring. Others may carry rings or bracelets.

The party collects qualifying crystals, ELDRIS-derived Dust and vaen opportunistically throughout all three blocks. Extended Blue exposure and combat fatigue limit how long the party can stay. This is Kaira’s first major exposure to Blue ELDRIS’ advanced expressed projections. Individual fights, specific casualties, formation times, yield and payment amount remain OPEN. Her Green rank stands. Day 999 is both the last travel day and Registry closeout. She spends Days 1000–1001 recovering before the Day 1002 visit to the King’s salon.
