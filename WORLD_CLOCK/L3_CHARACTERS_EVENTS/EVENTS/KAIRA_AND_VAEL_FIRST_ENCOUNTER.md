# OR-07 — First Vael conflict at neighboring abecca/raeon shops

**Book Red · Orange · 2026-11-08.**

## Character identity and source validation boundary

**Vael** = V-A-E-L. The author said "I think we named him Vale, V-A-E-L; double check" and then continued selecting the identity for this encounter. Earlier assistant identified Vael as a proposed name from an October 12 workshop; GitHub searches in current MK163 and Mk-147 did not verify that older proposal as an accepted canonical character entry. Treat **Vael as the current author-selected working name**, preserve the uncertainty about earlier name history, and DO NOT invent a family name, noble rank, lordship, ancestral estate or father. The November 7 character record does call him Blue-ranked and places the collision at a smithy. That wording remains historical: the current decision leaves his exact rank, family standing and tournament history unverified; do not silently promote those details without source.

Vael is a **nobleman's son**, proud, status-conscious, disdainful, prejudiced toward Afflicted people. There are **two friends** with him, a **three-person group counting Vael**. Their names, personality, whether they echo, laugh, disagree or intervene remain OPEN.

## Shop geography and doors

The **southeast hospitality/shopping area** contains a working **abecca** shop directly beside a large **raeon card shop**. These commercial shops use the story's open-faced entrances, with barrier sigils for closed hours rather than necessarily heavy conventional doors. Kaira is exiting the abecca shop and walking around the adjoining corner toward the raeon shop; Vael is leaving the raeon shop. Distinct from operational Genesis Cards, these are collectible/playable raeon Cards made of **maege-glass**. The broader game rules live in MK163 culture and _raeon source; do not invent a new rulebook.

## The accident — precise beat sequence

1. Earlier the same day Kaira gained two promising smithy contacts and arranged freight, after initial Registry anxiety. She is still celebrating/relieved, enjoying a day of independence; she has an ordinary backpack, no Ring.
2. She exits the neighboring abecca shop and rounds the corner toward the card shop; she is curious about the available game Cards and looking about rather than carefully watching the doorway.
3. Vael leaves with his two friends. They have been conversing about their Cards. He is looking at **two Cards held in his hand**, newly bought or at least one being newly purchased, and is **not looking where he is going**.
4. Kaira and Vael **collide accidentally** as they converge at the shop entrance/corner. **Both Cards are dropped** onto the paved stone ground.
5. In this encounter, the **maege-glass Cards remain undamaged**. The author specifically stressed that dropping the cards does not realistically scratch or injure them here; avoid turning that into a universal new proof of unconditional imperviousness of all maege-glass, and don't invent a reimbursement or broken-card subplot.
6. Vael is instantly outraged on the basis of his **noble station**: he feels it an intolerable affront that somebody collided with someone above them socially. It is his status, not tangible card damage, that fuels the outburst.
7. He then sees Kaira's white hair/white skin and **automatically categorizes her as untreated Afflicted**, paying no attention to her actual healthy Awakened physiology. He despises her and makes belittling/prejudiced remarks, looking down on her as worthless. No exact lines selected.
8. Kaira first feels shocked/put upon, and then experiences significant **genuine anger**. She knows collision was an accident and both were distracted; Vael's self-importance and cruelty make no sense to her. Past childhood/indenture shame, isolation and abuse are emotionally reactivated; anger is not suddenly triumphant social dominance, duel or magical retaliation. The user's intent is "Kaira gets angry for the first time" in response to being treated this way.
9. Kaira **extricates herself** from the confrontation and goes into the card shop; she does not require a won argument, public punishment, immediate challenge/duel or physical injury to Vael.
10. Initially flustered, she relives Afflicted childhood memories; the pleasurable experience of browsing her beloved raeon Cards gradually helps restore composure. She examines offerings and strategies rather than keeping her mind exclusively on Vael.
11. **Staging-area card prices are startlingly high** under the broad 1.3–1.7 locality premium. Kaira chooses **NOT to purchase any raeon Cards** despite browsing for some time. These cards are distinct from the three-basin General Metal Manipulation **operational** Genesis Card she earlier bought in the large city's Green District.
12. She exits the card shop and begins her journey back to the large city, later choosing the forest detour in OR-08.

## Character relationships and future seven-book scope

This is the **first meeting** and origin of a long-running **rivalry / arch-nemesis relationship** between Kaira and Vael across the seven-book saga. There was an original thought to stage a similar noble collision at Village Hub in Red; it was later inherited and relocated to Orange's Violet Dungeon staging community, with an intermediate October 23 concept outside a smithy. **Current author scene is beside the raeon shop**, and older placements must remain visible as superseded provenance, not parallel first encounters. The exact structure of future hostility, meetings, class/rank, plot consequences, eventual reconciliation or non-reconciliation is OPEN. It doesn't require importing MK147 story wholesale.

## Emotional arc and limits

The encounter interrupts an otherwise positive day: Kaira has two professional openings, a Registry posting and freight solution. She is **not permanently defeated by the interaction**; inside the card shop she returns to her hobby, recovers some enjoyment, sees prices she cannot justify and returns home. Angry and wounded are not mutually exclusive. Nyx and Black Sprite are not with her in this moment: Nyx is **not adopted until Orange's third dungeon-related return**, Sprite's first bond is in Yellow. Do not place them in this first Orange shop confrontation.
