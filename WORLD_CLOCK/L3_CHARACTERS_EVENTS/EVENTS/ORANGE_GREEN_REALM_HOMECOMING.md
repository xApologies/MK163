# Book Orange — Red Arc — Green Realm event authority (Checkpoint 006)

## Scope and provenance
This file supersedes the active event-outline portions of checkpoint 005, while retaining all earlier ZIPs unchanged in ARCHIVE. G-000 and G-001 are established; G-002 through G-005 are author-selected **working outlines**, not completed narrative prose. Do not invent precise times, casualty counts, distances, storm parameters, community names, dialogue, or an explanation for the Black Sprite's earlier displacement.

## G-000 — Enter the Green Realm
- White Realm Day 1663: expedition reaches P11 at 1700 and enters sometime afterward; crossing hour open.
- Kaira, Princess illi, Nyx (MALE; he/him), Black Sprite, and small flight-capable Crown entourage including regional guide.
- P11 is a natural, permanently active crossing. The search begins without knowing the Black Sprite's original community's location.

## G-001 — First Sprite colony
- Journey north from P11 to inhabited, still-growing colossal tree approximately 650 meters high.
- Sprites use native Resolution to shape chambers and passages in living Genesis-expressive wood; they absorb energy emitted by trees for sustenance.
- First collective hive-mind encounter for Kaira and illi. Collective speaks as 'we' and communicates through imagery; Black Sprite already communicates with Kaira.
- Community notices Black Sprite's difference but does not automatically know its origin or the Black Prophecy.
- Vegetarian feast for human visitors using locally edible flora, and coordinated Genesis-orb light show (Red–Violet); ordinary Sprite luminescence Green.
- Visitors rest and depart. Duration, tree species, and colony size remain open.

## G-002 — The inverted sea and storm on the horizon
- The party encounters a vast lake / small inland sea, with an inverted upper landmass (roughly one or two miles across, illustrative) and a water cycle flowing upward toward upper water and downward elsewhere.
- Rather than travel around the sea, the party uses CSR to reach upper land. Orientation changes make the transition difficult. Kaira falls 'up' onto the upper ground without injury; illi, a prodigy at Transduction, handles it nimbly and teases her.
- Upper land is intermittent: islands, land bridges, and large expanses of open sky; no continuous second ceiling. Large colossal habitation trees are separated by significant distances; many other large trees reach hundreds of meters.
- An independent Sprite hive mind, taking the form of an **Eldryn (Earthstrider Grazer)**, encounters the party. Its collective size is unspecified. It identifies possible communities/routes and a distant colossal inhabited tree.
- From the elevated perspective, the party sees a **Genesis storm** on the horizon near that tree. The Black Sprite urges the group to go help. This is an author-selected decision, not a coincidence that forces a new quest.
- They race toward the threatened community by CSR, navigating near terrain, around huge trees, over land bridges and islands, through changing local orientation. Repeated pulls toward different surfaces make flight sporadic and hazardous. CSR is still functional; geometric navigation is the difficulty.
- G-002 ends as they reach the threatened region; no fixed travel duration or distance.

## G-003 — Stormbound colossal tree: defense and recovery
- An active Genesis storm is causing **multiple separate localized Genesis collapses**, not one perpetually self-reproducing collapse. The storm is a broader environmental disturbance. No new continuous-collapse mechanism is introduced; no domai is required.
- Local collapses produce multiple ELDRIS and may yield Genesis crystals and other source-permitted products.
- ELDRIS observed: **predominantly Green** reinforced, physically formidable juggernauts; some Red close-combat and Blue expressed-projection types. No fixed proportions, number, or guaranteed exact distribution. Green Realm's roughly 80% Green / 20% other-basin makeup is environmental composition, not an ELDRIS spawn-probability equation.
- Green ELDRIS in this encounter are physical fighters, not Yellow/Blue/Violet-style ranged energy attackers. Author proposes that their physical attacks **also cause energy intrusion**, notwithstanding the older AERA family summary identifying Orange intrusion; this is a proposed MK163 refinement requiring explicit AERA reconciliation, not an already verified inherited universal rule.
- Native Sprites defend themselves effectively with Resolution and large collective hive-mind forms. Damage to a combined form can injure its constituent Sprites; contact intrusion may spread corruption across the collective. Exact healing, propagation, severity, and mortality remain open.
- The royal party joins a long, exhausting defense. The storm naturally subsides; after it ends, defenders defeat remaining ELDRIS. Nobody 'switches off' Genesis Collapse and no perpetual-collapse engine is established.
- Recovery follows. The Black Sprite, drawing on Resolution-based healing experience from Kaira's earlier dungeon-delving work, helps treat injured Sprites and possible corruption. Complete cures, casualty numbers and treatment details are NOT fixed.
- The community becomes grateful to the expedition. Its Sprites recognize where the Black Sprite came from and know how to reach the original community.

## G-004 — Guided journey and homecoming
- The rescued community supplies a native Sprite guide who knows the route to the Black Sprite's original community.
- The journey is prolonged through non-ordinary geometry. Sprites travel freely between upper/lower land; other inhabitants, including CSR users, must navigate local orientation and difficult terrain.
- Destination: the Black Sprite's original community in a colossal tree **on upper/inverted land**.
- The guide makes introductions, then returns to its own community rather than remaining with the human expedition.
- The Black Sprite is welcomed home. Celebration includes hospitality/feast and another Genesis-orb light show; its emotional purpose is reunion rather than first contact.
- No new prophecy revelation, permanent bond change, or invented history is required. Black Sprite remains bonded to Kaira and will return with the expedition.
- The Black Sprite's present lived experience began in the White Realm when Kaira bonded the Black crystal through a Binding Circle; the Green Realm journey allows it to experience its ancestral realm. Its exact earlier history and displacement mechanism remain open.

## G-005 — Return to P11
- The party travels back through the Green Realm. One or two **sporadic ordinary ELDRIS encounters** arise from independent localized Genesis collapses, not from the earlier storm or a continuous-collapse mechanism. Combat is comparatively minor and does not require a new major plot arc.
- A possible sighting of members of **Nyx's Vaeryn species** is deferred/optional, NOT established as an actual encounter. Nyx is male (he/him), and his species is distinct from Veshar.
- Party returns to P11 and crosses into the White Realm on **Day 1674**, eleven White Realm days after Day 1663 entry. Exact exit hour, Green Realm experienced duration and local clock remain open.

## Worldbuilding and authority boundaries
- Sprite native Resolution is not automatically an unlimited healing ability; collective injury and corruption mechanics can be developed during prose drafting.
- ELDRIS are persistent bounded collapse entities, distinct from the environmental Genesis storm and from individual localized Genesis Collapse events.
- AERA permits storms, collapses, and temporary bounded domai; this storyline selects a storm and does NOT select a domai.
- White Realm day is 31 hours; 1700 is hour 17, not Earth 5 p.m.
- The confirmed six illustrated compendium plates and their names in checkpoint 005 remain authoritative. Eldryn is the herbivore imitated by the independent collective.
- G-000 to G-005 form the full current Green Realm outline; scene-level development remains open.
