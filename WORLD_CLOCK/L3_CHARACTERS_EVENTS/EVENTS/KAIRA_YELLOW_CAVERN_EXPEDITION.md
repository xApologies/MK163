# Kaira’s third Blue Dungeon contract · failed Yellow cavern expedition

Kaira applies for three expeditions on Day 1019. On Day 1025 the Registry reports two acceptances. She chooses the Yellow cavern harvesting contract. The other accepted contract remains unidentified.

The seven-member vaelum consists of two Guardians, three Offensive Specialists, one combat Support Specialist and one Helkir. To limit overhead it hires two environmental Supports, Kaira and one other, each required to hold at least Green certification. They rotate shielding against ambient pressure. **Nine registered aithren** depart. Nyx and the Black Sprite accompany Kaira independently. The target is eleven full harvesting days of Yellow ELDRIS-derived Dust and naturally grown crystals.

| Days | Outcome |
|---|---|
| 1030–1036 | Morning rendezvous at the Crown Accord Registry on 1030 and seven days descending to Yellow. |
| 1037 | About half a day to the large cavern. The party establishes its operating position. |
| 1038–1040 | First three full harvesting days, with repeated Yellow ELDRIS engagements and recovery of qualifying resources. |
| 1041 | On the fourth harvesting day ELDRIS overwhelm the group and the vaelum’s sole Helkir is killed. Survivors disengage. The remaining seven planned harvesting days are canceled. |
| 1042–1048 | Seven-day emergency ascent without a Helkir. The survivors are injured and fatigued. Kaira sustains protection and the Black Sprite helps stabilize them. |
| 1049 | The remaining party surfaces. The Registry closes the failed expedition and records the casualty. Kaira receives her contracted share for qualifying material recovered and sold. |

The mission failed commercially and operationally. Kaira fulfilled her assigned protection and did not cause the death. During withdrawal she expanded from environmental protection to repeated combat shielding and used the new Genesis Beam for controlled offense. The Black Sprite drew on limited Resolution-related pseudo-healing, informed by the healers they had observed at Blackstone. Their support helped the survivors escape. Severe injuries were beyond their full restorative capacity and the Helkir remained dead. Nyx’s individual actions and kill count are unspecified.

The survivors and Registry recognize Kaira’s endurance and defensive support. She remains an independent Green Support Specialist. Weights, individual injuries, encounters beyond the selected fatal engagement, final payout, long-term effects and further contracts remain OPEN.
