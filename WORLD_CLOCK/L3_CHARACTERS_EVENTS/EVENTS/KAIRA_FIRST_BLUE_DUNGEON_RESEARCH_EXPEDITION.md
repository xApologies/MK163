# Kaira’s first Blue Dungeon research contract

**Status** · Completed on Day 943 of Book Red. The Crown Accord Registry records a favorable evaluation. Kaira remains an independent Green Support Specialist.

## Application and preparation

After her examination on Day 905, Kaira buys the Green Transduction rank badge for 20 eln. Its emerald signifies Green and her Registry record specifies Support Specialist. At the help desk she finds openings with several vaelum and harvesting groups, as well as a research expedition. She submits her name for the research group’s Support vacancy. She waits at home on Days 906–908. A Registry missive arrives on 908 confirming her selection and setting departure five days later.

On Days 909–912 she practices her individual Genesis Shield Card on Nyx and the Black Sprite. She already has Fireball experience from practicing with illi at Blackstone. By observing Kaira, the Sprite learns an independent protective Shaping that can help resist a dungeon’s environmental Genesis-field pressure. Their Shaping differs from Kaira’s encoded Shield and occupies none of her Card’s basins. A final Shield practice on Day 913 is possible. That day Kaira reports at the appointed time, meets the party and enters the Blue Dungeon.

## What the assignment establishes

Dungeon-born ELDRIS are confined to their compatible strata. Environmental Genesis-field pressure can independently threaten biological coherence even in a secured area. The researchers study naturally occurring geometric relationships in the Genesis field. Natural ru’ne and velis can be studied without visible carved glyphs. [Natural and engineered anchors](../../L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/PROFESSIONS/ARCHITECTS_AND_VELIS.md) records how this kind of observation informed deliberate sigil engineering.

Kaira excels at long-duration environmental protection and can contribute some offense. The Sprite sustains their own shielding and has limited offensive and Resolution-related restorative abilities. Other support personnel share the work. Nyx, Kaira’s independent intelligent family companion, helps significantly against ELDRIS. His contribution gives him no separate Registry certification. The assignment leaves Kaira’s Green rank and her limits in multi-target monitoring and shield-integrity perception intact.

The party notices Kaira’s Chainmail Robe and learns she created it. They receive her warmly as both its inventor and an effective Support Specialist. She enjoys the work and wants three or four further assignments, including vaen or Genesis crystal harvesting and ELDRIS-focused delving. The next two contracts are now dated.
