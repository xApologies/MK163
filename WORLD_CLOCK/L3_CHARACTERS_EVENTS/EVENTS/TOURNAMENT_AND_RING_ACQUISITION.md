# Book Red endpoint — Green placement and Dimensional Ring

**Project:** MK163  
**Status:** ESTABLISHED / INHERITED WITH AUTHOR AMENDMENTS  
**Canonical path:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L3_CHARACTERS_EVENTS/EVENTS/TOURNAMENT_AND_RING_ACQUISITION.md`

Event ID: **MK163-EVENT-RED-TOURNAMENT-RING**.

At the end of Book Red, Kaira competes with a team of friends she has met through ordinary dungeon delving and the community. Her team reaches **fourth place, Green**, in the kingdom championship. She receives an exceptionally good **Dimensional Ring** as her reward. Fourth place is not the overall championship, a Green-only dungeon finish, or Kaira's professional qualification.

The exact team roster, qualification history, final race sequence, winner names, prize ceremony and ring grade have not been set. The source twenty-page ring master governs its operation; no White grade or numeric capacity is inferred from “really good.” Her reward is a ring rather than the higher-capacity bracelet alternative.

For most of Red's adventuring, Kaira carries ordinary packs and strapped equipment. She learns to pack light and live with practical inconvenience. The ring does not retroactively solve these experiences. From the end of Red onward, through Orange–White, it allows her to carry her life with her and move without a bulky pack. This is enduring logistics relief, not a flashy new attack or automatic inventory interface.

Supply choices and acquisition remain meaningful. The device removes repeated carrying bookkeeping, not the need to own or obtain the relevant items. Conscious recognition/familiarity governs retrieval. She cannot retrieve an object she never acquired solely because a later chapter would benefit from it.
