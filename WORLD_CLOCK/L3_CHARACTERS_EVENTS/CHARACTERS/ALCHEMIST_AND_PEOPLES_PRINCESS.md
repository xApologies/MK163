- Woman, generally known as **the Alchemist**; given name **OPEN**.
- **Self-made genius** of alchemical botany: Genesis-mediated manipulation of plant life, seeds, fruit, vineyards and biological material properties. Extraordinary international vintner, creative cultural personality and commercially astute negotiator.
- Family ordinary/non-noble, modest means; no extraordinary inherited genius. Mother as apothecary, father as craftsman with amateur Genesis mathematics/Architect hobby are **WORKING** backgrounds.
- Age approximately **87 local years** **WORKING**, culturally young relative to 400-year-old adults, but sophisticated and accomplished. Enjoys parties, artistic/intellectual gatherings, social circles (Warhol's Factory was user's vibe analogy, not a literal historical transplant).
- Cultivars have numeric identifiers, examples **63, 78, 137, 163, 173**; different vines express different properties; older vintages may be discontinued. No cultivar effect or exact recipe canonized.
- Some premium wines obtainable in elite restaurants, others rare/finite and coveted internationally. Known especially in Green/Blue/Violet high society, less known in lower districts.
- Exchanges wine allocations for favors, land, vineyard access, advantageous commercial terms, possible tax privileges. Actual king/queen trades, estates and cases are illustrative, not canonized transactions. She has cultural influence, not hereditary title or sovereign powers.
- Already good friend of princess (occasional lunches, gatherings, not inseparable). In Red Yellow, she arrives at Kaira's door seeking **a unique original handmade Chainmail Robe**, not an imitation. Wealth allows extravagant commission, exact design/terms open.
- Their professional respect becomes **best friendship/older-sister mentorship**; sponsors Kaira's Blackstone tuition, admission and campus housing at Yellow's close. A rare case exchanged with administrator is **POSSIBLE**, not locked admissions corruption or procedure.
- Facilitates Kaira's introduction to princess and opens elite merchant/noble/royal circles, while preserving Kaira's own agency and achievements.

## The People's Princess

- **27 local years** author-selected, youngest daughter of the royal family, older siblings. **NOT the Blue Domain governor.** No territorial administration. Has public royal/social obligations, enjoys freedom to travel and pursue education; compassionate and supports livelihoods and dignity for ordinary people.
- The existing territorial map still has Violet King/Queen, Orange and Green princes, Blue governed by **a princess**. This must be reconciled as a **different royal** (identity OPEN), not by assigning Blue to the youngest daughter.
- Highly exceptional native Genesis-expression prodigy, extraordinarily precise in concept and Shaping execution; trained by elite private tutors; attends advanced Blackstone Transduction/mathematics immersion during Kaira's Green Arc. Connected genealogically to historical archmaegi; names/genealogy open.
- In Book Red she exceeds Kaira's native-Genesis mastery, though Kaira may later surpass her. No present unlimited output or full historical archmaegi rank.
- Alchemist introduces Kaira; princess and Kaira rapidly become **inseparable best friends**, with independent lives and royal public responsibilities. Princess provides support in aristocratic settings and native Transduction learning; Alchemist provides broader social reach and mentoring.
