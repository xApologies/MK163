# Kaira — Arc Two phenotype, social ambiguity and daily life
Author-locked update to existing KAIRA.md; don't overwrite longer biography.
- Born rare inherited Afflicted, not a special nobility-only mutation; noble and royal Afflicted exist historically.
- White Awakening remakes biology and removes her pathology but keeps natural white skin, healthy white hair, steel-gray eyes (subtle internally contained luminescence), recognizably human but unmistakable enlarged orbital proportions, slightly larger eyes, angular/symmetrical exotic appearance, lean denser athletic frame and existing ELDRIS scars. No old black spectral eyes imported.
- General AERA Awakened anatomy is inherited, not rewritten; all compatible Afflicted could undergo similar restoration regardless of wealth.
- Awakened appearance readily recognized by public; not hidden by Afflicted complexion. Nobility overwhelmingly most able to obtain White Dust/Awakening, but rich merchants and private expedition recipients may also acquire it.
- Kaira's new Orange District apartment and low-cost living create status ambiguity. Neighbors speculate she is disowned/estranged daughter of an unknown noble family. Rumors reach noble circles who consider her possible genealogy, patronage, Crown ties and resources. Public lacks true collapse/potion story.
- Some commoners jealous, some indifferent or kind; nobles may be curious, calculating, or uninterested. Educated city less Affliction-superstitious than frontier Village Hub. Avoid universal social stereotypes.
- She isn't noble and has no established noble connections. Later Chainmail Robe/fashion identity amplifies recognition; throughout all seven books strangers often assume noble origin. Series-spanning motif.
- Breakfast bowl of fruit at home; little cooking skill, usually buys lunch and dinner in restaurants, spends time at abecca houses and markets. She likes collecting living plant vaen and keeping them in apartment.
- Apartment selection: three viewings, first opens with failed haggling. Residence NW Orange near northern gate, not exact coordinate. All individual reactions and prices remain open.
