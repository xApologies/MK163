# Nyx — independent Green-native feline, second principal character
Nyx (dictated Nix) rescued ~6–7-week-old black orphan on Kaira's third dungeon return, beside dead mother in orange-flowered cave. Father killed by hunters, mother fireball-injured and died after retreat; parents crossed Green Realm into White. Territorial enormous feline species with predatory appearance but NO meat diet: Green Realm The Unwritten naturally realizes living flowers (parents orange, other individuals may make different colors/forms), which gather sunlight and Genesis energy; cats nonlethally siphon sustenance. Flowers survive mother's death, sustain cub, later appear at Nyx's apartment sleeping corner. Secondary associations Living Art/Covenant Gardens, no new AERA mechanics. Energy extraction may stunt nearby vegetation. Nyx proud, affectionate on own terms, mischievous, friendly, Kaira's shadow/family; not a Binding, not a Sprite, not one of six Black bonds. Exceptional high-end individual of species, below fully merged Sprite hive; cannot hive-merge. Initial black cub later develops tiger-like dermal Genesis-channel stripes, grows fast into enormous mount, mascot, early/pseudo tank, later Primordial primary tank.
