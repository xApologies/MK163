| Family | Review and condition |

| Topology I | Foundations → continuity/connectedness → compactness/mappings/quotients is reasonable. The same axiom example repeats all 16 weeks. The literal doubled-brace family is not a topology; the likely single-brace family is. Preserve ambiguity; do not assume Week 1 pupils know later axioms. |
