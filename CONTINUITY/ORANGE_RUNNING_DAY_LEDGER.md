# Book Red · Orange running day ledger through Day 39

Day 0 is the ring commission, not the beginning of Book Red or an absolute calendar date. Count **31-hour days** and **six-day weeks**. Negative day numbers may be used when earlier chronology is reconstructed; month, weekday and exact clock times remain OPEN. Grouped days describe intervals, not mandatory scenes.

| Relative day | Current events | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Orders 300,000 rings from master mailwright on Yellow Blacksmith Row; 1,000 eln base. | Commission clock begins. |
| ~3 | First batch (~80,000 illustrative); begins 23–25k-ring Offset-Lattice demonstration shirt. | Not every batch size is selected. |
| ~6 | Second ring pickup; ordinary work and city routine. | Approximate. |
| ~9 | Third ring pickup and city routines. | Not the ninth elapsed shirt-work day. |
| 11 | Final pickup and end of eleven-day manufacture. Shirt finished after eight elapsed assembly days; consults mailwright and begins experimenting on the physical five-layer robe that evening. | Replaces ~11–12; robe not complete. |
| 12 · visit 1 | Asks to list repairs at the staging Registry, learns a professional rank is required and postpones. Hears the warning about territorial Vaeryn and leaving paths alone. Two refusals, third smithy interested, one further contact; independent freight referred. Sandwich/abecca, Vael collision, browsing without buying; first return with moss and three mineral vaen. | Shirt in backpack. No active posting, dungeon descent or Nyx. One versus two abecca stops still OPEN. |
| 13 | Yellow District alchemists make poor offers (20 eln illustrative); friendly apothecary buys moss and refers her to a Green District Binding specialist. Sells two larger stones, keeps the smallest; returns home. | Actual sale prices and structural identities OPEN. No tutor inquiry today. |
| 14 | Morning abecca with keepsake; knowledgeable regular city shop assistant helps complete her first custom 60-card raeon deck; one or two casual games; buys The Geometry of Existence on way home. | Opponents, results, cardlist and price OPEN. |
| 15 | Green District city Registry: asks about an accredited independent aen instructor; asked to return in two days. Shops for pots/plants with next-day delivery, reads and dines at a local restaurant. | No named tutor, fee or first lesson. |
| 16 | Succulents/planters and first shipment of 11 damaged chainmail items arrive at home. Inspects them, determines ring requirements; ordinary life and robe work continue. | Receipt and inspection, not finished repairs or payment. |
| 17 | Orders repair-compatible rings and obtains tools from the Yellow Row mailwright; returns to the city Registry for instructor contact/scheduling. | Separate errands. Material may vary, including steel; no assumed specialty-shirt stock. |
| 18 | Preparation, other work and leisure. | Flexible; no compulsory scene. |
| 19 | Repair rings arrive or are collected; starts customer repairs that evening. | Compatible rings from supplier. |
| 19–23 inclusive | Five-day repair period: completes all eleven items, minor through extensive; fruit, abecca, bought meals, limited cooking, plants, reading and occasional raeon. | No eleven compulsory scenes, fixed hours, damage distribution or margin. |
| 24 | Green District Registry directs her to the city office of the same independent freight provider; books outbound pickup. | Registry is not the carrier. |
| 25 | Provider collects eleven repaired items at her apartment for the staging smithies. | Fees and precise transit hours OPEN. |
| 26 · visit 2 | Shirt in backpack, local café abecca, both original smithies approve delivered repairs and offer more work; third smithy joins on trial. Casual raeon games. At staging Registry: finished-shirt appraisal AND supervised practical; White Chainmail Armorsmith. At dinner she shows her White Craftsmanship badge in her favorite Orange District restaurant. | Eligible for an account, not an active professional listing; no Nyx, White Transduction rank or completed robe. |
| 27 | Shows her badge to her friend/master mailwright on Yellow Blacksmith Row; they celebrate. Returns to work on the unfinished robe for the rest of the day. | Short personal scene and work. |
| 28 | Second shipment of damaged chainmail arrives. | No selected item count, pricing or payment. |
| 28–35 inclusive | Eight-day second repair cycle; fruit, regular abecca (sometimes twice daily), purchased lunch/dinner, reading, plant tending, occasional raeon and Genesis Card window-shopping. Frugal about coveted Cards; robe work when time permits. | Narrative passage, not eight mandatory scenes. |
| 36 | Completes/checks, organizes and prepares repairs for dispatch. | Background or brief passage. |
| 37 | Same provider picks up repairs at the apartment; Kaira looks forward to available robe-work time. | Approximately two days before visit 3. |
| 38 | Works on robe; ordinary life while freight is in transit. | Background, may be omitted in prose. |
| 39 · visit 3 | Backpack without demonstration shirt. Confirms delivery before checking in with the three satisfied smithies; may browse one-off inquiries without taking excess work. Forages, follows extensive multicolored flowers to shallow rocky den, finds dead mother and mewing, black seven-week male cub without adult markings. Takes Nyx into her care. | Not a descent. Father hunted, fate unconfirmed; assailant and exact Shaping OPEN. Kaira sees aftermath only. |

Visits 1–3 are staging visits, **none a dungeon descent**. First physical Chainmail Robe completion/public reveal still closes Orange on an unassigned day.
