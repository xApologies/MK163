# Kaira's first Blue Dungeon research expedition — author-selected outcome

## Contract and purpose
Kaira is a newly certified Green Support Specialist and an independent aithren, not yet a permanent vaelum member. The research group hires her through the Crown Accord Registry for environmental protection while investigating naturally occurring ru’ne/sigil structures in the **Yellow and Green internal strata of the Blue Dungeon**. The group composition, names, pay, scientific findings, and detailed travel route remain OPEN.

## Established environmental basis
MK163 `WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/ENVIRONMENT/DUNGEONS.md` and the inherited AERA `L2/ENVIRONMENT/03_DUNGEONS.md` establish persistent prismatic chromatic dungeon strata. Environmental Genesis-field pressure can threaten biological coherence independently of ELDRIS combat, even in secured areas, particularly in deeper strata. Naturally occurring ru’ne/sigils are Genesis-field/geometric relationships, not necessarily visible carved writing; consult `PROVENANCE/WORKSHOP/LIVE_MODEL_009B/WORKSHOP/02_NATURAL_RUNE_SIGILS_AND_ENGINEERING.md`.

## Preparation and operational roles
- **Kaira**: practiced Fireball at Blackstone with illi; trains extensively with her newly completed individual Genesis Shield during Days 909–913, applying it to Nyx and the Black Sprite. In the dungeon she excels at long-duration environmental protection, and can contribute some offense. Her certification remains Green; do not infer higher mastery or universal multi-target shield competence.
- **Black Sprite**: learns its **own** form of protective Shaping from observing Kaira's practice; it is not a copy of her encoded Card or an independent ordinary Card and does not reserve her Card's basins. Its sustained shielding reduces the workload on other support specialists. It also has limited offensive and Resolution-related restorative contributions. No unbounded power or exact numerical shield throughput is established.
- **Nyx**: helps significantly against ELDRIS and remains an independent intelligent family companion, not a separately certified Registry practitioner.
- **Other support personnel**: their existence and workload are recognized; Kaira and Sprite relieve pressure, not replace the entire team's safety apparatus.

## Social and professional outcome
Party members notice Kaira's Chainmail Robe and discover she is its original creator / progenitor. She is well received as both an inventor and an effective newly certified Support Specialist. The research party completes the assignment successfully. On Day 931 the leader validates participation with the Registry, Kaira receives her payment (sum OPEN), and her Accord Registry dossier records favorable feedback / strong performance. She enjoys delving and wishes to pursue three to four more assignments across harvesting and ELDRIS-focused parties.
