## Learning assessment and proof discipline

Each Genesis session should distinguish (1) source definition/theorem, (2) professor-created example, (3) student exercise, (4) narrative comprehension or misconception.
Source assumptions remain assumptions. In particular A1 universal energy, White symmetric readout, and SU(n) readiness must not be promoted to proved physical laws.
Conventional subjects are retained as v3 scheduled proposals; they do not derive their science from Genesis and require separate textbook selection if full textbook fidelity is requested.
