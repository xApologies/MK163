# Blackstone — Complete Five-Semester, All-Course Syllabus (v5)

**Authority and status:** proposed professor-level instructional syllabus; MK163 owns class scheduling, `_bricked` owns Genesis mathematics, AERA owns operational transduction. This does not assert completed attendance or comprehension.
**Coverage:** 5 semesters × 16 weeks; all required classes, including Kaira Semester II Ailor. One lesson per scheduled required class per week. Each class is 2 hours. The day ledger additionally lists study/rest days.
**Math reference:** The original v4 mathematics and source anchors are retained in full in each calendar. Standard subjects use original conventional teaching proposals, not invented citations to Genesis.
