# MK163 — Book Violet (Book Six): cumulative author-selected macro

**Authority:** author decisions through 2026-11-21; seven arcs Red → Orange → Yellow → Green → Blue → Violet → White. **Scope:** macro plan only; exact scenes, dates, names, military tactics and governments remain OPEN.

## Book frame
Book Blue ends with defeat of the coordinated Pirate King/Lords organization and interrogation of Cabal of **Maegi** members at its headquarters, exposing centuries of manipulation toward world domination. Books Violet and White form a **two-book international war against the Cabal**. Violet escalates and substantially disrupts the Cabal across continents; White continues its defeat and culminates in Kaira's local planetary Genesis stabilization. The Cabal holds knowledge beyond current mainstream magic, including information relevant to ELDRIS, Genesis storms and collapses. Kaira's eventual stabilization mechanism is **not yet selected**.

## Red — five northern Cabal cities, then return
Kaira returns to the **Violet Domain capital** following Book Blue's pirate campaign and receives **one or two military decorations** for her service (names/ceremony OPEN). Interrogation intelligence from Book Blue reveals a Cabal foothold at the **first of five occupied cities** on the northern continent, among **eleven aqua-marked cities** on the author's map. The **five white-X cities** are Cabal-occupied; the other six are not known to be occupied. Kaira is dispatched with a Crown delegation to the merchant cities. Investigations and interrogation at each Cabal location yield links to the next; by the **third occupied city**, the last **two** have been identified. The delegation systematically disrupts/dismantles all five footholds and Kaira returns victorious to the Violet Domain capital. The western home kingdom and eastern **Republic of States** are not known to have Cabal infiltration; do not equate that with a universal proof of absence. The far-right **pink-X** coalition operation is separate from Kaira's route (see below).

## Orange — Blue Realm and sixth Black bond
Kaira visits the **Blue Realm**, bonds with the **Blue Black Leviathan**, initially egg-like; the egg hatches. Leviathans feed on **Blue Genesis energy** and can grow to immense sizes; Kaira uses her trained **aen** access to assist the juvenile's energy-supported growth. By departure from the Blue Realm the young Leviathan is approximately **30–40 feet long**, enormous to humans but still a juvenile to Leviathans. Exact energy transfer, development rate and realm time mechanics OPEN; do not invent a quantitative growth law or claim aen mastery beyond established training.

## Yellow — southwestern kingdom and outward missions
Princess **illi** and Kaira travel from **Violet capital to Green capital via the King's Highway**, gather a larger royal/kingdom delegation and travel by **warship/armada** to the southwestern continent (bottom left on map). A white-circled **purple-X capital** serves as a base for multiple outward investigations against Cabal footholds, with returns to the capital between missions (yellow arrows). The polity's precise governmental form is OPEN; 'kingdom' is a working label. The Cabal's influence there is destabilized.

## Green — southern follow-on foothold
Kaira, illi and their growing delegation follow the **green route** to the next marked Cabal location in the lower part of the southwestern continent. They engage local authorities, investigate and destabilize that foothold. Names, exact operations and political structures OPEN.

## Blue — Leviathan flight to southern continent
The young Black Leviathan has grown further and **can fly**, carrying Kaira, Princess illi, Kaira's companions and a small number of other selected personnel across the water along the **blue route** to the southern continent. User discussed a later scale of roughly **60–100 feet** as illustrative, **not a fixed measurement**. The party uses the southern kingdom's capital as a base, investigates Cabal influence and disrupts local footholds. Do not assert the entire growing delegation travels on the Leviathan or that the creature has reached adult kilometer scale.

## Violet — southeastern marked location
The party follows the **purple route** to the bottom-right Cabal-marked location and carries out another campaign of investigation, cooperation and destabilization. Exact city, tactics and governmental form OPEN.

## White — final marked route, information and homecoming
The **pink route on the author's map denotes the White Arc**, not a new pink-ranked arc. Kaira's party continues operations against the Cabal, recovers further intelligence and returns to **her home kingdom's Violet Domain capital** to end Book Violet. This is a substantial international victory and handoff to Book White, **not the Cabal's final worldwide eradication**. Do not invent a final planetary Genesis stabilization event in Violet; that belongs to Book White.

## Parallel allied operation — far-right pink X
A distinct **pink X on the far-right continent** denotes a Cabal foothold targeted and **successfully dismantled by collaborating foreign kingdoms independently of Kaira's traveling group**. Its timing/arc, participating kingdoms and tactical details remain OPEN. It is **not a stop on Kaira's itinerary**, and its pink X must not be confused with the pink route used for White Arc.

## Cross-book constraints
- Kaira is high-ranking Crown Intelligence / Special Projects, with exceptional authority under Princess illi; illi is a diplomat and close friend, not a Special Projects officer.
- The sixth Black bond is the **Blue Black Leviathan** in this book. Bonded Black companions have Kaira-specific cross-chromatic realm travel eligibility; **Nyx is not Black-bonded** and cannot use that exception. Preserve companions' independent agency.
- Leviathans may assume human form; some visit the White Realm, but most stay in Blue Realm in natural form. Some exceptionally large individuals may reach **~8 km**; this is not the size of Kaira's juvenile.
- No new King's Tournament is assigned in Book Violet. The second King's Tournament was selected for Book **Blue Red Arc** and triennial recurrence is addressed in separate Blue Revision 004.
- Retain **Cabal of Maegi**, Kaira, illi, Nyx, Vaelith, ELDRIS, Domai, vaelum, **aen** spelling. Do not assign new personal names, kingdoms, exact dates, chapter counts or unapproved mechanics.
