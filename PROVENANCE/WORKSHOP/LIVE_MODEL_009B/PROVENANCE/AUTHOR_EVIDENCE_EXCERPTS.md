> “the cards themselves become an anchor for that system ... They become part of the construct. They become part of the system.”

> “it's like a maintained system that anybody can interact with depending on what it's doing. Or like I said, it could just be a barrier. That barrier is just passive.”

> “So we can say that this is Kira's contribution as a cardsmith to that domain of transduction.”
