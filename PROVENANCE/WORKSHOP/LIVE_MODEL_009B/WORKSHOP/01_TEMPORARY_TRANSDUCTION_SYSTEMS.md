# 01 — Temporary Transduction Systems: Kaira's Cardsmith contribution

**Status:** AUTHOR-SELECTED conceptual direction; coupling mathematics and first prototypes remain OPEN. These are **ordinary** Genesis Cards, not the retired special Black Genesis Cards.

## Discovery and distinction
Kaira develops a modular way to use activated Genesis Cards as functional parts of a single **Temporary Transduction System**. Instead of treating one Card as synonymous with one prepared Shaping (e.g., Fireball), she can engineer Card components whose compatible architecture forms a persistent shared manifold. Cards become **structural anchors of the construct**; the system is assembled from them and owns them in the operational/structural sense. The transductionist owns and maintains the active **basin connections**, supplying and directing energy and sustaining the system. This is not a transfer of legal Card ownership.

**Frame-of-reference correction:** the composite manifold is **external to the practitioner**, referenced to the spatial configuration of the Cards, not centered on Kaira's body. This is the primary geometric change. Ordinary personal manifolds are internally anchored to a practitioner (though they may extend outside the body); Card systems instead use an external Card-relative frame. Underlying manifold geometry remains ordinarily invisible. Activated Cards and realized effects may be visible. A system's location is independent of the practitioner's physical center; its operation remains dependent on their energy connections.

## Modularity and persistence
A system may be small, potentially even one component, or may use multiple Cards; six is not a mandatory progression. The author wants open-ended discoveries of different component arrangements and systems throughout the books. Components may be engineered for structural functions without producing independent useful effects. The combined system may manifest a barrier, turret, or other interactive/passive construct. A larger number of Cards is not necessarily better. The resulting manifold persists **temporarily while the sustaining transductionist can maintain it**, limited by fatigue, throughput, competency and active Card integrity. No arbitrary time limit or new mana resource.

The Card technology offloads **prepared geometric complexity**: Kaira may understand an advanced Shaping without yet being able to form/hold its manifold natively. She can pre-engineer structure into Card components and activate the system, still retaining responsibility for energy transfer, timing, direction, conscious realization and fatigue. This is a bridge during her native-mastery development, not her ultimate magical identity.

## Illustrative applications — NOT approved designs
- **Defensive dome:** Cards establish a shared external manifold; the realized barrier is centered on the Card configuration rather than Kaira. It can passively protect a group. No required Card count, radius, energy cost, or duration selected.
- **Turret:** Cards sustain a turret-like construct. Kaira maintains the connections, while another person may operate exposed aim/fire controls without themselves performing Transduction or gaining basin access. No autonomy, automatic targeting or free energy implied.
- **Fabrication workstation:** a craftsperson could use a temporary Genesis-supported tool while the transductionist sustains it; the function is an example, not a first prototype.
- **Single-card system:** a one-part system may be possible, but its meaningful distinction from an ordinary prepared Card is unresolved.

## Relation to Architects
Architects already can manipulate the local Genesis Field to establish externally referenced constructs, possibly permanent or infrastructure-supported city-wall turrets and barriers. Kaira does **not** invent turrets, barriers, external manifolds or a fourth interface. Her innovation is **portable modular Card-based deployment of architecture normally associated with Architect field engineering**. An Architect's system need not always be a physical carved sigil; AERA distinguishes temporary `velis` topologies from physical ru’ne/sigils. Card systems are temporary and sustained by one transductionist's fatigue-limited connections. Others may interact with realized functions without taking ownership of sustainment.

## Crucial occupancy correction
An earlier proposed simultaneous native/bond-derived intervention through basins already occupied by active Cards was rejected. Ordinary Cards lock all their required basins across personal interfaces. Six disjoint single-basin Cards R/O/Y/G/B/V occupy all six; no independent native or bond access to those basins while the Cards are active. A system must operate through its Card connections. Black bonds may influence Kaira's knowledge/design, not bypass occupancy. Third-party use of a turret does not grant the user independent Transduction access.

## Open engineering questions
How Card manifolds couple; when the shared manifold is formed; card placement, orientation, movement, range, compatibility, damage and failure; card interchangeability; whether a sustaining operator can hand off; how user commands reach the construct; whether multiple functions coexist; one-card system distinctiveness; exact fatigue/throughput; what first prototype Kaira develops. Do not impose formulas, guaranteed system behaviors or an invention chronology.
