# 02 — Natural ru’ne, sigils and scientific discovery

**Status:** AUTHOR-SELECTED worldbuilding principle; detailed field physics/grammar OPEN.

## Natural mathematical language
Protected **ru’ne** are elementary discovered mathematical/geometric expressions, analogized to letters; coherent compositions form **sigils**, analogized to words. They describe/express naturally occurring relationships of the Genesis Field and starscape. Humans did **not** invent these relationships: they observed phenomena, inferred mathematics, and learned to reproduce smaller expressions. The author's analogy is humanity's mathematical description of natural physics (e.g., quantum-field models); do not treat a model as necessarily identical to the underlying ontology, and do not turn real-world analogies into AERA axioms.

## Natural occurrence
Ru’ne/sigils may arise naturally throughout the world, not just in dungeons or ruins. Some are transient, appearing during changing environmental/field conditions; others persist for centuries or millennia. Natural structures can be vast and interrelated, more complex than humanity can currently reproduce. A natural sigil need not look like a human-inscribed symbol or a visible carved mark: the expression may be in field topology, geology, mineral/crystal structure, environment, or sometimes visible geometric patterns. Some phenomena may comprise an entire large-scale sigil system. The environmental Genesis Field can form the relationships without a designer.

## Human discovery and reduction
Civilization observes natural phenomena, identifies ru’ne/sigil structure, mathematically characterizes it, isolates manageable relationships and reproduces a **reduced-complexity local implementation**. Human sigils can be carved/embedded/grown into stone or other objects, or implemented via appropriate phase-field projection as supported by existing AERA/MK147 rules. Example: two stone blocks carry compatible spatial-locking sigils, permitting large stable architectural structures. Human engineering copies a *specific useful relation*, not nature's entire massive architecture. Nature's complexity and human engineering complexity must remain distinct.

## Environmental examples (illustrative, not mapped locations)
- **Floating islands**, compared by author to Pandora's floating mountains in Avatar: the entire island may form a natural sigil system giving levitation-like behavior without an ancient enchanter. A second natural structure might collect, transport, or generate water, producing waterfalls from the floating island to the land below. The exact source of water, conservation accounting, geometry and levitation mechanism are OPEN; don't assert unlimited matter creation.
- **Dungeon chromatic boundaries**: prismatic transitions among R/O/Y/G/B/V strata may involve immense naturally formed ru’ne/sigil structures helping stabilize the stratification. Some could be studied in Blue zone; not every boundary must display a visible glyph or a mandatory sigil.
- **Ephemeral field formations** may occur in storms or changing environments; not every domai necessarily contains a sigil.

## Science and narrative
Natural sigils are a source of scientific expeditions, research professions, institutions, discovery, competing hypotheses and new engineering. Observing a sigil does not mean understanding it; knowing the math does not guarantee the ability to reproduce the phenomenon. Reader learns through environments and Kaira's encounters, not exposition dumps. Researchers can be noncombatants who hire aithren escorts to access dangerous natural structures. An island or cave can remain the same while Kaira's capacity to recognize its mathematics develops over books.

## AERA source boundary
AERA `L2/SIGILS_ARCHITECTS_VELIS.md` defines **physical ru’ne** as strongly orthogonal inscribed components, sigils as physically realized structures, and `velis` as temporary local topology. This new MK163 author direction expands the *natural phenomenon/discovery* perspective; Codex must reconcile scope rather than overwrite AERA's specific human physical inscription taxonomy. Don't conflate field topology, human sigil inscriptions, or Architect velis.
