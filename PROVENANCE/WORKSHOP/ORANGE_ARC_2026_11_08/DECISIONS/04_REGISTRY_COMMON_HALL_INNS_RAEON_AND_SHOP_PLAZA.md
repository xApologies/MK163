## Accord Registry: Crown institution and social space

The **Accord Registry** remains a Crown-owned public institution, not a vaelum, guild, mercenary corps or military command. It registers/recognizes individual aithren, independent vaelum and specializations; coordinates expedition contracts and professional recruitment; keeps records of work, ratings, resources and fulfillment. **At the Violet Dungeon staging settlement it is also a community gathering place**. Aithren may sit and meet, exchange stories, socialize, plan jobs, discuss research, inspect postings, register availability or speak to staff. Public-facing social activity does not change institutional ownership. A professional open hall is preferable to a dingy fantasy guild tavern or bureaucratic maze.

The author selects **commercial craft-service postings as an additional staging Registry use**: Kaira can lodge her independent armor-repair availability with the Registry, even though she isn't an independently registered dungeon Support Specialist in Orange; the Blue-arc first Support assessment still happens years later. Registry staff can tell her where to find specialist services, including referring her to independent freight companies; **Registry does not automatically become freight operator, government advertising agency, a universal employer or a private artisan guild**. The exact fee, moderation, eligibility, posting format and records-processing requirements for commercial craft notices remain OPEN.

## Inns, taverns, hotels and residences

The selected ordinary term for lodging is **inn**, not obligatorily "tavern" or "hotel". A tavern denotes a social/drink/meal area and may operate inside an inn; it does not itself guarantee lodging. A full-service inn can contain a receptionist, dining room, clean private rooms, informal social areas, a Genesis fireplace, maege light ambiance and perhaps a small decorative water feature. The working size is **approximately 11 to 20-something rooms**, varying by site. Inns may be one to four stories; top-floor larger suites for longer stays are optional. An inn is designed for expeditions arriving for a few nights, resting after a descent, or gathering before another mission. Permanent residences in NW/NE can be rented for six months, a year, two years or continuously. Premium residence sizes may reach thousands of square feet.

## City/settlement culture and environmental identity

People meet at inns, coffee shops and the Registry; socialize and play raeon; trade dungeon products; buy clothes, weapons and repairs; plan research; return from dangerous dungeon work. All streets and the public plaza use laid stone and are maintained. Rich gardens, water and light are pervasive aesthetic tools, but *not every workbench or smithy needs a pond*. Pricing is broadly 1.3–1.7 × comparable large-city rates because of dungeon access; elegance is present across price bands while amenities and size increase with expense.
