## Why the selected imagery matters in the narrative

The Accord Registry is beautiful and civic, yet practical and socially inhabited; it is the setting where Kaira awkwardly posts availability and observes aithren. An inn has a living community, not dingy medieval grime. The smithy area's individual open bays demonstrate a sophisticated matter-conducting professional economy and offer her first subcontracting breakthrough. The frog image supplies the visual indicator leading Kaira to examine a moss/mineral site, not an encyclopedic monster or fantastical companion. The map stays under author-owned cartography; renderings may not create authoritative street coordinates.
