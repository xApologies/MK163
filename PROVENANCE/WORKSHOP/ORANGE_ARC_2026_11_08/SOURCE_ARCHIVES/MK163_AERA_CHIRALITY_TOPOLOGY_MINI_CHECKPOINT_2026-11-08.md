## 1. Author-selected conceptual distinction

> **Chirality is organization. Topology is structure.**

This is the operative explanatory distinction for MK163's native Genesis specializations moving forward.

- **Topology — structure.** Refers to the structural and relational form of an admissible realization: how constituent parts connect, whether boundaries or closed manifolds are present, continuity and the relationships that sustain a distinct topology. It asks **what structure is realized**. This is the native primary emphasis of the **Orange Realm's Vaelith**.
- **Chirality — organization.** Refers to the grammar, arrangement, orientation, occupancy relationships and admissibility of Genesis states *within/through realized structure*; it asks **how states are organized**. This is the native primary emphasis of the **Red Realm's Dragons**. The established chirality fabric/energy occupancy descriptions remain primary where technical precision is required.
- **Distinction:** Similar observable effects can arise by different native mechanisms. A well-defined topology is not identical to the occupancy/organizational state it carries. Neither domain implies exclusive ownership of an effect by one race.
- **Scope safeguard:** These are author-selected **conceptual descriptions inside the fictional AERA/MK163 Genesis framework**. They are not new formal equations/operators or a claim that mathematical chirality in ordinary topology has been redefined; its conventional mathematical use concerns handedness/mirror asymmetry. Do not replace, truncate, or silently reinterpret source-level math.
- **Formal-open safeguard:** Current MK163 repository retains a five-node chain: `Genesis Field → Resolution → Bandwidth → Persistence → Chirality`. **Topology's exact formal placement is OPEN**; do not insert a sixth chain node or claim a fully typed operator because of this checkpoint.

## 3. White — coherent superposition and human generalism

- **White is the seventh, material realm, realized as the coherent superposition of Red through Violet.** It is not a seventh independent chromatic basin; Black is not an eighth realm.
- **Humanity** inhabits White. Its provisional dwarf-like, human-like and elf-like physiological expressions are populations of the underlying human race, not three additional chromatic realms.
- Humans are **adaptable generalists** with potential access to all six chromatic basins/specializations through their existing six-strand neurogenesis and the three principal Transduction interfaces. Generalism means **versatile admissible access**, not effortless mastery of every native race's specialty, equal biological output, or identical channel development among all humans.
- Current AERA physiology models individual and population variation in neurogenesis, knowledge, training, Resolution, Bandwidth, fatigue and physical limits; preserve those constraints. Access to an effect does not mean reproduction of a denizen's exact native operator.
- Human ability to work across the six realms explains the story's prominent engineered Transduction culture, but **these three interfaces are not exclusive to humans** by logical definition and should not be promoted as such without new canon.

## 4. Three principal interfaces for Transduction — KEEP SEPARATE from six native specializations

All three interface methods implement the **same six-stage Transduction lifecycle**: `Conceive → Shape → Hold → Resolve → Manifest → Release`.

1. **Native Genesis / Neurogenesis (native Genesis expression):** The practitioner consciously conceives, constructs and sustains admissible manifolds. Requires strong conceptual understanding and direct control, subject to Resolution, Bandwidth, energy, attention and fatigue; unlike a fixed installed repertoire, it is extensible with knowledge and competence.
2. **Binding Circles and installed Bindings:** An identity-resolving physical circle installs reusable topology; deployed PSSP supplies scaffolded **Shape and Hold**, reducing the need to construct the full manifold afresh. Installed Bindings permanently occupy a **finite share of personal Genesis bandwidth**. Exact costing remains OPEN.
3. **Genesis Cards:** Prepared, encoded manifolds in maege-glass Cards coupled by the practitioner. Active Cards occupy the chromatic basins they require **exclusively across that practitioner's interfaces**; ordinary Cards cannot simultaneously overlap in any required basin. Card use still requires direction, skill and incurs fatigue; six concurrent ordinary Cards is possible only when each uses a distinct single basin. The cultural **Raeon** card game is a separate topic, not automatically identical to operational Card mechanics.

**Do not confuse:** six realms, six Dragon capabilities, six lifecycle stages, three interfaces, or the White material superposition.
