# MK163 — Urban Transportation and Animal Livery Infrastructure
Authority: Author-approved Green Arc workshop, 2026-11-12.

## Public passenger rickshaws
- Human-pulled rickshaws are the ordinary commercial passenger transport of the capital.
- A puller may use Genesis augmentation for strength and endurance; augmentation is common and valuable in this occupation, but NOT mandatory. Non-augmenters can operate rickshaws.
- Preferred passenger configurations: two or four passengers. Five-passenger variants are physically conceivable but not established as a standard.
- Pulled, not pedal-driven: a deliberate preindustrial/romantic 1700s–1800s aesthetic choice, with reduced mechanical manufacturing complexity.
- Carriages may use metal construction. Exact wheel counts, materials, dimensions, suspension, fares, operating rules and routes remain OPEN.
- Day 212: Kaira and the Alchemist use a rickshaw between Blackstone Keep and the Alchemist's Blue District residence.

## Privately owned beasts and mounts
- Noble households and wealthy merchants may own selectively bred Genesis-expressed draft beasts and personal mounts.
- Ownership is costly, including acquisition, food, secure accommodation, handling, grooming and care. Noble breeding lines carry prestige.

## Livery yards and city planning
- City design reserves a limited number of designated stable/livery zones, often a few acres, leased to professional operators.
- Commercial livery operators board animals owned by different people; they feed, groom, maintain, exercise and securely house animals during stays.
- Noble visitors can board a carriage beast or mount for a few nights while visiting the city from an outlying estate.
- Wealthy merchants can board personal mounts similarly.
- Agricultural animals generally remain housed at rural farms outside the city and return after deliveries or work.
- Caravan beasts primarily use caravan facilities and may need temporary city accommodations.
- Exact land allocations, stable economics, care standards, species-specific acreage and zoning locations remain OPEN.
