# Author decisions — Book Blue Revision 004

## Tournament
- The King's Tournament is now held **once every three years**, superseding the previous four-year recurrence.
- Kaira **participates in a second King's Tournament in Book Blue Red Arc**.
- Order in Book Blue Red: Kaira receives the kingdom's highest service decoration for Book Green cartel work; she visits the Violet Realm and learns of the Black Primordial in the Yellow Realm; she returns to the White Realm; the King's Tournament occurs and **concludes Red Arc**.
- Existing favorable intelligence report can remain during Red, but must not replace the tournament as arc conclusion.
- Orange Arc begins the Special Projects deployment against **three merging Domai** (NOT merging political domains).
- Kaira's second tournament team/vaelum, placement, exact calendar dates, race details, and prizes are **OPEN**. The first Book Red tournament result (fourth/Green; seven White-ranked Dimensional Rings) is unchanged.
- Existing tournament institution's race format, rules, and three political domains remain unchanged unless a direct scheduling consequence requires author review.

## Interrealm exception
- Normally White Realm humans may enter all chromatic realms; nonnative chromatic beings cannot enter other chromatic realms.
- **Kaira's six Black-bond companions**, through their bond with Kaira as the **Black Singularity**, gain an exception enabling them to accompany her into other chromatic realms, effectively sharing the White Realm native's cross-realm eligibility.
- Eligible when bonded: Black Sprite, Black Vaelith, Black Dragon, Black Unicorn, Black Primordial, Black Leviathan.
- **Nyx is not one of the six Black bonds**, so he does not receive this exception; he stays in the White Realm during Kaira's cross-chromatic realm visits. Where he stays/what he does is OPEN.
- This exception is specific to the bonds, not all members of their races. Independent agency and consent are unchanged. Do not invent a transfer of citizenship, universal permission, or a precise magical mechanism.
- The existing Book Blue Red Arc 'only Black Unicorn accompanies Kaira' and Yellow Arc 'all non-Yellow-native companions stay behind' restrictions are superseded for **bonded Black companions**. The precise travel party remains OPEN; eligibility is not compulsory attendance.

## Leviathan maritime support (carry forward earlier author-approved delta)
- About **five or six** Leviathans with an affinity for the White Realm assist the multinational coalition in **Book Blue Green Arc** open-ocean operations.
- They protect maritime travel and participate in attacks on mobile pirate fortresses, helping coalition forces dismantle them; **not** single-handed fortress destruction.
- Most Leviathans stay in the Blue Realm in natural form; some visit the White Realm and can take human form; exceptionally large individuals can reach approximately **eight kilometers**. Do not assign this size to the participants.
- Kaira remains Crown Intelligence / Special Projects, not a naval officer; her primary land-based resource-island role in **Blue Arc** is unchanged.
- Do **not** move the sixth Black Leviathan bond from Book Violet into Book Blue.
