# Approved and exploratory visual design references

Two user-approved visual concepts underpin the contrast:
1. Brotherhood of Maegi robe: flowing hooded textile, consistent geometric decoration, dominant chromatic rank color, gray accents for Red–Violet and black for White. No white or gold trim on colored ranks, even if an illustrative rendering depicts such accents. Rank is real Accord certification; robe signals Brotherhood membership.
2. Kaira's five-layer Chainmail Robe: dark steel/black chainmail only; no textile, plate armor or separate epaulettes; sculptural pointed shoulder ridges integrated into interlinked weave, three-dimensional recessed 5→4→3 layer patterns, opacity always retained. Mass reduction allows fitted comfortable garment. User enthusiastically approved renderings of this sculptural direction. Detailed topology/engineering is still open.

The package includes two local generated concept images in `VISUAL_REFERENCES/` as illustrative art, not authoritative engineering diagrams. User-uploaded Maegi artwork was discussed and approved in conversation; its exact source is not duplicated here unless specifically matched. When image text and protected canon differ, textual canon governs. Do not interpret illustrative rendering as proving ring count or physical manufacturability.
