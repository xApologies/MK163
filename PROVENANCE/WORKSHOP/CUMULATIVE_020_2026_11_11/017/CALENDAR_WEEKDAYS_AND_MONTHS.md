## Calendar physics and open discrepancy
31 hours/day; 6 days/week; 60 weeks/year; 360 days/year; four 90-day seasons; 11 accounting months. Existing MK163 calendar records a historical 11-month duration list totaling **351**, nine short of 360. No invented intercalary days, length reassignment, 12th month, or fixed weekdays. Naming does not solve duration discrepancy. Month lengths and exact relative-day-to-calendar mapping must be audited separately.
