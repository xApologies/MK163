# Chainmail Robe — manufacturing, economics, retail and training

## Product distinction
Five-layer original: 850 eln rings + 150 mass-reduction enchantment + 300 labor allocation + 250 margin = **1550 eln**. Dark steel/black metal, five-layer opaque Offset-Lattice, raised pointed chainmail-only shoulder ridges, 5→4→3 local relief with no transparent holes. No chromatic Maegi rank color. Independent market robes: 1 layer 560, 2 770, 3 980, 4 1190, 5 1400 eln; standardized 150 enchantment included in each. Lower layer-count robes can be plain and less opaque; even a five-layer imitation is not automatically Kaira's weave. Independent smiths may design their own repeatable patterns and employ journeymen. No exclusive legal rights or prohibition on imitation established.

## Turnaround and first production
Kaira's **ten-day average** means ten elapsed calendar days under her usual flexible work rhythm; occasionally eight days with extra effort. It is NOT ten continuous full-day shifts. Day 65 + Day 74 + Days 76–83 = ten working-date allocations for first commissioned robe, finished end 83, delivered 84. Subsequent timing approximate. Day 114 batch of robes #3/#4/#5 is explicit author-selected milestone; do not derive impossible individual schedules from earlier tentative completion estimates.

## Supply and competition
Day 107 city Blacksmith's Row smiths have started copying the general chainmail-robe garment in one-to-three-layer forms. Day 116 staging-area smiths see originals at/through Registry and may independently develop variants. A simple established weave can be repeated by journeymen; innovation lies in weave design and geometry. Kaira originals remain premium and attract commissions even from smiths who could make simpler robes themselves.
