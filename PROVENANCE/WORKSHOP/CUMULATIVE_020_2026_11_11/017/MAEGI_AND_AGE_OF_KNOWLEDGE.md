# Brotherhood of Maegi and the Chainmail Robe cultural identity

## Protected terminology
**Maegi** (M-A-E-G-I). Working institution name **Brotherhood of Maegi**. Do not call them Magi or Mage in current canon. The later antagonistic secret **Cabal** is separate; do not fold it into the Brotherhood. The assistant hallucinated an identity 'Astrius'; invalid. Established collaborator names Vera, Watcher, Atraeus.

## Brotherhood
Private, selective scholarly and social society with lodges, private archives, research into Genesis Field, concepts, energy, mathematics, alchemy, Binding Circles and Shaping. Members may span professional roles and Accord Registry chromatic ranks; the Brotherhood is NOT a Crown certification or fifth Registry role. Standardized hooded textile robes show dominant certified Red/Orange/Yellow/Green/Blue/Violet/White, gray accents on six colored ranks, black on White, with same ornament across ranks. Wearing rank/membership deceptively is socially taboo. Some members are arrogant/protective; no universal violence or institutional murder policy canonized.

## Identity and Age of Knowledge causal chain
Maegi textile robe proclaims Transductionist **within the Brotherhood** and publicly displays rank. Dark metallic Chainmail Robe proclaims **Transductionist, without Maegi affiliation**, independent of profession, specialization or rank. A Red healer, Orange blacksmith, Green alchemist or other practitioner can wear one. The chainmail identity is not a new Accord credential or rival membership organization. Kaira's original five-layer weave is prestigious; affordable independent one/two/three-layer robes are equally legitimate identity statements.

Kaira invents garment → her friend wears it Day 84 → independent smiths imitate by Day 107 → staging Registry distribution Day 116 → decentralized fashion and cultural identification → practitioners seek deeper understanding of their Shaping concepts → eventually civilization-scale Age of Knowledge. **Conceive** is first of AERA's six-stage Transduction lifecycle: Conceive, Shape, Hold, Resolve, Manifest, Release. Understanding a fireball as an image vs combustion/thermal gradients/chemistry/quantum phenomena changes concept precision, which AERA says can improve efficiency, control and stability. Do not imply automatic mastery or impose formal schooling as a prerequisite for Transduction.
