# Shapings, Bindings, Cards and direct energy access
**Terminology:** A **Shaping** is the realized Transduction operation. A **Binding** is one method of preparing/using its manifold, not a synonym for the Shaping.

**Universal lifecycle:** Conceive → Shape → Hold → Resolve → Manifest → Release.

Three established personal interfaces: native Genesis expression, installed conventional Binding, and ordinary Genesis Card. The prepared geometry does not erase the practitioner's concept, control, fatigue or execution responsibility.

**Ordinary Card occupancy:** An active Card exclusively occupies every basin it requires across personal interfaces; two simultaneously active Cards must use disjoint basin sets. Six independent single-basin Cards are the theoretical six-Card case, subject to operator capacity and endurance. Native same-basin concurrency is not automatically blocked except by an occupied Card. No automatic access to occupied basins via aen.

**Author addition:** Six Foundational single-basin energy-access Cards, one per R/O/Y/G/B/V, at the MK163 Foundational benchmark of 100 eln each. They may supply charging access as a conceptual design decision. **Engineering reconciliation required**: confirm their behavior against existing Card conduit rules, whether they are existing catalog entries or new named Shapings, and how energy is routed to a diamond. Do not silently create new catalog IDs or claim no fatigue from initial basin extraction.
