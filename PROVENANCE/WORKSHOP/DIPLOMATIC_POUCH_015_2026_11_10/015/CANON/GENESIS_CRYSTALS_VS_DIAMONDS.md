# Genesis crystals and Genesis diamonds — AUTHOR-SELECTED NOVEMBER 10
**Scope:** MK163 World Clock, globally applicable, not Yellow Arc-specific.

## Genesis crystal
- A naturally forming, basin-specific **structured Genesis energy resource**. Ordinary practitioners cannot create Genesis crystals by Transduction.
- Supplies *intrinsic energy structure* as well as energy. Its structural role is especially important in Binding Circle installation, alchemy, ru'ne/sigil work, and other fabrication.
- RAM / structural configuration is an analogy, not literal computer hardware.
- Mining/harvesting, natural scarcity and basin identity underpin the existing raw-crystal market.
- Genesis crystals may be consumed by an installation or recipe where specified. Do not assume all uses are nondestructive.

## Diamond
- An ordinary diamond lattice can be charged with organized Genesis energy by a person drawing from one or more accessible basins.
- Diamond energy is **stored, organized, docile energy**; the difficult basin-extraction/organization fatigue was paid **upfront during charging**.
- Discharging stored energy does **not** itself incur *basin-extraction fatigue*. It does not confer Shaping knowledge, geometry, concept, or automatic conscious execution.
- The same diamond can be discharged and recharged, subject to lattice integrity and any as-yet-unspecified engineering limits.
- Diamond reserves may be mounted in rings, staffs, weapons, equipment, or used in sigils/Binding Circles as supplemental energy.
- Diamonds **do not substitute** for naturally formed Genesis crystals when a recipe requires intrinsic basin-specific structure. Extra energy is not a substitute for the correct structural topology.
- An energy supplement may allow greater operation duration/output where supported by the design; no automatic safe overclocking or unlimited throughput is established.

## Analogy
Crystals: structured resource/configuration plus energy. Diamonds: rechargeable energy reserve. This is a functional distinction, not a claim of equivalence to RAM/batteries in physical chemistry.
