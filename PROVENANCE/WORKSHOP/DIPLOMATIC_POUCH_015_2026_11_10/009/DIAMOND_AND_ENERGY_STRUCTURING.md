## Fundamental separations

1. Six basin *structural coordinates*: Red 1/7, Orange 2/7, Yellow 3/7, Green 4/7, Blue 5/7, Violet 6/7. Dimensionless energy-organization coordinates, NOT energy density in joules/volume and NOT mass.
2. White is the superposition of six basins, not a seventh basin; individuals may have different component proportions and different transfer efficiencies.
3. Prime **quantity/equivalence** ladder: 13 Red = 1 Orange; 11 Orange = 1 Yellow; 7 Yellow = 1 Green; 5 Green = 1 Blue; 3 Blue = 1 Violet. Cumulative Red units: 1, 13, 143, 1001, 5005, 15015.
4. **Material mass**: raw Genesis crystalline energy 25mm Red = 100g; other basin 25mm mass = 100[1 + 0.2 log10(F)]g. 7mm crystal mass scales by (7/25)^3 = 0.021952. Diamond mass is the corresponding raw Genesis mass plus carbon lattice mass, using diamond density 3.515g/cm³ (7mm carbon ≈1.205645g; 25mm carbon ≈54.921875g). This is a fictional additive mass convention, not a real-world E/c² derivation.
5. **Market prices** for raw Genesis crystals per gram in eln: Red 0.20, Orange 0.35, Yellow 0.60, Green 1.10, Blue 2.30, Violet 5.00. Prices of finished charged diamonds are not established by crystal prices alone.

## Equal-six-basin White charging

Working author-accepted calibration: **2h42m = 2.7 active hours per normalized Red-charge unit** for a White-superposed operator with equal accessible component mix, under a constant-throughput additive model. At this rate, incremental charging time = (F_target − F_previous) × 2.7 hours; cumulative time = F_target × 2.7 hours.

**Arithmetic audit / outstanding correction:** the earlier assistant claimed 2h42m followed from the historical 8/7/5/3/2/1 hour rates via r = (1/6)Σ(1/t). The actual result is r = 1933/5040 Red charges/hour, T = 5040/1933 ≈ **2.607346 hours = 2h36m26s**. Thus 2h42m is **not** the exact harmonic-throughput consequence. Retain 2h42m as an author-accepted **provisional calibration** (potentially accounting for modest losses/rounding), and do not claim it was mathematically derived from those six rates. No charging efficiency coefficient has yet been author-locked to explain the difference.

**Fatigue:** User discusses approximately one Red charge session before fatigue starts. Treat 2h42m as a provisional **active-session reference**, not proof that every person reaches an absolute physiological cutoff at that exact instant. Rest/recovery duration and daily charging capacity remain unspecified. The hours below are active labor hours, not continuous sessions or calendar durations.
