# MK163 World Clock — Direct-Basin Genesis Diamond Charging

**Checkpoint:** 011 | **Domain:** global world-system / Genesis energy / diamonds | **Authority:** author-approved conceptual pathways; additive-throughput mathematical working model.

## Three distinct charging mechanisms

1. **Native White superposition:** individual internal six-basin composition, not six independent full-power channels. Existing author-accepted working baseline: **2 h 42 min active input** to bring a standard 7 mm empty diamond to Red. This is a provisional calibration, not a fatigue-onset law.
2. **Binding Circle direct access:** opens one or multiple individual energy basins; throughput depends on which basins are open and can be transferred concurrently.
3. **Genesis Card direct access:** activated cards supply the named basin(s); simultaneous cards may contribute separate channels if the operator/sigil can manage them. Card throughput and limits are not independently verified.

**The diamond sorts and organizes input energy.** Direct Violet input does not instantly make the stored diamond Violet. Target thresholds use the prime ladder. **Input basin** and **stored diamond charge state** are different axes.

## Historical single-basin reference (empty → Red, original 1/4-inch chip)

Red 8 h; Orange 7 h; Yellow 5 h; Green 3 h; Blue 2 h; Violet 1 h. The standard chip is now 7 mm; reusing these times at 7 mm is an explicit working calibration, **not** a measured or historically proven conversion.

## Direct-basin additive throughput model

For a standard 7 mm diamond, normalize the required empty→Red charge to 1 unit. Independent basin i has rate r_i=1/t_i normalized Red charges/hour. With simultaneous independent, full-rate channels S:

r(S) = sum_{i in S} 1/t_i;  T_7(S, target) = F_target / r(S).

For a transition between two stored states a→b, T_7(S, a→b)=(F_b−F_a)/r(S).

25 mm extension: T_25 = T_7 × (25/7)^3 ≈ 45.55393586, **conditional on** proportional charge capacity by volume, constant operator throughput, and no geometric transfer bottleneck. This is inherited from Checkpoint 010, not empirically established.

### Limits / unresolved world rules

- Multiple open basins may require separate circles/cards or a multi-channel transfer sigil. Whether a human can sustain all six at full historical throughput is **not yet canonically established**.
- Fatigue constrains active session duration and recovery; **do not equate 2 h 42 min with universal fatigue onset**. The prior native White time is an input duration to reach Red, not an independently derived fatigue limit.
- Direct access can be limited by transfer efficiency, operator skill, card output, sigil bandwidth, heat/instability, and safety. No coefficients are canonized yet.
- Values are ideal active-input hours, not calendar time, and assume throughput remains constant across diamond charge states.
- Existing raw crystal masses, charged diamond masses, 7 mm/25 mm sizes, market prices, energy-structure fractions 1/7…6/7, and prime ladder are **inherited without alteration** from Checkpoint 010 and its embedded earlier checkpoints.
- This is a world-clock/world-system module, **not** a Yellow Arc narrative update.
