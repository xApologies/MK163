# King's Tournament — current White Arc stage design

**Authority:** Explicit author selections from 2026-11-19 conversation. **Supersedes in scope:** older MK163 four-domain and three-Blue-race/eight-Violet-circuit descriptions. Historical documents remain preserved for provenance.

The kingdom now contains **three political domains**: Green, Blue, Violet. Orange remains a chromatic stratum and noble rank/district descriptor, but not a fourth political domain. The tournament is a quadrennial dangerous **dungeon race**, not a PvP arena or dungeon clear. Each team chooses a route through persistent mapped passages, reaches the designated deepest checkpoint to activate its marker, then returns to the surface. Only successful completion qualifies for placement. Teams may deviate from a preplanned route; ELDRIS, oscillations, water and other natural conditions remain part of the race.

## Stage 1 — local qualifying races
All **24** mapped Green-depth-or-deeper dungeons host qualifiers, with kingdom-wide opening on **Day 1376, Aerith 1, Ailar (weekday 1)**. A vaelum enters exactly one local qualifier; all venues begin the same day, preventing repeat attempts at another qualifier. Seven highest completed places per dungeon advance (White to Red, 7 through 1). Domain dungeon counts: Green political domain 9 (4 Green, 3 Blue, 2 Violet); Blue political domain 8 (3 Green, 3 Blue, 2 Violet); Violet political domain 7 (3 Green, 2 Blue, 2 Violet). Maximum 168 advancing entries if each venue produces seven distinct finishers.

## Stage 2 — domain Blue races
Each domain holds **two Blue-dungeon races**, scoring cumulative points across both. Top **seven vaelum per political domain** advance, giving 21 total. Exact venue selection and dates remain OPEN. A team can score zero in a race if it fails to finish or places below seventh. Equal cumulative totals require a tiebreaker **not yet selected**; do not compare raw elapsed times from different dungeon layouts as though they were equivalent.

## Stage 3 — kingdom Violet circuit
All 21 advancing teams compete across five Violet-depth dungeons, in this author-selected order:
1. Blue political domain **V2**
2. Blue political domain **V1**
3. Green political domain **V2** (Green capital dungeon)
4. Green political domain **V1**
5. Violet political domain **V1**

Cumulative circuit points select top seven for royal final. Exact race dates, standings and tiebreaker OPEN.

## Stage 4 — royal final
**Violet political domain V2**, the kingdom's royal-capital dungeon. Seven finalists race; this final alone determines their finishing places, with prior points serving only to qualify. First White (7 points), second Violet (6), third Blue (5), **fourth Green (4; Kaira's vaelum)**, fifth Yellow (3), sixth Orange (2), seventh Red (1). A nonfinisher receives no finishing placement. Kaira receives an exceptional **Dimensional Ring** at the Book Red conclusion; exact properties OPEN.
