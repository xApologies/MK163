# Violet vaelum — traversal logistics and CSR economy

**Status:** author-selected working continuity for Book Red Violet, beginning Day 1081 (Aiven); twelve expedition occurrences on ten distinct itineraries are selected through the Day 1346 outlined-arc closure; individual scenes remain OPEN. This is an additive L2 story-world logistics record; it does not rewrite the existing dungeon, tournament, Registry, or Blue Arc records.

## Seven-person vaelum and companions
- Kaira joined the existing six-person vaelum at the Day 1080 dinner after the Green cave-in rescue. Seven registered aithren: two Guardians, two Offensive Specialists, one Helkir, one combat Support, and Kaira as environmental Support. Nyx and the Black Sprite are companions, not counted among the seven.
- Party leader: one Guardian, **son of an Orange-ranked noble from another Blue Domain city**. Affluent, with access to high-quality expedition equipment. His name, personality and detailed resources OPEN.
- Leader already possesses a **CSR Genesis Card** and a **dimensional bracelet**. The bracelet has a larger practical storage capability than an ordinary ring and holds ropes, anchors and other expedition tools. Exact model/capacity OPEN.
- Another vaelum member may own a **backup dimensional ring**; identity and item list OPEN. Redundancy principle: 'one is none; two is one.'
- One Offensive Specialist is an **air-mobility specialist** with flight capabilities and air-based offensive techniques; exact named Shapings, basin assignments, flight mechanism and load capacity OPEN. Air specialist can cross a gap to establish far-side anchors; repeated/sustained flight causes fatigue.
- Black Sprite can use demanding localized **Resolution/topological control to lift and move Nyx** across gaps Nyx cannot traverse with ropes. This is not general unrestricted flight, teleportation or repeated transport of the whole group; maximum span, mechanism and recovery OPEN. Nyx is large (~4 ft at shoulders, ~1000 lb by Blue close), and can carry riders; the Sprite's help is deliberately reserved for him.

## Dungeon strategy
- Goal: train for King's Tournament **race to marker and return**, emphasizing routes, fatigue conservation, ELDRIS avoidance, coordination and safety. Chasms, shafts, underground rivers and lakes impose real navigation constraints.
- Standard toolkit: dimensional-bracelet-carried ropes, anchors, climbing/safety equipment; scouting, far-side anchoring by air specialist or leader CSR, secured crossing for others; Sprite assistance for Nyx when needed. **Do not make a boat standard carried equipment.** The detailed crossing protocol and exact geography remain OPEN.
- Flight does not make multi-day dungeon descents trivial. CSR fatigue ranges: low 1–2h, moderate 3–5h, high 5–8h at ordinary speed, with speed-dependent fatigue. Exhausting oneself early can make an ELDRIS encounter fatal. The earlier Blue Arc research party took seven days to reach Yellow. The selected first Violet Blue-depth trial takes eight days outbound and seven back. Proposed two-to-three-day CSR-assisted alternatives remain speculative.
- Kaira begins Violet **without** CSR. Her future Cardsmithing project is to manufacture DG-0031 using established native-blackwood desk, reusable gold sigil plates, maege glass Card substrate, Genesis Crystals/Dust, appropriate vaen, ru’ne engraving and stylus-guided projection. Do not invent plate dimensions, consumable quantities, recipe or completed manufacture date. Existing large gold plates should be checked for suitability first.
- CSR four-basin O/Y/B/V Card locks out Kaira's Shield O+B, Beam Y+G and Fireball R+B while active. She may own all Cards but cannot operate these overlapping configurations simultaneously. The noble Guardian's CSR Card obeys the same basin rule.
- The vaelum uses equipment and multiple specialists to cross obstacles before Kaira finishes CSR; later CSR increases route options, not unlimited safety.
