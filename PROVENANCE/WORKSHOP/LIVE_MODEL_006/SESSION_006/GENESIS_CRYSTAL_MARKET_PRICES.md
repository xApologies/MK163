## Decision provenance
Author proposed Red 200, Orange 350, Yellow 600 eln/kg. Assistant proposed Green 1,100, Blue 2,000, Violet 3,600. Author explicitly amended Blue to 2,300 and Violet to 5,000, accepting the resulting scale; Green remains 1,100. White deliberately deferred for separate discussion.

## Constraints
- Mass measured in kg as reference, with grams for small quantities.
- Crystals remain expensive but available in small quantities; do not assume one kilogram per Genesis Card.
- Genesis crystals, Genesis Dust, White dust, vaen, ahm/eln, and charged diamond chips are distinct.
- Manufacturing costs, margins, market fluctuations, regional pricing, and resource specifications remain open.
- Green→Blue ratio 2300/1100 ≈ 2.09; Blue→Violet ratio 5000/2300 ≈ 2.17.

## Session 006 author amendment — White price locked (2026-10-19)
Author selected **31,000 eln/kg for White Genesis Crystal**, explicitly preferring the 31-based number over the exact sevenfold alternative of 35,000. This **supersedes the OPEN White row above**; current authoritative price is 31,000 eln/kg = 3,100 eln/100 g = 31 eln/g. Relative to Violet at 5,000 eln/kg, the White premium is 6.2×. The White-crystal price does not set White Genesis Dust price or processing yield. The author's preference was for a prime-related numeral: 31 is prime, while 31,000 is composite.
