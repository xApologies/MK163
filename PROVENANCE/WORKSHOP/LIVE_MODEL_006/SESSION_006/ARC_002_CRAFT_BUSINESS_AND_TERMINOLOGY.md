# MK163 Live Model 006 — Arc 2 author decisions, 2026-10-19
Status: AUTHOR-ESTABLISHED WORKSHOP; not yet committed to GitHub.

## Dictation / lexicon correction
- `aen` (A-E-N): cutaneous biological Genesis anchors, dot→arc→ring→band.
- `Ain` (A-I-N): an individual persistent enchantment, distinct from `aen`.
- Do NOT automatically convert every voice-to-text `ain` to `aen`; resolve from context. Earlier overly broad alias rule is superseded.
- `Kira` in dictated MK163 character discussion means Kaira; `Rayon` means raeon.

## Arc architecture
- Arc 2 is a substantial, slow-paced slice-of-life arc focused on Kaira's independence and freedom, while introducing Genesis Card mechanics, aen training, raeon social play, artisan commerce, city life, and the broader World Clock.
- Develop arcs before allocating chapters. Author targets ~1,100–1,300 pages per book and provisionally ~40–80 pages per chapter, possibly shorter; no fixed Arc 2 chapter count.
- The first robe's completion and public reveal ends Arc 2; wider popularity and business growth accelerate in Arc 3.

## Separate competencies
- Kaira already owns and knows activation of her mother's Fireball Genesis Card. She is NOT new to Genesis Card activation.
- Metal Manipulation Genesis Card is a new operational discipline requiring precise control and practice; knowledge of Fireball does not confer Metal Manipulation mastery.
- Kaira practices Metal Manipulation on ready-made chainmail rings bought from a Blacksmith Row smith; she does not manufacture these rings herself during this new business setup.
- Independently, Kaira learns cutaneous `aen` control, dot to arc to closed ring to widened band; mastery and basin access require practice, and this frustrates her. Aen do not increase operational Card throughput under AERA rules.

## Artisan identity and business
- Kaira is an expert manual chainmail armorsmith: heating metal, drawing wire, winding mandrels, forming rings, closing/assembling mail and finishing; years of manual craft preceded her use of Metal Manipulation.
- She begins a friendship with a particular Blacksmith Row supplier (identity not yet chosen). He respects her craft knowledge. She purchases a large box/quantity of ready-made rings from him.
- She constructs an ordinary, high-quality demonstration chainmail shirt to show her skill, not a magical showcase object.
- She approaches dungeon delvers to demonstrate workmanship and offer pricing, initially acquiring few orders; business grows slowly before the robe attracts widespread attention.
- Raeon is primarily social recreation and relationship-building in this arc, distinct from Genesis Cards as functional Transduction equipment.

## Author's narrative intent
- Arc 1 introduced Kaira, her life-threatening Affliction, crisis and Awakening. Arc 2 builds her foundation and lets readers learn the world by observing her daily life.
- Kaira's new life is no longer dominated by expectation of early death. Slice-of-life pace is deliberate rather than a narrative omission.
- Preserve her residual Afflicted stigma and mistaken noble-status rumors as separate reactions to her appearance and costly Awakening.
