# MK163 — Chromatic Individual Income Scale

Status: AUTHOR-ESTABLISHED, conversation 2026-10-19; local live-model record, not yet committed to GitHub.
Owner: WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/ECONOMY/ (proposed dedicated income file)

The author sets reference Red individual income at 100 eln per month and uses eleven accounting months to derive 1,100 eln annually. Retain previously discussed relative multipliers ×1, ×2, ×4, ×12, ×30, ×50. The resulting reference individual earnings (not fixed district residency thresholds or professional ranks) are:

| Tier | Multiple | Eln/month | Eln/year |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Red | 1 | 100 | 1,100 |
| Orange | 2 | 200 | 2,200 |
| Yellow | 4 | 400 | 4,400 |
| Green | 12 | 1,200 | 13,200 |
| Blue | 30 | 3,000 | 33,000 |
| Violet | 50 | 5,000 | 55,000 |

Economic distinction: major jump Yellow→Green, further elite jump Blue→Violet. Individual income is the accounting unit; households can combine incomes. Nobility, commercial revenue, accumulated wealth, professional rank, and residency are distinct. No apartment rents or Genesis equipment prices are locked by this decision. Former Red weekly five-eln suggestion and fifty-eln rent proposal are superseded for modeling purposes.

Calendar caveat: MK163 currently has eleven historical months totaling 351 days while the canonical year is 360 days. The author expressly selected eleven-month annual salary accounting; this does not resolve the missing nine calendar days or specify their wage treatment.

Currency: eln banknotes and ahm coins; 1 Red eln = 180 ahm. The six White eln note values are 180/360/540/720/900/1080 eln.
