# MK163 Live Model 006 — Genesis-engineered heavy weapons and impulse geometry
Status: AUTHOR-APPROVED WORKSHOP CANON (2026-10-19). Target: MK163 World Clock L0/L2, with AERA/_bricked inheritance references; not yet committed to GitHub.

## Author-approved mechanism
Massive war mauls, oversized swords (including six-to-seven-foot examples), and large war axes can be wielded through Genesis-engineered reduction of experienced handling inertia and gravitational load, combined with a distinct impact recoupling function. Personal augmentation can complement the equipment. Inertial decoupling with impact recoupling is the accepted conceptual architecture. Reaction/recoil management is required. Weapon attachment/anchoring to the Genesis Field or Shield as the way to displace apparent mass is a **working hypothesis**, not yet an established sigil topology or proven physical mechanism. No universal percentage of reduction, enchantment recipe, or energy cost has been fixed.

## Preferred geometric impulse representation
Vector displacement in momentum space:
J = p_final - p_initial = Δp.
For a sufficiently isolated *combined* system, vector closure:
Δp_weapon + Δp_target + Δp_GenesisField + Δp_wielder/surroundings (if applicable) = 0.
A closed vector polygon is the primary geometric visual language. The simplified three-term closure is valid only if all omitted external interactions are negligible or already included in the field term. A target's received impulse depends on its own momentum change; the maul's initial momentum alone is not automatically the target impulse.

## Classical calculus cross-reference (preserve)
J = ∫_(t_initial)^(t_final) F_net(t) dt = Δp; average net force F_avg = J/Δt. For constant mass and ordinary mechanics p = mv, E_kinetic = (1/2)mv². An engineered field supplying additional momentum/energy must balance the full weapon–target–field–wielder system. Geometry does not remove the need for conservation laws.

## Illustrative 300-pound maul example (NOT universal design parameters)
Physical mass ≈ 136 kg; experienced handling inertia ≈ 13.6 kg; impact speed 10 m/s. The illustrative reduced-state momentum/energy are 136 kg·m/s and 680 J; full-mass equivalents are 1,360 kg·m/s and 6,800 J. A field-mediated equivalent would have to account for differences of 1,224 kg·m/s and 6,120 J under these simplified assumptions. Gravitational weight reduction is a separate control from inertia reduction. Momentum, energy, contact geometry, duration and recoil determine actual outcomes; no fixed damage multiplier is implied.
