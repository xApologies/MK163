## Author's selection and qualification
The author selected this exact six-note image after several render iterations. Its format, engraving language, heraldic ornament, horizontal proportions, and red/orange/yellow/green/blue/violet accents are the accepted reference. **The actual in-world notes are to have a distinctly whiter background than depicted in this image**: bright white/pearl-white rather than warm yellow, amber, aged parchment or rustic cream. The image is an approved *reference*, not a literal approval of its warmer paper tint. Do not regenerate it and claim a new variant was approved.

## Six denominations
- White eln with Red markings: 180 eln
- White eln with Orange markings: 360 eln
- White eln with Yellow markings: 540 eln
- White eln with Green markings: 720 eln
- White eln with Blue markings: 900 eln
- White eln with Violet markings: 1,080 eln

The notes bear **AELLIN CROWN** and **ELN CERTIFICATE** on the artwork; Aellin Crown is the royal house. The six are banknotes, not White ahm coins. This visual series is for high-value transactions and is separate from the standard chromatic 1/6/12/18/24/30 eln note series. The exchange baseline separately accepted in workshop: six Violet ahm coins, each 30 ahm, equal one Red eln (180 ahm).
