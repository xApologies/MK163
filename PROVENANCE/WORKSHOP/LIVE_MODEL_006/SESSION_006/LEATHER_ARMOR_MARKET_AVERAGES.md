# MK163 — Leather armor market averages

Status: AUTHOR-APPROVED, 2026-10-19. World Clock L2; local live-model delta, not yet merged into MK163 GitHub.

## Canonical unenchanted average retail prices (eln)

| Configuration | Average eln | High-end artisan example |
|---|---:|---|
| Light leather | 50 | Unassigned |
| Medium leather | 110 | Approximately 200–300 eln possible |
| Full leather | 180 | Approximately 400 eln possible |
| Standard chainmail shirt (comparison) | 200 | See CHAINMAIL_SHIRT_PRICE.md |

These are market averages, not uniform mandated prices, and do not depend on district identity. Price varies by fit, quality, materials, artistry, coverage, and craftsmanship. High-end examples are indicative, not hard price caps. All listed values exclude enchanting, Ain, Genesis materials for enchantments, and special magical enhancement. Three coverage classes are MK163 working equipment categories, not claims of standardized medieval classifications. The 200-eln chainmail comparison is independently locked and includes ordinary materials and assembly but excludes enchantment.
