# MK163 Live Model 006 — Standard Chainmail Shirt Price

Status: AUTHOR-APPROVED, pending Codex integration into MK163.
Date: 2026-10-19
Owner: World Clock L2 / armorsmithing economy; relevance: Red Arc 2, Kaira's chainmail livelihood.

## Canon decision
Standard well-crafted, non-enchanted chainmail shirt: **200 eln** reference retail price.

## Context
User approved this price following a comparison with English fourteenth-century mail-shirt prices and modeled wages. This is an MK163 price decision, not a historical currency conversion. A Red-profile individual earning 100 eln/month has two months of gross income equivalent; Orange 200/month one month; Yellow 400/month half a month. Actual savings time depends on expenses.

## Boundaries
The 200-eln price is for a standard finished shirt, not Kaira's ring acquisition cost, manufacturing cost, repair fees, enchantment surcharge, or customized armor. Kaira buys premanufactured rings from a Blacksmith Row supplier, manually assembles a demonstration shirt, and markets her craft to dungeon delvers. Her precise margins, supplier pricing, and negotiated commissions remain open.

## Provenance
Author message: 'we can just lock it in 200 eln'. Supersedes earlier speculative 150–300 eln range as the standard reference.

## Session 006 author-approved production allocation — 2026-10-19
Status: LOCKED in the working live model; pending Codex integration.
- Standard non-enchanted shirt: 23,000 prefabricated chainmail rings.
- Finished retail benchmark: 200 eln, unchanged.
- Ring acquisition: 80 eln per 23,000-ring batch (40% of retail benchmark).
- Remaining 120 eln (60%) covers Kaira's assembly, fitting, finishing, incidental business costs and commercial margin; **not automatically net profit**.
- Implied bulk unit cost: 80 / 23,000 eln per ring (approximately 0.00347826 eln, or 0.626087 ahm at 180 ahm/eln). This is a derived unit equivalent, not a mandated individual-ring retail price.
- No enchanting or Ain is included. Enchanted variants and other armor classes remain unpriced.
- Kaira sources finished rings from a Blacksmith Row supplier; she does not need to manufacture the rings herself.
- Supersedes the earlier statement in this record that supplier pricing and margins were wholly open. Her actual net profit, assembly time, and incidental expense breakdown remain open.
Provenance: Author expressly approved the 80/120 eln allocation after discussion of 23,000 rings per shirt: 'I agree with this. Let's lock it in.'
