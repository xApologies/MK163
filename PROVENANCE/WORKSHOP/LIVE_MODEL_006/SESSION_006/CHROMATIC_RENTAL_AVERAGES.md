# MK163 — Chromatic residential rental averages

Status: AUTHOR-ESTABLISHED, workshop 2026-10-19; not yet integrated into remote MK163.
Owner: WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/ECONOMY/ (proposed new rental record); dependencies: CHROMATIC_INDIVIDUAL_INCOME_SCALE.md and CHROMATIC_DISTRICTS.md.

The author explicitly approved the six plotted midpoint values as **average monthly residential rents**, rather than mandatory prices or minima for each residence. The prior model's broader rental ranges were proposals, not separately approved hard bounds. All prices are in eln (banknotes). The income values below are previously approved individual monthly income reference values, not household incomes, and district economic categories do not imply professional rank.

| District | Average rent (eln/month) | Reference individual income (eln/month) | Average rent / reference income |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Red | 50 | 100 | 50% |
| Orange | 90 | 200 | 45% |
| Yellow | 175 | 400 | 43.75% |
| Green | 600 | 1200 | 50% |
| Blue | 1400 | 3000 | 46.67% |
| Violet | 3750 | 5000 | 75% |

The major Yellow→Green jump and Blue→Violet luxury premium are deliberate. White is a civic district, not a residential tier. Cleanliness and basic infrastructure are preserved across districts; higher rents represent larger residences, services, amenities, luxury and location, not decay of the lower districts. Kaira's Orange apartment price is still OPEN; 90 eln is the district average, not her agreed rent. A household may pool multiple earners' income. The calendar's eleven inherited months contain a known nine-day discrepancy against the 360-day year; do not silently repair it. Historical 50-eln Red rent was withdrawn as a universal price and reintroduced only as the average through explicit author approval.
