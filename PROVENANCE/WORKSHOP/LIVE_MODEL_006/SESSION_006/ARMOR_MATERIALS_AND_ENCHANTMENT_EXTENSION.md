# MK163 Live Model 006 — Armor materials and enhancement decisions
Status: AUTHOR-ESTABLISHED workshop decisions, 2026-10-19. Target MK163 World Clock L2. Not yet pushed to GitHub.

- Ordinary leather armor in this setting is animal-free, produced from cultivated fungal mycelium rather than hides. This does not remove fauna such as Nyx from canon.
- Velis-supported agricultural growth fields accelerate mycelium cultivation; exact growth rates, processing yields, and resource costs remain open.
- Average finished non-enchanted armor prices: light leather 50 eln; medium leather 110 eln; full leather 180 eln. Premium physical craftsmanship may reach 200–300 eln for medium and around 300–400 eln for full, without assuming higher defensive performance merely from aesthetics.
- A standard chainmail shirt may be worn beneath full leather armor. Combined average base purchase is 380 eln, excluding any additional fitting, underlayers, or enchantments.
- Ordinary and premium armor may be enhanced via compatible sigil engineering, persistent Ain enchantments, advanced alchemical materials, and Genesis resource inputs. Enchantment costs are separate and not yet universally priced.
- AERA owns the five material types (Type 1 ordinary chemistry; Type 2–5 Genesis-mediated materials) and four enchantment configurations. Vaen are separate identity-bearing resources. These are possible inputs, not mandatory components of every armor or Genesis Card.
- Genesis Card custom crafting may begin with an existing card or be commissioned anew. Classification benchmark and added resource costs are distinguished, with safeguards against double-counting the price of already-incorporated materials. The example Advanced 540 eln plus 1300–1800 eln of additional resources was illustrative, not a fixed surcharge.
