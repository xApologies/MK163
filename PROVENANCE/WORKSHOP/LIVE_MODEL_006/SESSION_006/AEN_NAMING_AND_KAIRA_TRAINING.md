## Canonical spelling and dictation
The canonical term is lowercase `aen` (A-E-N). In dictation, contextual references to "Aen" or wrist rings representing Awakened biological Genesis anchors mean `aen`. Do not conflate `aen` with `Ain` (an enchantment), `Aineth` (enchanting process), or `Aenith` (enchanted object). Preserve exact original quotes in provenance. Apply normalization to active narrative and canonical prose. Add this rule to MK163's naming convention at Codex integration.

## Arc 2 — Kaira's learning curve
Kaira begins deliberate aen practice during Red Arc 2. She has theoretical knowledge of Genesis Field mechanics but is a beginner at exercising the newly Awakened biological anchors. Basin access, formation, closure, width, endurance, and control are learned skills, not automatic benefits of Awakening. She struggles, becomes frustrated, and improves through repetition; competency and mastery require practice, as with other disciplines in the book. Do not preassign a successful milestone, time-to-mastery, exact basin, or chapter location without author decision.

## Author pronunciation clarification — 2026-10-19
- `aen` (lowercase; A-E-N): pronounced approximately **AY-n**, /eɪn/; long A as in *day*, soft final n. Refers to cutaneous biological Genesis anchors.
- `Ain` (capitalized as established lexical term; A-I-N): pronounced approximately **EYE-n**, /aɪn/; vowel as in *eye*, soft final n. Refers to an individual persistent enchantment.
- The pronunciations are author-provided aids for dictation disambiguation; **never globally autocorrect `Ain` into `aen`**. Interpret according to phonetic cue and context; flag genuinely unresolved ambiguity.
- Preserve separate `Aenith` and `Aineth` terminology and existing repository orthography.
