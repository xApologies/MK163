# MK163 Live Model 006 — Melee combat competency and powered weapons
Status: AUTHOR-ESTABLISHED workshop decisions, 2026-10-19. Target MK163 World Clock L2/L3 combat culture. Not yet pushed to GitHub.

- Proposed population distribution among conventional melee weapon users: 60% swords, 25% mauls/maces, 15% axes. This is not a population-wide share of all aithren, and remains a proposed rather than locked demographic statistic.
- Massive sword, massive war axe, and massive maul are author-level working labels; physical manufacturing average 165 eln each before enhancements.
- Weapon combat competency includes guard stances, transitions between guards/weapon forms, attacks and recovery, plus coordinated Genesis activation timing.
- Approved conceptual mechanism: inertial decoupling during handling, Genesis-mediated impact recoupling and optionally amplified collision response. The wielder may activate an impact system shortly before contact and release it for recovery; sustaining the amplified state makes guard transitions and handling harder unless augmentation/engineering compensates.
- The proposed field-mediated exchange must account for energy, momentum and reaction forces; simply assigning a larger mass does not automatically generate impact energy. Exact topology, limits, activation latency, and reservoir mechanics remain open.
- Personal augmentation, armor (leather, mail, plate/Juggernaut), and melee weapon engineering coexist, allowing martial specialization without assuming superiority over other Transduction interfaces.
- AERA owns intrinsic, activated, finite-reservoir, and chromatic-conduit enchantment configurations; they may be composed in a single item and are not weapon rarity tiers.
- Formal mathematics, including geometric impulse and calculus equivalence, is in GENESIS_WEAPON_IMPULSE_GEOMETRY.md; do not duplicate or change equations here.
