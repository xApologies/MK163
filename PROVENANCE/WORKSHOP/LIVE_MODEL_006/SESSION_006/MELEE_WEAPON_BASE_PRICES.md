# MK163 Live Model 006 — Melee weapon base prices

Status: AUTHOR-APPROVED, established in conversation 2026-10-19. Target: MK163 World Clock L2 equipment economy. Pending Codex repository integration.

## Standard unenchanted physical weapons (average retail reference)
- Standard sword: 35 eln.
- Standard large war axe: 30 eln.
- Standard war maul: 20 eln.

## Massive unenchanted physical weapons (average retail reference)
- Massive sword: 165 eln.
- Massive war axe: 165 eln.
- Massive maul: 165 eln.

The shared 165-eln average supersedes the provisional 130–200-eln range as the central market benchmark, without forbidding quality, size, or commission price variation. The terms 'massive sword', 'massive war axe', and 'massive maul' are author-level working labels, not new formal weapon ranks.

All six prices describe physical construction before Genesis engineering. Intrinsic mass/handling modification, activated impact recoupling/amplification, sigil work, Ain enchantments, Genesis crystals, Genesis Diamonds, vaen, and other enhancements are priced separately as applicable. No automatic enhancement fee or formula is established.

The medieval English shilling prices were used only as historical inspiration, not a literal currency exchange rate. Do not conflate an inferred historical price with documented market averages.
