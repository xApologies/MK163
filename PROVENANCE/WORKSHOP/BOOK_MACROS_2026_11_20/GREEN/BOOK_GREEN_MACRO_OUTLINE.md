# MK163 — Book Green (Book Four) — Author-approved macro outline

Status: conversation-approved macro outline; chapter/scene realization OPEN. The seven colors below are **arcs within Book Green**, not seven books or realm restrictions. Kaira and illi are protected spellings.

## Book-level frame
Kaira enters Book Green as a newly recruited high-authority officer of **Crown Intelligence**, the intelligence/espionage branch of **Special Projects**, an independent Crown command. Other Special Projects branches address conventional warfare and ELDRIS/Domai operations. The Crown Intelligence officer's authority is directly below Princess **illi** in the relevant established authority relationship and generally can supersede ordinary command structures; formal rank and limits are OPEN. The old operative label “Reaper” is **author-level legacy only**, not canonical; a replacement following ae/ai naming conventions is OPEN. Princess illi is a Crown diplomat, a gifted Transduction practitioner, and Kaira's close friend; she is **not** a Special Projects operative. She was not a domain governor.

The book features direct involvement with regional cartels and pirate-supported international trafficking, culminating in substantial disruption of **continental cartel/black-market infrastructure**. The **coordinated Pirate King/Lords organization is NOT dismantled in Green**; that is a Book Blue milestone. Criminal trade and its consequences persist.

## Red Arc — Crown Intelligence orientation
Kaira undergoes accelerated officer training in Crown Intelligence, learning diplomatic conduct, intelligence gathering, espionage-related responsibilities, and operation under her exceptional Crown authority. Her existing relationship with illi provides prior diplomatic familiarity. Exact instructors, duration and missions OPEN.

## Orange Arc — Domestic cartel pressure
The western home kingdom faces renewed kidnapping/ransom operations, drug problems, cartel activity, and pirate-linked black-market pressure. The noble-ransom cartel dismantled in Book Orange **stays dismantled**; other organizations exploit its power vacuum. Crown-level attention and domestic investigation are established. Exact operations and outcomes OPEN.

## Yellow Arc — Noble siblings and merchant-city pursuit
An older noble sister (~20) and younger brother (~13) are kidnapped in the kingdom's Violet Domain. Kaira and Princess illi investigate with a Crown/diplomatic entourage. They identify cartel involvement and follow an overland route into one of the central merchant city-states toward a bay, where the kidnappers plan a pirate-vessel pickup. Merchant cities have import/export trade but comparatively weaker naval enforcement than the home kingdom, making maritime extraction attractive. The two siblings are **successfully rescued**; exact rescue method, ship timing, and identities OPEN. The party returns overland.

## Green Arc — Unicorn bond and operational synchronization
Kaira and Princess **illi** return overland from the successful rescue of the two noble siblings. illi reports to the Crown. An older Unicorn delegation from the Violet Realm has waited in private palace quarters, with the living Black Unicorn kept secluded. The Unicorns authored all eight prophecies and maintain an information network; Kaira's third Black bond with the Black Dragon egg prompted their visit. They interview Kaira before bringing the Black Unicorn forward to bond. Kaira does not travel to the Violet Realm for this bond; the Unicorn retains independent agency.

**Former Blue Arc training is consolidated into Green.** Kaira receives authorized training autonomy, roughly splitting her time between formal Special Projects shaping instruction and independent repeated Violet Dungeon delves to synchronize with Nyx, the Black Sprite, Black Vaelith, Black Dragon and Black Unicorn. The mapped dungeon offers comparative control but real combat stress. No permanent Special Projects escort is selected. Concurrently, illi investigates the cartels, black market and pirate connections. Precise interview, training, delves, outcomes and mechanics OPEN.

## Blue Arc — Central merchant city-state cartel campaign
**Former Violet Arc moves to Blue.** Kaira and illi return to the merchant city-states, gather intelligence on interconnected cartels and disrupt their black-market infrastructure. The home Crown works against cartels in the western kingdom. No individual city, cartel, leader, tactical operation or full eradication is selected.

## Violet Arc — Eastern republic relations and merged Domai
**Former White Arc's republic arrival and Domai encounter move to Violet.** Kaira and illi travel into the eastern republic's constituent states, establish working relationships there, and Kaira helps defeat a locally forming **merged Domai**, earning credibility with the republic. This is distinct from Book Yellow's merged Domai engagement. Exact state, participants, mechanics and diplomatic arrangements OPEN. The republic cartel campaign does not yet conclude here.

## White Arc — Republic cartel disruption and continental black-market impact
Kaira and illi work to identify, isolate and disrupt the eastern republic's regional cartel network. This culminates in substantial **continent-wide disruption of interconnected black-market infrastructure**, following the home Crown's domestic efforts and the girls' merchant-city campaign. The disruption significantly hinders the pirates' drug and human trafficking operations, without eliminating all cartels, the black market, trafficking or piracy. The coordinated Pirate King/Lords organization remains the Book Blue milestone. Exact operations, timing, scope and final scene OPEN.

## Geographic / political frame
The continent includes the western home kingdom, central enormous merchant city-states (author-level term “sanctuaries”; number uncertain ~5–7, perhaps seven; roughly 20–30 mile radius each), and eastern states forming a larger militaristic republic oriented toward fighting ELDRIS. Merchant cities are governed by merchant houses, supported by sigils for water, accelerated agriculture, waste dissolution and runoff. Their comparatively weak naval presence contributes to the trafficking route. Do not canonize tentative names, counts, boundaries or routes beyond the author's decisions. The uploaded Kingdoms map is an author visual reference; do not invent named places.
