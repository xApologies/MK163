# LIVE_MODEL_009 — Current author decision

**Status: AUTHOR-DEFINED / SUPERSEDES ACTIVE MK163 BLACK-CARD MECHANIC.**

Author: “I think we’ve been trying to force a mechanic with the black cards that don’t need to exist. The black cards were an interesting mechanic for 727 when genesis cards acted as the only domain of transduction, but now the black cards I think are creating a problem that doesn’t need to exist. We have 3 forms of transduction now I don’t think we need to add a new flavor of complexity just for the sake of it. Kaira can still craft cards. Still use genesis cards, but I think we keep the ‘black’ mechanic strictly to the prophecies and the native races rather than make up an arbitrary ‘cool’ mechanic.”

Author follow-up: “We allow the neon to be expressed through her relationship with the black. We were making things overly complex by trying to force a black card mechanic.”

## Governing outcome
- **Retire** specialized Black Genesis Cards in MK163: six realm-specific universal Card anchors, six-card unified coupling, Black Card intelligence framework, special Neon Card activation/occupancy subsystem, mandatory Armor/CSR/Orbs/Halo/Domain as Black Card powers, and any requirement that Kaira forge a Black Card in Arc V.
- **Keep** three existing Transduction interfaces: native Genesis expression, Binding Circles (Kaira incompatible with conventional installations, exceptionally bonds with Black denizens), and ordinary Genesis Cards. No fourth interface.
- **Keep** Black Singularity and unified Neon as a feature of Kaira's Black identity and her exceptional relationships with the six Black denizens, not the engineered cards. Black denizens retain independent agency and native realm magic; Kaira learns and develops expression through these bonds.
- **Keep** ordinary Cardsmithing as Kaira's profession, use of existing Fireball and Metal Manipulation cards, and Arc IV formal Cardsmith education. No invented Arc V replacement milestone.
- **Keep** LIVE_MODEL_008 internal manifold topology, invisible geometry, potentially visible loaded and dissipated energy, persistent scaffolds, practitioner-controlled energy and ordinary Card occupancy. Keep dungeon/community development.
- **Preserve** MK727 and LIVE_MODEL_008 Black Card designs as provenance, not active MK163 requirements. Armor of the Abyss can be reconsidered as an optional future Shaping only through new explicit author approval; do not add it now.

## Open after retirement
Exact future Arc V activities, Kaira's first original ordinary Card (if any), black mushroom use, advanced native Neon expression and its limits, detailed Black bond progression, and existing ordinary card occupancy interactions. These are not instructions to fabricate solutions.
