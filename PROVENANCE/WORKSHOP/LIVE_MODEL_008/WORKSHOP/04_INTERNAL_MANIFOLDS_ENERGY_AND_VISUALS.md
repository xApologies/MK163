# Workshop 04 — Internal manifold topology, Shaping, residual energy and visual grammar

## Author's new fundamental localization rule
The user moves personal Genesis-field manifold formation and maintenance **inside the transductionist's Genesis Field**, rather than requiring visible external giant geometry. Manifold geometry is ordinarily invisible, although a practitioner can understand, sense or experience its configuration through their Genesis-field competency. The practitioner's internal field is the primary anchor, **not necessarily the complete spatial support**: for exceptional high-complexity or large-scale Shapings the same internally anchored manifold may extend a few feet, meters, or potentially much farther outside the body. Such extension does not require the manifold topology itself to become visible. This author clarification refines how native Genesis expression, conventional installed Bindings and active Genesis Cards are depicted. It does not prohibit external physical Binding Circles, alchemy tables, sigils, ER Foam or purposely projected effects.

## The spiky-ball Binding analogy
A conventional installed Binding is like a compact spiky ball that expands into a sphere/manifold. The Binding is installed by a physical Binding Circle earlier, then exists as reusable personal architecture. On activation its scaffold expands internally and is sustained by the person's Genesis Field. Large Bindings may have extended spatial support outside the body while retaining internal anchoring. The Binding reduces the structural burden of forming and holding complex topology, but does NOT supply limitless energy or skill. Kaira is conventionally Binding-incompatible; her Black universal Card architecture offers an alternative path, without replacing the Binding profession for others.

## Cards and practitioner responsibilities
A conventional physical Genesis Card may float beside or orbit a maegi, visible and active, anchored in the practitioner's field. Its embedded manifold organizes the practitioner's **internal** active scaffold. The user emphasized: the transductionist controls energy movement into the manifold, corridor loading, throughput, distribution, conscious collapse, timing and subsequent reloading. The card or Binding helps provide geometry, not complete execution. Native expression demands the practitioner form/stabilize the geometry as well. Same card in two practitioners yields different reliability, cadence, efficiency, fatigue and precision. Formal AERA lifecycle remains `Conceive → Shape → Hold → Resolve → Manifest → Release`; any five-step teaching summaries are not a replacement canon.

## Reuse and rapid fire
The scaffold is **not necessarily destroyed** when organized energy is consciously collapsed into a realized Shaping. Fireball example: activate and expand Fireball Binding internally, load Red/Yellow/Green or whatever **actual** required energy profile (the author mentioned colors illustratively; do not assign definitive Fireball basin recipe), collapse energy into a fireball, retain scaffold, reload, repeat. Speed is limited by energy transfer, concept, fatigue and control. Ten fireballs in rapid succession can generate billowing energy around a caster because earlier residual dissipation has not fully dispersed before later casts. An active card can remain visible throughout.

## Energy visibility — important supersession
The author first emphasized that **inefficiency/residual energy** escaping during Shaping becomes visible as wisps/puffs/clouds and is absorbed into the local environment. Later explicitly clarified that **organized energy being moved into/through the manifold may also be visible**. Therefore **not all visible energy is waste**. Distinguish:
- Invisible manifold geometry/topology: internal or externally extended, ordinarily not visually traced.
- Loaded/organized Genesis energy: may be visible while moving/accumulating in the scaffold, including around an extended manifold.
- Residual/inefficient Genesis energy: visible chromatic puffs, billowing, clouds drifting away from the practitioner and dispersing into the environment as energy is not fully incorporated into the intended Shaping.
- Intended Shaping: external visible fireball, plasma, etc. where applicable.

Do not assert every casting has an aura or that an aura always measures power. A novice may waste much energy; a master may handle so much energy that even small fractional loss creates large clouds. Author favors large, spectacular but internally anchored manifolds for high-fantasy master Transduction. A massive manifold does not automatically mean large output; geometry, spatial extent, throughput and efficiency are separate variables. No exact conservation formula, visible-color palette per spell, half-life of dissipation or radiation hazard has been selected.
