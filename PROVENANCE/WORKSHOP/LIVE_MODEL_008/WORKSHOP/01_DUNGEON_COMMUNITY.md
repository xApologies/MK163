## Distinct aithren communities
Dungeon-delving aithren culture blends **1980s computer-engineering curiosity**, **NASCAR infield social/festival atmosphere**, and **forward-operating-base logistics and professionalism**. Storm-chasing aithren instead blend **tornado-chasing** and **elite field operators**, operating against storms/domai in the Wilds. Do not mix their distinct operational contexts or suggest the dungeon staging settlement is continuously threatened by monsters escaping.

A dungeon is a persistent environmental Genesis prism, stratified in contiguous chromatic basins. Eldris are restricted to compatible strata and cannot escape into the outer community. This explains the safe, convivial surface economy without making deep expeditions safe. Inside, mapped routes, changing spawn/resource sites, fatigue, logistics and environmental conditions make expeditions serious. People outside play raeon, eat, drink abecca, exchange Binding and Genesis Card configurations, compare transductive builds, trade materials, discuss new alchemy and relax. Competence and mutual contribution matter.

## Logistics
Teams may arrive and stay at inns two or three nights to assemble, coordinate and purchase supplies, descend for roughly two weeks (illustrative), then return for food, sleep, equipment repair, information exchange and resupply. Some remain six months, renting private housing. A large dungeon may extend **17–23 miles** down (working specific range), with long access travel and multi-week logistics; depth is not guaranteed route length. Dimensional rings/bracelets can simplify food/tool storage, but not every group has one. Kaira has NO dimensional ring before Book Red's tournament ending. Professionalism is rooted in preparation and survival, not constant martial severity. Staging settlement hospitality is a major economic sector.
