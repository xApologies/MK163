# Intersemester break — revised author-selected chronology

Calendar: Semester I ends Day 319; break Days 320–340 inclusive (21 days); Semester II starts Day 341. These are cumulative story days, not semester-local days. The established 353-day annual academic cycle is unchanged.

## Day 319 closing scene
The Alchemist attends the Semester Symposium & Feast to celebrate Kaira and illi. She reports that original and imitated Chainmail Robes are spreading through other large cities and capital cities and that the transductionist identity is inspiring renewed interest in academies. She invites Kaira to stay at her existing Blue District residence in the Blue Domain capital.

## Days 320–330 — visits and personal work
- Kaira stays with the Alchemist for approximately eleven days at her established Blue District apartment. She takes Nyx and the young Black Sprite; the mature Sprite Professor has no standing sessions, having returned to his own research.
- Kaira brings the unfinished handmade robe for Elaira and completes it during this visit, without asserting a precise work schedule or completion date.
- Kaira enjoys returning to the Yellow District's prestigious Blacksmith Row. She visits working smiths, admires their craftsmanship and reconnects with her early training in chainmail. An unnamed smith who recognizes her designs reports their expansion into other cities and capitals and commissions an original robe personally made by Kaira. No price, ring count or deadline is selected.
- Princess illi **visits**, rather than moves home to, her older sister **Princess aina** (exact lowercase name), who governs the Blue Domain. aina resides at a palace in the **White District of the Blue Domain capital**. aina sees illi's original five-layer robe, becomes fascinated by it and asks for a Kaira-made original of her own. illi relays the request when she rejoins Kaira. The precise date of the sisters' visit and of the request remain open.
- Elaira's independent break plans, family circumstances and exact whereabouts are not established.

## Day 331 — reunion and gift
Kaira returns to Blackstone with Elaira's completed chainmail robe and surprises E with it. illi rejoins the group and conveys Princess aina's commission request. The timing within Day 331 remains open; do not assert a precise arrival hour.

## Day 332 — three-girl city trip
Kaira, illi and Elaira take a rickshaw into the Blue Domain capital. Kaira places a **new large chainmail ring order covering two separate personally crafted original robes**: one for the unnamed Blacksmith Row smith and one for Princess aina. The girls spend time together in the city and return to Blackstone. The exact shop, price, quantity, delivery day, payment terms and whether the orders use a single production batch remain OPEN.

## Days 333–340 — remainder of break
The girls relax, share meals, play raeon, enjoy campus and city leisure, and exercise Nyx. Kaira may plan the two commissions, but **no ring delivery or construction start is established**. Semester II begins Day 341.

## Protected distinctions
- The completed robe for E is a personal gift, not part of the two-robe Day 332 procurement.
- Princess aina's robe and the smith's robe are two separate prestige commissions, distinct from ongoing Accord Registry originals and market knockoffs.
- Blacksmith Row's three prestigious artisan plazas belong to the Yellow District of the capital; do not relocate them to Green, Blue or White Districts.
- The existing living flower wall behind Kaira's bed remains at Blackstone during the visit.
- These are approved event anchors and an interval outline, **not** 21 separately authored 31-hour daily schedules or finished prose.
