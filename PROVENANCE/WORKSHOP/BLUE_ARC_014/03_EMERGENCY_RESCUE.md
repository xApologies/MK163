# Fourth expedition — Green cave-in emergency retrieval

**Identity:** Blue Arc's final/fourth expedition, a large urgent **volunteer rescue**, not reconnaissance, resource harvesting, or a planned commercial contract. **Departure:** Day 1055. **Return:** Day 1080. **Duration:** 26 calendar days inclusive (10 descent + 3 clearance + 13 ascent).

## Trigger and geography
A research expedition was headed for a known study objective in the Blue stratum. Deep in Green, near **10–11 vertical miles below the Blue capital dungeon entrance**, a cave-in split the expedition. Those on the upper/surface-accessible side withdrew and eventually reached the Registry; the deeper-side researchers/escorts are trapped. The escaped party has a **map** and knows the location. This is not a newly discovered cavern, a moving dungeon map, or a Green/Blue boundary breach. Existing Green stratum 6.5–11.5 mi; Blue starts at 11.5 mi. Existing local Green oscillation period is six days, but the author selects a **physical cave-in** as the isolating cause, not an oscillation-driven rearrangement of passages.

The original research group could not safely excavate or break through the obstruction. The rescuers need structural/excavation expertise, Guardians and combat specialists for ELDRIS, Helkir for injuries, environmental Supports for Genesis pressure, guides, logistics and provisions. Rescue force is **large and assembled from multiple volunteers**; no precise headcount, roster, survivor count, casualty count or excavation technology has been selected. Do not treat earlier assistant example of 23 rescuers or 8 trapped as canon.

The trapped party is exhausted, may have injuries and reduced supplies. Their precise survival provisions, method of enduring prolonged Genesis pressure, and survival outcome are not yet selected. The scenario requires plausible shelter/protection and resources; Codex should flag any unaddressed logistical constraints rather than invent devices or a communication system. Eldris confinement by stratum remains intact.

## Mission chronology
- **1055–1064:** ten-day rapid descent using the mapped route, minimizing discretionary delays but preserving necessary rest, security and shielding.
- **1065–1067:** three days of sustained rotating work stabilizing and removing the cave-in; the route is reopened.
- **1068–1080:** thirteen-day slower ascent with the stranded personnel; assistance, supplies, environmental protection and medical support as needed.
- **1080:** the rescue operation returns to the surface and Registry closes it; Kaira receives a **positive professional dossier commendation** for completed unpaid service, **not** an automatic rank promotion. She remains Green-certified.

The selected endpoint is a **completed rescue**, but precise individual fates, injuries, personal acts, engineering methods and other complications remain OPEN pending later scene writing. Do not assert 'all survived' unless the author selects it.
