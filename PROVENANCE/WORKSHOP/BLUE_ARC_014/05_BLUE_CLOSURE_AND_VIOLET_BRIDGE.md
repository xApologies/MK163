# Blue Arc final scene and Violet Arc bridge

## Day 1080 — Aithen · closing scene
The rescue force returns to the surface and the Crown Accord Registry accounts for the rescue and records Kaira's positive volunteer-service commendation. **That night**, at dinner in the Blue capital dungeon **staging settlement**, six members of a small existing vaelum who served alongside Kaira in the rescue invite her to join. During dinner Kaira gets along very well with them and **accepts**. This is the **last scene of Blue Arc (ARC_005)**. Do not end the scene on an unanswered invitation; acceptance is explicit author-selected canon.

The group has been **training at dungeons for the upcoming King's Tournament**, whose exact date is still OPEN. They have been looking for an environmental Support, and their shared rescue service gives them a credible basis for inviting Kaira. The group is independent within the larger multi-vaelum rescue force, not its overall commander. The six have distinct identities but **names, personalities, ranks and leadership are OPEN**.

## Tournament and arc transition
The King's Tournament **allows up to 11 registered members per team** (new author-selected cap). This seven-person group can compete below that maximum. No tournament date selected. Tournament institution and White Arc finale remain unchanged: quadrennial race circuit and Kaira's eventual fourth/Green finish with Dimensional Ring reward.

**Blue Arc complete at Day 1080.** **Violet Arc opens Day 1081 — Aiven** with Kaira already affiliated with the group. Violet centers on the vaelum's dungeon training runs, mapped route practice, formation and teamwork development, and preparation for the King's Tournament. Existing Violet Genesis Robe story must be preserved; this bridge does not erase it. White Arc carries the tournament itself.
