# Third expedition settlement and Day 1054 trigger

## Day 1049 closeout
- Third expedition returns from Yellow, its Helkir's death is documented and commercial/operational failure recorded.
- Group leader declares and submits the recovered qualifying Yellow Dust and Crystals for weighing and the Registry establishes the contracted shares; the system relies on the group's honest declaration of the recovered harvest.
- Existing market terms remain: 49 eln/100 g wholesale qualifying Yellow recovery; 35 eln to the seven-member vaelum, 7 eln to Kaira, 7 eln to the other environmental Support, before other expenses or internal divisions. No actual harvest weight is selected.
- Leader **promises deposit at the Crown Accord Registry on Day 1054**, five calendar days after Day 1049.

## Settlement framework
- Seven-day settlement window after closeout: Days 1050–1056 inclusive; Day 1056 final contractual deadline.
- Registry intermediates payment and guarantees the contracted Support share if the vaelum defaults by the final deadline; default causes a negative dossier entry for the responsible group. Additional collection/enforcement mechanisms are OPEN.
- A promised date before the deadline is not itself the default deadline. In this specific story, the author subsequently establishes that **Kaira collects her payment on Day 1054**, so her payment is actually available then. Do not invent the amount, source of funding or additional penalties.
- Compensation is **eln**, not automatic physical Dust transfer. The original '7 eln per 100 g' is a rate, not 7 grams or 7 eln in dust.

## Day 1054 — Aiveth
- Kaira comes to the Crown Accord Registry, collects her third-contract funds.
- Exhausted members of a different research expedition arrive and report a cave-in deep in Green near the Blue boundary. One separated group escaped upward; others remain stranded on the deep side.
- The returning members bring a map identifying the known passage and collapse location. They cannot safely clear the blockage with their existing research escort/equipment.
- The Registry organizes an urgent volunteer call to local dungeon-delving aithren, and Kaira signs up as a Green-certified environmental Support.
- The rescue is **unpaid volunteer service**. Registry positive dossier recognition follows completed service on Day 1080, not initial enrollment.
- The emergency's arrival coincides with Kaira's collection; the exact hour and the precise timing of the original cave-in remain OPEN.
