# Binding and Genesis Card access — cumulative author clarification

**Status:** author-approved working rules from MK163 Violet workshop, 2026-11-17. AERA Layer 1 interface mechanics. Read with `00_READ_FIRST.md`, `02_BINDING_CIRCLES.md`, `03_GENESIS_CARDS.md`, `04_CONCURRENCY_AND_AUTHORITY.md`, `../FATIGUE_CONCEPT.md`. Do not replace or delete those files.

## Shared Shaping, distinct interfaces
- A Shaping's chromatic energy-basin requirements are shared by its Binding and Genesis Card realizations. For **Continuous Spatial Resolution (CSR; DG-0031)** the current author-selected requirement is **Orange + Yellow + Blue + Violet**, superseding the catalogue's earlier Orange + Yellow record.
- **Operational Binding:** installed identity-resolved topology occupies finite personal Binding bandwidth; PSSP projects Shape/Hold geometry (author's expanding spiky sphere analogy). Practitioner consciously resolves energy into projected geometry and manifests the Shaping. CSR's illustrative working footprint is **approximately 60% of personal Binding allocation**; exact accounting, including access Bindings, remains OPEN.
- **Chromatic basin-access Bindings:** practitioners using a CSR Binding normally have access interfaces for Orange, Yellow, Blue, Violet, supplying energy to the CSR PSSP. Whether each access Binding consumes bandwidth and whether the 60% includes these is OPEN. Do not treat White superposition as a seventh ordinary chromatic basin.
- **Native White-superposed energy:** can be moved into a projected Binding manifold to fill energetic requirements; this draws heavily on finite personal reserves and is commonly emergency/backup supply, not an indefinite replacement for direct chromatic access. This is the author's practical analogy to an auxiliary fuel tank. Genesis diamonds also use stored Genesis energy; do not invent quantitative conversions.
- **Binding concurrency:** skilled practitioners can direct energy from the same basin to multiple simultaneous Bindings or compatible native Shapings, limited by control, throughput, endurance, installed bandwidth and admissibility. Example: CSR Binding in flight plus a compatible Fireball Binding sharing Blue may operate together if the practitioner's installed repertoire and access permit it.
- **Genesis Card exclusivity:** an active Card exclusively occupies ALL required chromatic basins for that practitioner, blocking overlapping Card, Binding, and native Shaping access. CSR Card locks O/Y/B/V while active. It cannot concurrently run Kaira's Shield (O+B), Beam (Y+G), or Fireball (R+B) as Cards. An inactive Card has no basin lock. Card activation precedence against an already-running conflicting Shaping remains OPEN.
- **No Card inventory cap:** a person may own arbitrarily many Cards, but simultaneous activation is limited by basin exclusivity and other operational constraints. Cards carry encoded geometry and are generally easier to operate than Bindings; Cards do not consume permanent Binding allocation. Bindings are cheaper but require installation bandwidth and greater operator control.

## CSR fatigue and deployment
- Sustained ordinary-speed CSR flight: low endurance/skill ~1–2 hours, moderate ~3–5 hours, high ~5–8 hours. These are working approximate upper-duration bands, not universal guarantees.
- Lower speed generally reduces fatigue accumulation; higher speed increases it. Complex maneuvering, load and other Shapings can also matter; exact formula OPEN.
- Even direct basin access cannot prevent practitioner fatigue: finite ability to access, resolve, sustain and control energy. Rest/recovery remains necessary. Rapid long-distance flight can leave an aithren unable to defend against ELDRIS.
- A Binding has high persistent bandwidth cost; a Card has four-basin exclusivity while active. The two restrictions are NOT equivalent.

## Occupational choice
- Dungeon-delving aithren often favor CSR **Cards** to preserve Binding allocation for combat, helkir and protection, even though Cards are expensive and constrain active basins.
- Storm-chaser vaelum may use dedicated CSR **Binding** flight specialists to establish spatial-corridor relay endpoints in the Wilds. Other personnel transit through linked gateways; the specialist accepts high Binding allocation because mobility/logistics is their principal job. Distances, relay protocol, required corridor Shapings, crew numbers and fatigue logistics remain OPEN.
- No claim that every aithren must have a Binding, Card, or identical access physiology. Respect AERA's author-selectable interfaces.
