# Day 865 gala — cumulative scene decision ledger (outline, not finished prose)

**Where:** Blue Domain capital. **When:** Story Day **865**, Aiven (sixth day of six), with midsummer-style gala atmosphere; exact seasonal astronomical correspondence OPEN.

## Gathering and companionship
- Aiselle is **already resident in the capital**, not arriving from another city. Kaira, Nyx, the independent Black Sprite and **Princess illi** meet at Aiselle's Blue District residence. The three women travel to the gala together. illi stays at a palace in the capital and is Kaira's close Blackstone friend.
- Aiselle wears her original Chainmail Robe; illi wears the five-layer original personally crafted for her by Kaira; Kaira wears her unique personal five-layer robe transformed by involuntary Genesis expression on **Day 171**. Aiselle introduces guests, circulates in her professional social network; illi provides Kaira a comfortable friend and social buffer. Aiselle occasionally disappears from Kaira's immediate company, allowing her to handle herself.
- Many attendees wear high-quality Chainmail Robes, including lower-layer and five-layer copies. Robes have become accepted fine attire and cultural signifiers of the Age of Knowledge. Kaira is widely recognized as the progenitor/inventor, an eccentric genius and designer; some guests are fascinated by her work.
- Kaira's personal robe retains dark-iron five-layer construction, hood and relief, with **intrinsic irregular Red-through-Violet material streaks** caused by a one-time involuntary Genesis outburst linked to her young Black Sprite bond. Do not describe it as ordinary applied paint, deliberate enchantment, repeatable manufacture, or proven superior armor. Her distinctive garment inspires conventional colored finishes, splash/speckle/stripe decoration, and display-only artistic robes in glass cases. Others imitate appearance, not the material event; artistic movement grows from here, no invented sales counts or formal guild.
