## Competition identity and cadence (retained)
- Kingdom-wide dungeon traversal/racing championship **once every four years**; exact year and race dates OPEN.
- Goal: descend through a huge persistent dungeon, activate the designated deepest marker, return to the surface as quickly as possible. Route selection, mobility, survival, avoidance of ELDRIS, scouting and team coordination matter; defeating every monster is NOT required.
- Dungeons can be enormous, with examples roughly 10–17 miles deep and substantial horizontal travel. Races can last days.
- Four royal domains: Violet, Orange, Green, Blue. Dungeons are always available for ordinary training and exploration outside tournament events.
- Seven finishing colors/points in qualifying points races: White 7, Violet 6, Blue 5, Green 4, Yellow 3, Orange 2, Red 1. Final championship finishing colors are White through Red.

## Revised staged championship (author selection; supersedes older 28-team final)
1. **Green local qualification:** each of four domains conducts local Green Dungeon qualifiers; successful teams advance. Exact number of participating Green dungeons and qualified teams OPEN. This stage is local, not a kingdom-wide travel circuit.
2. **Blue regional knockouts:** qualified teams in each domain convene at the first of **three selected Blue-ranked Dungeons in that domain**, race all three, and face a regional elimination/advancement cutoff based on results. The exact knockout method, scoring aggregation and advancing counts remain OPEN.
3. **Violet kingdom-wide points circuit:** all regional Blue-stage survivors converge at the first Violet Dungeon in the **Blue Domain**. The unified circuit then travels **Blue → Green → Orange → Violet**, with **two selected Violet-ranked Dungeons per domain** (eight races total). Scores accumulate across these eight races using the seven-color placement system. **The top seven teams across the entire kingdom** advance after all eight races. Whether interim knockouts occur during these eight races is NOT settled; do not insert them automatically.
4. **Royal final:** the final seven teams race in a **separate ninth Violet-ranked Dungeon** in the Violet Domain, associated with the royal capital and not previously used in that year's eight-race circuit. The championship is a single decisive final: earlier circuit points determine eligibility, but **the last race alone determines final White-through-Red placements**.

## Transport infrastructure
- During the organized tournament, the Crown deploys **temporary large-scale spatial corridors** of the type used for military long-range movements, connecting host capitals and selected dungeon locations in competition sequence.
- These are temporary event infrastructure, not a permanent new network. Ordinary caravan travel is still possible and explains Vael's training travel outside official competition.
- The route above is a conceptual ordering; exact map-based corridor paths and staging schedules await geographic review.
