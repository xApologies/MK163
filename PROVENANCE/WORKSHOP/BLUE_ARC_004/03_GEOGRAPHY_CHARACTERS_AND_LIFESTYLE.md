## Climate and feel
Mild, pleasant climate for roughly ten months of year, reminiscent of San Diego; shorter cool, potentially rainy winter period. Kaira loves climate, temperament and opportunity for occasional beach outings. Avoid translating Earth December/January literally into AERA's eleven-month calendar; seasonal mapping remains open. New city is a mature dungeon-service hub, not a primitive frontier village. Its Blue-ranked Dungeon staging district is **2×2 miles** per repository Blue outline.

## Aithren community
The repository's `DUNGEON_DELVERS_AND_STORM_CHASERS.md` distinguishes settled dungeon-delver culture from mobile Wilds storm chasers. Settled aithren community is sociable, relaxed outside the dungeon, professionally disciplined inside. People gather for abecca, raeon, discussion of Genesis Cards, Shapings, Bindings, builds, supplies, expeditions and research. The author analogizes 1980s computer-engineering curiosity, NASCAR infield conviviality and professional expedition logistics. ELDRIS are contained within dungeon chromatic strata, so surface staging community is relatively secure; this is not a claim the interior is safe. Aithren = individuals/community; vaelum = independent organizations; Accord Registry = Crown-owned accreditation/contract coordination, **not a guild**.

## Kaira's emotional transition
Blue Arc is about recovering personal freedom after five Blackstone semesters: no lecture schedule or mandatory morning studying. Royal capital is socially prestigious, nobility-heavy and not Kaira's preferred home. She is still processing her history as Afflicted and finds belonging among down-to-earth, curious dungeon workers reminiscent of childhood. She leaves not because she dislikes Aiselle, illi or E, but because she wants to choose her own life. She has real financial resources from the original robe operation but do not conflate modeled business capacity with guaranteed personal cash flow.
