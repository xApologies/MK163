# Chainmail Robe enterprise — cumulative three-city model

## Product definition
Original **five-layer interlinked chainmail robe** made of Genesis-infused iron elastic/self-closing rings, opaque, hooded, sculpted localized five→four→three relief, magnetic slit closures, standard **mass-reduction enchantment only**. Robe silhouette (not skin-tight athletic armor); may be used as meaningful physical protection by helkir, ranged support/offense specialists and backline summoners. Hyper-mobile frontline Guardians/Augmenters generally less likely to choose it. Additional compatible enchantments, e.g. damage mitigation, are independently procured by customers; Kaira **does not** sell bespoke enchantment packages. Enchantment capacity/bandwidth constrained by material/atomic structure, exact numeric capacity not fixed. Physical damage metrics, 3D weave proof, precise mass/ring count unresolved. Kaira's personally transformed chromatic robe is a distinct exceptional artifact, not the standard commercial SKU.

## Southern-city ring supply and assembly shop
- **Day 831:** three **independent ring-making blacksmith suppliers** commissioned on Blacksmiths' Row; ring production is separate from robe assembly. Ring delivery at assembly shop Day 838. Exact quotas/capacity, terms and recurring logistics remain open.
- **Day 835:** Kaira rents a simple **Yellow District** shop at **200 eln/month**, specifically suitable for six employees working at tables and storing rings. No furnace or forging setup necessary; finished rings arrive ready to weave. Work tables, chairs, tools, ring inventory, inspection/holding area.
- **Day 837:** six experienced robe-making smiths selected through Registry introductions; given shop address; begin work with Kaira Day 838.
- **Days 838–855:** six trainee robes + one Kaira demonstration robe; **seven completed by Day 856**. Kaira steps out of regular production thereafter. Six new robes every ten days targeted from Day 866 onward.
- Three ring suppliers are **not** included among six salaried assemblers; their work is a purchased material input. No double counting.
- Planning estimate **250,000 rings/robe** (not audited). Seven robes ≈1,750,000 rings initial; six per batch ≈1,500,000 rings every ten days. Supplier capacity and lead time require credible Genesis-assisted fabrication, not hand-rolling millions of rings in a week.

## Unit economics — source vs proposed extensions
- Existing MK163 wholesale price: **1,550 eln/robe** paid by Registry to Kaira's enterprise; Registry retail: **1,700 eln/robe**; retail markup **150 eln/robe**.
- Existing modeled direct costs: **850 eln rings/materials + 150 eln mass-reduction enchantment = 1,000 eln/robe** (specific other personal commissions may use student enchantment discounts; don't transfer them automatically).
- Existing modeled employee salary: **580 eln per smith per accounting month**, covering approximately three robes/month at capacity.
- Existing **original-city** Registry manager compensation: **130 eln/month**, separate from retail markup. New-city fees not finalized.
- **Southern Yellow shop rent: exactly 200 eln/month** as author-selected lease, not district average. Kaira's **Green residential district average: 600 eln/month**, specific apartment lease not fixed.

## Operational boundaries
Accord Registry is Crown-owned and handles procurement coordination, scheduling, wholesale purchases, inventory, logistics, retail sales, and professional service referrals by contract; not a vaelum or Kaira's owner. Kaira checks in occasionally, keeps craft/quality control authority, and can voluntarily make occasional robes. New Yellow shop is assembly/stock room, not a competing retail storefront. Original Green-city smiths worked from their own workshops/homes; southern six explicitly **work together in rented shop**, so do not retroactively change original arrangement.
