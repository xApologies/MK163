# King's raeon salon, challenge coin and palace chronology

**Institution:** King hosts informal raeon gathering each **Aiden**, fifth day of AERA six-day week, in Violet Domain royal capital. Nobles, generals, administrators and other officials may attend. It is a social game, not an audience or compulsory court ceremony. Raeon is distinct from Genesis Cards.

- **Day 951 (Aiden):** After Days 948–950 with illi at **Blue** capital palace, illi takes Kaira along King's Highway to Violet royal capital. illi leaves Kaira at King's table and returns near end of game three. Kaira wins **four consecutive games, 4–0**. King recognizes she is a card-game ringer; salon continues after girls depart. No fabricated private audience.
- **Day 952 (Aiveth):** On return to Blue capital, **illi**, not the King in person, gives Kaira the King's **challenge coin**. It authorizes **fare-free travel on King's Highway** and a standing invitation to fifth-day royal raeon games; it expresses King's hospitality to illi's friend. No nobility or unrestricted security access. Kaira and illi then visit staging Registry; Kaira is accepted for second expedition.
- **Day 999 (Aiden):** Kaira returns from 41-day expedition; no same-day royal salon attendance.
- **Day 1005 (Aiden):** After recovery Days 1000–1004, Kaira goes to Blue palace, learns illi is at Violet capital, uses coin to travel there and attends second salon. Plays five games, wins **four**, loses **one**. Others celebrate the player who finally beats her; winner identity OPEN.
- **Days 1006–1007:** two days with illi at **Violet** royal palace.
- **Day 1008:** Kaira travels home to Blue capital.

Superseded incorrect date: second salon formerly proposed Day 1003, which is **Aiven**, third day; corrected to Day **1005 Aiden**. Earlier first salon Day 939 and coin Day 940 become 951 and 952 under +12-day first expedition repair. Do not retain obsolete days as concurrent events.
