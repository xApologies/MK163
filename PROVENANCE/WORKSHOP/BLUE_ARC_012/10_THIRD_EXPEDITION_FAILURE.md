# Third Blue Arc expedition — Yellow cavern, failed extraction

**Selected chronology:** Applications Day 1019; two accepted and Yellow contract selected Day 1025; departure Day 1030; fatal engagement Day 1041; emergency ascent 1042–1048; surface and Crown Accord Registry closeout **Day 1049**. See `07_DAYS_1009_TO_1049_LEDGER.md` for complete dates and weekday validation.

## Team and recruitment
- Established **seven-member vaelum**: **2 Guardians, 3 Offensive Specialists, 1 combat Support Specialist, 1 Helkir**.
- **Two separately contracted environmental Support Specialists**, including Kaira, must be **Green-certified or higher** for high fatigue/endurance demands. Total **9 registered aithren**. Nyx and Black Sprite accompany Kaira, not additional registered personnel.
- Group minimizes overhead by hiring only two environmental Supports; they rotate Genesis Shield protection against ambient dungeon pressure. Combat Support is distinct from those contracts.
- Goal: protracted Yellow ELDRIS hunting for Genesis Dust plus Yellow Crystal harvesting in high-density cavern, intended **11 full harvesting days**; not a quick excursion.
- Contract: per 100g Yellow Crystal/Dust sold wholesale at 49 eln, vaelum retains 35, each of two Supports earns 7. Payment for actual recovered resources even after failed expedition; quantities OPEN.

## Catastrophic fourth harvesting day
- During Day **1041** (fourth full day of harvesting, after 1038–1040), ELDRIS overwhelm the party. **Helkir dies**. Kaira did not cause the death; she performs assigned protection and exceeds it in emergency.
- Survivors disengage rather than finish planned harvest. Kaira supplies repeated Genesis Shields for environmental and battlefield protection and uses newly completed **Genesis Beam** for controlled offensive support, stepping into a broader combat-support function. Her stamina/endurance is pivotal but not infinite.
- Black Sprite's independently learned, limited Resolution-related pseudo-healing (observed/learned from healers at Blackstone and earlier demonstrated) stabilizes injured survivors enough for escape. Not full medical Helkir capacity; cannot undo death or fully heal severe injuries. Nyx remains companion; do not invent an individual kill count.
- **Emergency seven-day ascent 1042–1048** without a Helkir, group ragged, injured, fatigued; Sprite's limited care and Kaira's shielding continue. Planned harvest days 5–11 are canceled, **not** additionally spent in cavern.
- **Day 1049:** surface arrival, Registry signoff, casualty report, contractual share for actual resources; recognition by surviving vaelum/Registry of Kaira's pivotal role in getting them out. No automatic promotion, title, formal award, specific pay, precise casualties beyond one Helkir, or invented additional deaths.

**Narrative emphasis:** Failed commercial mission with real cost; technical excellence cannot guarantee success. Kaira and Sprite recognized for adaptability and endurance, not omnipotence. Long-term effects/trauma, later contract availability, and fourth expedition OPEN.
