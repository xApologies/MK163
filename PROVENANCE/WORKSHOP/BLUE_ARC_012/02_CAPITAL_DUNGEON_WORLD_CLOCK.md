## Blue mineral-rock vaen
Rare, naturally smooth rounded/irregular pebble-like nodules, no jagged facets, varying sizes, translucent to nearly clear with some internal cloudiness, deep sapphire-blue Genesis luminescence throughout their interior with no visible localized emitter. Identity-bearing **vaen**, not ordinary Genesis Crystals. Favorable Blue oscillations can produce/grow them; no guaranteed yields, prices or predefined Shaping identity. Earlier Kaira discovered smooth green mineral vaen with moss on a return journey and retained the smallest approximately 2-inch keepsake. These are distinct specimens, not retrospectively Blue.
