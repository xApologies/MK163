# Accord Registry — Four role certification framework

## Institution and roles
- **Crown-owned Accord Registry**: records recognized individual aithren ratings, independent vaelum and their members; recruits/matches job postings and contracts, receives/validates recovered materials, pays and transfers to clients; Crown can obtain national workforce statistics. NOT a guild, vaelum, or military command.
- Vaelum are independent organizations; multi-vaelum teams and independent practitioners may contract. Vaelum may take an agreed service margin; no universal fee is set.
- Four assessed combat roles: **Guardian**, **Helkir**, **Offensive Specialist** (formal in-world title OPEN), **Support Specialist**.
- Profession/interface (Architect, Augmenter, maegi, Summoner, Cardsmith) is not role. All are Transduction practitioners. Summons count as Bindings/Shapings and may perform support or offense according to function.
- All four roles use **one 100-foot-radius / 200-foot-diameter** circular examination chamber with a **single integrated floor sigil**. Instructor activates **13 distinct trial configurations** one at a time. Helkir biological manifolds may be centered; combat scenarios use the chamber.
- ELDRIS are physically interactive recursive-topology simulations with reduced capacity compared with natural ELDRIS. Guardian trials can cause real injury or death.
- Every exam has 13 sequential stations; Genesis fatigue accumulates. **Every station must be completed for an overall certification**; aborting early means no rating.
- Station scale: Failed=0, Red=1, Orange=2, Yellow=3, Green=4, Blue=5, Violet=6, White=7. Completed overall = `floor(sum(13 station scores)/13)`. Mean below 1 => Failed. Overall White requires 13 White stations. 'Paragon' is only a working title.

1 Localized Coherence Sustainment

4 Complex System Sustainment
5 Superficial Tissue Restoration
6 Muscular Restoration
7 Skeletal Restoration
8 Vascular Restoration

12 Critical Localized Trauma

For **1–4** a fully coherent White manifold is exposed to timed Decoherence; the ring's **color at the end** of the interval is scored (not lowest transient level).
For **5–13** a damaged manifold begins with assessment ring OFF, then progresses Red→White as Coherence is restored; score the **highest achieved** at candidate's chosen stopping point. These are projected biological training manifolds; not necessarily injured live patients. Exact times/biological simulation thresholds OPEN.

## Offensive Specialist: 13 elimination stations
1–6 single stationary Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Violet ELDRIS respectively.
7–8 two escalating **static group** configurations.
9–10 two escalating **dynamic group** configurations.
11–13 three moving mixed hostile/allied engagements requiring elimination of hostiles and protection of allies.
**Score by elimination time**, fastest qualifying interval White down to Red, failed if required objective/time not achieved. Exact thresholds per test OPEN. Allied-protection requirement exists in 11–13; **exact offensive ally-injury deductions and failure conditions remain OPEN** (do not silently import Guardian rule).

## Guardian: 13 containment stations
1–6 single Red→Violet ELDRIS.
7–10 four escalating group configurations.
11–13 three allied-protection mixed scenarios.
- **Seven concentric bands from center outward:** White, Violet, Blue, Green, Yellow, Orange, Red. All hostile projections start at center.
- For 1–10 score the **farthest ring reached by ANY hostile at ANY time during active trial**, not mean or end position. Outer Red boundary escaped => Failed.
- For 11–13 combine worst containment breach with allied trauma penalties. **Any protected ally death => Failed (0)**. Severe nonfatal injury lowers the chromatic score; precise deduction algorithm OPEN. White requires perfect containment and no relevant ally injury.
- Trial duration is fixed by instructor/test, but duration is **not** the chromatic rank (earlier time-based Guardian scoring superseded).
- A suitably rated **Accord Registry-designated Helkir** can heal Guardian **between** trials; **never during an active trial**. Emergency deactivation allows immediate intervention but not successful completion credit. Major injury, limb loss, and death are possible. Healing does not automatically reset Genesis fatigue.

## Support Specialist: 13 randomized choreographed combat stations
1–6 one projected ally versus one Red→Violet ELDRIS.
7–10 two allies (**Guardian and Offensive Specialist**) versus escalating hostile groups.
11–13 full allied party versus complex moving ELDRIS; exact roster OPEN.
- Choreographies have randomized event paths, not memorized fixed scripts; engagement must complete, hostile ELDRIS defeated, and every protected ally survives.
- Candidate may use shields, barriers, terrain shaping/earthen berms, ice fields, movement repositioning, limited emergency stabilization, opportunistic Genesis beam/Fireball, and support summons. These are **examples of allowed tactical roles**, not required exact Binding list.
- Common foundational support function is a **personal shield** attached to an ally's Genesis field. Multiple shields increase operational demands.
- **Score by accumulated average group damage across protected allied manifolds**, NOT worst-injured ally. Lower average damage yields higher White→Red rating. **Any protected ally death => Failed (0)** regardless of average. Exact numerical damage brackets and sampling time (before vs after restoration) remain OPEN.
- Guardian controls hostile ELDRIS; Support Specialist **controls the battlefield** and increases allied effectiveness.
- Support may also perform environmental sustainment outside combat on contracts; certification tests focus on combat.
