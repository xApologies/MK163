# Accord Registry — Guardian examination medical protocol

**Status:** Author-locked MK163 workshop decisions, 2026-11-07.

## Core intent
The Guardian is expected to absorb or endure damage while sustaining battlefield occupation and protecting allies. The examination is a genuinely dangerous evaluation, not an intangible illusion. ELDRIS manifestations are interactive, limited projections of recursive topology; they simulate behaviors and can injure or kill the candidate, though they do not possess all capabilities of natural ELDRIS.

## Room and trials inherited unchanged
- One Crown-owned Accord Registry examination room, circular, **100-foot radius / 200-foot diameter**, with an integrated floor sigil and instructor-controlled activations. Same room is available for all four professional examinations.
- Guardian 13 trials: **six** singular ELDRIS Red through Violet (Blue is trial 05); **four** escalating group configurations; **three** engagements protecting projected allied manifolds.
- Trials occur consecutively. Accumulated Genesis fatigue remains relevant; test duration is instructor-controlled, precise intervals OPEN.

## Medical staffing and boundaries — new lock
- The Accord Registry designates an **appropriately rated Helkir** to be available throughout the Guardian examination. The minimum rank and staffing number are not yet fixed.
- **No healing during an active trial.** The candidate must sustain the engagement without outside healing.
- **Between completed trials**, medical care may be administered, including substantial repair, before the candidate proceeds. This restores injuries as the Helkir can, but is **not an automatic reset of the candidate's Genesis fatigue or professional performance**. Exact post-treatment rest and fatigue rules OPEN.
- **Emergency intervention:** The instructor can deactivate the active trial so medical treatment can begin immediately. This is an interrupted trial, not a completed successful trial. Whether interruption terminates the full attempt, permits retesting or constitutes a zero-scored completed station is OPEN; do not award a pass merely for intervention.
- Severe trauma such as loss of a limb may occur during a trial. A Guardian who finishes a trial despite such an injury may then receive healing before the next trial, assuming sufficient recovery. This is illustrative, not a required injury or guarantee of regeneration.
- A catastrophic penetrating injury, including the author's Blue ELDRIS example at trial 05, can be fatal. **Death is a possible risk**; availability of a Helkir does not guarantee survival.
- The candidate's objective is not necessarily to avoid damage: scoring concerns sustained defensive function, survival and—where applicable—protection of allied manifolds. Enduring an injury does not itself guarantee White. Exact injury thresholds and scoring metrics OPEN.

## Important source boundaries
- The user's rule that the exam can be fatal is an in-world fiction decision, not a recommendation for real-world hazardous testing.
- Do not invent universal resurrection, instant full limb restoration, immunity, mandatory catastrophic injury, unlimited healing, or fatigue reset.
- The Accord Registry is a **Crown-owned institution**, not a guild; `vaelum` are independent recognized organizations and `aithren` are individual practitioners.
- Canon spelling: **Kaira, Nyx, vaelum, aithren, helkir, vaen, ELDRIS**.
