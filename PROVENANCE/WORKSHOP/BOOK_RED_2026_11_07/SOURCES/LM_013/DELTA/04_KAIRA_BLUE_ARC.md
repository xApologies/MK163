# Kaira — Green Arc graduation and Blue Arc entry (author decisions and proposals)

## Locked motivation and circumstances
- Kaira graduates from Blackstone at the end of **Book Red, Green Arc**, trained in sigil-assisted Cardsmithing, not yet an accomplished Master Cardsmith.
- She grew up in a dungeon-oriented village hub; its culture and smithy clientele gave her a deeply **romanticized childhood aspiration to become an aithren / dungeon delver**.
- She genuinely wants to enter the community but has no clear idea where she belongs. This uncertainty is deliberate and shared by the author at the planning stage; do not preassign her a permanent role.
- Kaira moves to a **large Blue-ranked dungeon city in the Blue political domain**, relatively near Blackstone; precise distance/name OPEN.
- She has meaningful wealth and continuing earning potential from her prestigious **Chainmail Robes**. Original robes made by Kaira command luxury prices (author analogy: a designer Gucci dress), not inexpensive mass-market garments.
- Kaira personally crafts a **small integrated chainmail-ring maker's signature** on each original robe, perhaps chest, shoulder, or back of hood; exact emblem, placement and whether it varies per garment OPEN. This identifies her original craftsmanship.
- She enjoys chainmail repair and armorsmithing as intrinsic to her identity and now also offers **Cardsmithing services**.
- She has Nyx and the Black Sprite as companions. They are independent identities, not interchangeable equipment.
- She is financially capable of living comfortably in the new city, without needing to accept every job for subsistence.

## Workshop suggestions not yet author-locked
- A comfortable apartment in a 'Green district' is a floated choice, not final geography.
- A flexible repair arrangement near the dungeon or with an existing smithy rather than a full permanent shop is an assistant proposal.
- She could visit a local Accord Registry hall to see jobs, vaelum recruitment and role testing.
- A first expedition invitation, first test result, precise role choice, party composition and sequence of Blue Arc events are NOT locked.
- Narrative emphasis: she is choosing a childhood aspiration and gradually discovering what professional contributions suit her, not being forced into a hero class.
