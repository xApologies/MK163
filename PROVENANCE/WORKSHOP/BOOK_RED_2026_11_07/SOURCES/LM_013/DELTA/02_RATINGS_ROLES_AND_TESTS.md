# Crown-recognized examination framework — established and open details

## Four role categories (author selected)
1. **Guardian** (working name): front-line control, interception, corral/containment, positioning, relieving pressure from others. NOT game-like automatic aggro or magical threat generation. Often practiced by augmenters or protectors; not limited to a specific magical interface.
2. **Helkir** (protected name; healing specialist): repairs actual biological injury and trauma from combat, accidents, cuts, gashes, broken limbs and similar damage, subject to genuine capability; no assumption that every healer can restore any lost limb.
3. **Support specialist**: sustained environmental survivability, protective fields, barriers, topology reinforcement, operational utility and endurance; may use healing-like techniques to maintain people under persistent environmental pressure, but this is a support mission rather than direct trauma treatment. Multiple specialists may rotate to manage accumulated fatigue.
4. **Offensive specialist** (formal in-world title OPEN): eliminates or neutralizes threats through offensive Transduction or weapons; commonly maegi, but not restricted to them.

Roles describe **operational responsibilities**, not fixed character classes, mutually exclusive professions or four magical schools. Individual practitioners may have cross-role skills. A vaelum can specialize in only one role.

## Assessment structure
- The Accord Registry owns/maintains the tests, 13 assessed competency domains **for each of the four roles**, and records certifications and chromatic rankings.
- Scores per domain: Red=1, Orange=2, Yellow=3, Green=4, Blue=5, Violet=6, White=7.
- Equal-weight mean of 13 domain scores; **floor/truncate downward** to the corresponding integer rank: `rank = floor(sum(scores)/13)`.
- White overall requires all 13 domains White. Twelve White plus one Violet yields 90/13=6.923..., so overall Violet.
- **Paragon** is the current working title for all-White qualification; final formal nomenclature OPEN.
- An example average 5.7 produces Blue (5), not Violet.
- Overall rating is a **role-specific competency assessment**, not a universal raw-power metric.
- A proposal was floated for 13 shared headings with role-specific scenarios. The author has NOT locked the exact list of 13 domains, whether headings are shared or independent, their weights beyond current equal weighting, examination apparatus, retest protocols or safety procedures.
- Crown ratings and dungeon/domain colors are distinct concepts; avoid conflation.
