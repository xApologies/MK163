# LIVE_MODEL_012 — Complete new author decisions: Cardsmithing and architecture

## 1. Professional classification (explicitly author locked)
- **Cardsmith:** the ordinary professional Cardsmith who uses prepared physical sigils, anchored resources, a workbench-projected field, and specially crafted styluses to engineer and manufacture operational Genesis Cards.
- **Master Cardsmith:** a Cardsmith who can **natively** construct, manipulate and sustain the Genesis-field geometry necessary to produce Cards without relying on a prepared sigil's geometric scaffold.
- Do **not** introduce 'Architect-Cardsmith' as a third formal professional rank. Earlier assistant usage of that phrase is superseded by the two professional labels above.
- **Architect** describes the cross-professional native Genesis-field topology competency, which may be applied in Cardsmithing, agriculture, alchemy, enchanting, infrastructure and other fields. An Architect need not be a Cardsmith; a Master Cardsmith possesses the relevant native architectural capability.
- Architecture is a learned skill with variable proficiency. Being able to work natively does not make a practitioner automatically superior to an experienced sigil-assisted Cardsmith. Do not impose a universal quality ranking, arbitrary credential hierarchy or fixed skill grades.

## 2. Biological access versus competency
- Kaira's extraordinary White Awakening potion gave her the neural Genesis biology/channels needed for eventual native field manipulation.
- Genesis Potions may develop or enhance the channels that make native architectural work possible. The presence of those channels alone does not grant conceptual knowledge, practiced field manipulation, architectural competency, or mastery.
- Three distinct factors: **biological capacity**, **mathematical/conceptual understanding**, **practiced control/mastery**.
- Kaira starts with assisted sigil/stylus Cardsmithing; her education and technical intuition develop first. Across later books she gradually learns to interact with the Genesis Field natively and progresses toward Master Cardsmithing. No exact book or chapter for the transition is selected.
- She may mathematically understand a geometry before she can sustain it natively. Her Black bonds and native Genesis growth are not shortcuts to instant mastery.
- A novice Architect may produce a less effective Card than a highly experienced sigil-assisted Cardsmith.

## 3. Workbench design — author-supplied drawing
- Large physical sigil inscribed on a workbench, composed of `ru’ne`, with a **central violet-colored Card anchor** and six surrounding chromatic resource anchor locations, Red through Violet.
- The central violet-colored position identifies the Card's workbench anchor; **it does not establish that every Card must draw Violet-basin energy**.
- Blank **maege-glass** Card sits in the center. The card substrate permits persistent adherence/encoding of engineered Genesis topology.
- Other anchors accept selected `vaen`, Genesis crystals, Genesis dust, and other suitable resources. Not every Card requires every chromatic resource.
- The workbench's sigil provides a generic geometric scaffold and maintains its field during manufacture. Activation projects the coupled Genesis-field topology/manifold associated with the Card.
- This projection is coupled to the Card itself, not a detached duplicate or free-standing automatic editor.
- Cardsmith uses **two specially crafted styluses**, one in each hand if desired, to interact with the projected topology: drawing connections, moving/adjusting relationships, and completing the manifold. Their detailed construction, materials and ergonomics are not yet fixed.
- The practitioner commands/initiates stabilization once the intended connections are complete. The sigil helps resolve/commit the configuration and resources into the Card's persistent Genesis identity.
- The sigil is deactivated; the projected manufacturing field collapses/disengages while the Card retains its encoded configuration.
- The apparatus is analogous in function to a miniature prepared Binding Circle in its geometric scaffold, anchored resources and resolution of an identity, **without** equating manufacture with installing a personal Binding or creating a new universal Transduction interface.
- This method allows efficient repeatable, general-purpose production (e.g. ordinary Fireball Cards) while preserving the possibility of sophisticated sigil-manufactured Cards using excellent sigils and exceptional `vaen`.

## 4. Direct native method — Master Cardsmith
- A trained native Architect constructs the local Genesis-field topology personally, sustains its geometry, forms all required connections, and resolves the engineered configuration into the maege-glass Card.
- No mandatory prepared physical sigil, workbench projection or sigil-resource recipe. The absence of a prepared recipe is **not** freedom from energy, fatigue, admissibility, concentration, material substrate, or failure.
- Direct construction is unusually labor intensive, difficult and less standardized, but permits more individual, non-template geometry and potentially unique, very advanced Cards.
- Direct native work is not inherently better than every sigil-assisted product; quality depends on experience, concept, engineering, resources and admissibility.
- A tentative price illustration was ~**2.3×** for an individually Architect-crafted Card compared with a comparable sigil-made Card. This is a *working example*, not a universal fixed price multiplier or locked economy.

## 5. Architects throughout the story
- **Agriculture:** an Architect may form and sustain a temporary `velis` to support enhanced crop growth.
- **Alchemy:** an Architect may sustain Genesis-field topology for specialized alchemical processes.
- **Cardsmithing:** native construction of encoded Card geometry is Master Cardsmithing.
- **Enchanting:** direct manipulation of object-local Genesis identity.
- **Infrastructure:** civic barriers, field stabilization, utilities, growth, transport and other local architectural systems.
- These are applications of a transferable skill, not necessarily separate species-native realms or new universal magical schools.
- `velis` is a temporary local Genesis-field topology, not an ordinary physical `ru’ne` or sigil.

## 6. Kaira's narrative trajectory
- White Awakening makes the biological route to native architecture possible.
- Blackstone formal education: sigil-assisted Card engineering, stylus manipulation, mathematics, resource and manifold knowledge.
- Book Red adventuring: Kaira's principal professional identity is Cardsmith/card wielder, not an already accomplished direct-field Architect or conventional healer. She has a working Fireball Card, a planned Green Resolution support/healing Card, and a candidate raw Genesis beam Card; exact encoding/compatibility, basin allocation and manufacturing specifics remain open.
- Long-term: progressive native architectural proficiency, eventual Master Cardsmithing, possibly unique complex Cards that ordinary generic sigils cannot readily manufacture.
- Her Black Sprite bond, Nyx, and future realm bonds continue as independent sentient relationships; no retired special Black Card subsystem is reactivated.
- No exact date/book of Master Cardsmith attainment selected.
