# Accord Registry — Offensive Specialist Examination

**Status:** MK163 author-locked testing framework. Crown-owned Accord Registry administers the examination. The formal in-world title for the offensive specialist remains OPEN.

## Examination chamber
- **Circular room**, radius **100 feet**, diameter **200 feet**.
- Floor area: `10,000π square feet` ≈ **31,415.93 ft²** (≈2,918.64 m²).
- **Entire floor is an integrated sigil** capable of projecting controlled field manifolds representing ELDRIS and, for later stations, allied personnel.
- The instructor/tester **activates the room one test at a time**. Each station is a distinct **activation configuration of the same floor sigil**, rather than requiring thirteen separate rooms or independent physical sigils.
- Instructor activates **Test 1**, candidate completes its objective, instructor activates **Test 2**, and so forth through **Test 13**.
- The projected manifolds simulate hostile configurations and allied presence; they are examination constructs, not actual living ELDRIS or people. No ungrounded assumptions about their sentience, physical danger, or biological equivalence.
- Room height, control console, protective barriers, activation reset timings, target motion profiles and objective measurement apparatus remain OPEN.

## Thirteen tests — exactly three categories

### Category I — Six individual ELDRIS configurations (Tests 1–6)
Candidate must damage/disrupt/destroy one projected ELDRIS manifold per station, progressing from Red through Violet.
1. **Red ELDRIS** — one individual Red configuration.
2. **Orange ELDRIS** — one individual Orange configuration.
3. **Yellow ELDRIS** — one individual Yellow configuration.
4. **Green ELDRIS** — one individual Green configuration.
5. **Blue ELDRIS** — one individual Blue configuration.
6. **Violet ELDRIS** — one individual Violet configuration.

### Category II — Four ELDRIS group configurations (Tests 7–10)
7. **Static Group I** — stationary multiple ELDRIS, group composition to specify.
8. **Static Group II** — another stationary ELDRIS grouping, potentially mixed chromatic configurations; exact composition OPEN.
9. **Dynamic Group I** — multiple ELDRIS manifolds move in a controlled scenario.
10. **Dynamic Group II** — another moving ELDRIS group with a different configuration; exact movement/composition OPEN.

The group compositions draw from Red–Violet ELDRIS configurations. **Exactly two static group tests and two dynamic group tests.** No allied manifolds are required in Category II.

### Category III — Three dynamic mixed allied/ELDRIS configurations (Tests 11–13)
11. **Allied Interposition** — dynamic hostile manifolds plus projected allied/Guardian manifold(s); neutralize ELDRIS while avoiding allied damage.
12. **Dynamic Battlefield** — moving hostiles and allies; maintain target discrimination and effective offense.
13. **Complex Allied Engagement** — complex simultaneous movement of hostile and allied manifolds; neutralize hostile topology without injuring allies.

The primary competency here is **offensive effectiveness with ally preservation**. The projected allied/Guardian manifolds represent friendly personnel, not targets. The exact number, motion and placement of allies/hostiles are OPEN.

## Examination operation and scoring inherited from Accord Registry
- Candidate takes **13 consecutive tests**; fatigue accumulates across them, rather than resetting for each activation.
- Station scoring is **Failed=0; Red=1; Orange=2; Yellow=3; Green=4; Blue=5; Violet=6; White=7**.
- After completing all thirteen, overall rank is `floor(sum(13 station scores)/13)`; result below 1 is Failed. Overall White requires thirteen White scores; **Paragon** remains a working designation.
- If fatigue or another condition prevents the candidate from **completing all thirteen**, **no certification rating** is issued for the attempt.
- Candidate can intentionally conserve fatigue by accepting lower performance, but exact per-station timing, thresholds, target destruction criterion and the consequences of allied damage are NOT YET AUTHOR-LOCKED.
- Do **not** automatically assign a White score merely for destroying all hostiles if allied manifolds were damaged. Exact penalties/caps/automatic-failure conditions remain OPEN.
- Ratings assess **role-specific operational competency**, not a universal ranking of magical power or specific Binding class.
- Offensive specialists may use suitable native Transduction, Bindings, Cards or Summoning, consistent with their actual capabilities and interface constraints. The test does not mandate maegi-only participants.

## Purpose
The first six stations evaluate individual-target offensive capacity against the full Red–Violet ELDRIS spectrum; the next four evaluate multi-target capability and movement; the final three evaluate ally-safe offense in a dynamic team environment. Together the thirteen stages assess offensive capability suitable for real dungeon expeditions, not just raw damage output.
