# Accord Registry — Offensive Specialist Examination

## Universal chamber
- Crown-owned Accord Registry examination institution.
- One circular room, radius **100 feet**, diameter **200 feet**, with a full-floor integrated sigil.
- Instructor/tester activates the room's 13 configurations individually, in order.

- The room serves all four roles; the configurations differ by professional examination.

## Author-locked station rating rule
- **Elapsed elimination time is the primary chromatic scoring measure.** The faster the candidate completes the specified hostile elimination objective, the higher the station score.
- Per-station qualifying time brackets run **White (7), Violet (6), Blue (5), Green (4), Yellow (3), Orange (2), Red (1)**, then **Failed (0)** for exceeding the permitted time or failing essential requirements.
- The exact qualifying intervals and maximum time may vary with scenario difficulty; **none have been specified**. Do not invent universal seconds/minutes or infer that a Red ELDRIS requires the same time as Violet.
- Category III has an independent allied-protection condition: rapid elimination does not automatically excuse allied harm. Exact offensive allied-damage penalty or disqualification thresholds are OPEN; do not import the Guardian's newer mandatory ally-death failure rule without author decision.
- Candidates may use admissible offensive techniques through any permitted interface; no particular Binding or maegi class is compulsory.
- Thirteen trials are consecutive; fatigue accumulates across the entire examination.
- All thirteen must be completed for an overall certification rating. Failure to finish the sequence means **no rating**.
- Completed rating: `floor((s1 + ... + s13) / 13)`, with station scores 0–7. Mean below 1 => Failed; White overall requires all 13 White scores. 'Paragon' remains a working title.
- This scoring is **distinct** from Guardian containment/displacement scoring and Helkir biological Coherence scoring.
