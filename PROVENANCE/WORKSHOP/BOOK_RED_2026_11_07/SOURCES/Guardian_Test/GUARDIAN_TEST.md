# Accord Registry — Guardian Certification Examination

## Universal shared chamber (newly locked)
- The same **single circular chamber** serves all **four** role examinations: Helkir, Offensive Specialist, Guardian, and Support.
- **Radius: 100 feet; diameter: 200 feet; area: 10,000π ≈ 31,416 square feet (approximately 2,919 square meters).**
- The entire floor is an integrated physical sigil array. An instructor/tester activates the desired configuration for each of the **13 tests** in sequence; no need for thirteen different physical rooms.
- **Helkir** tests primarily use a biological manifold projection in the chamber's center, including timed sustainment and damaged-manifold repair.
- **Offensive Specialist** tests use the full room for ELDRIS elimination, groups, dynamic movement, and avoidance of allied damage.
- **Guardian** tests use the full room for occupation, endurance against hostile attacks, threat control, and protection of allies.
- **Support** shares the room but its thirteen activation configurations have **not yet been designed**.
- Ceiling height, spectator/examiner station, physical construction, and containment/safety mechanisms remain OPEN.

## Projected ELDRIS ontology and physical consequences
- Projected ELDRIS are **interactive simulations/realizations of recursive ELDRIS topology**, not mere light images or harmless illusions.
- Their projected configurations reproduce relevant ELDRIS behavior and attacks but **do not possess the full capabilities of natural ELDRIS**.
- **Projected attacks can actually injure the candidate**. Guardian certification thus measures genuine survivability and control, with real risk. Specific medical intervention, abort thresholds and safety protocols are OPEN.
- Projected allied manifolds represent the people the Guardian must protect. They are not automatically invulnerable.

## Purpose of Guardian role
- The Guardian is the front-line professional responsible for **occupying and controlling the battlefield**, intercepting and containing hostile ELDRIS, managing movement and positioning, sustaining hostile attacks, and relieving pressure from healers, support personnel and offensive specialists.
- This is **not** MMO-style automatic 'aggro' or supernatural forced monster targeting. A Guardian must achieve protection through actual positioning, interception, control, and admissible Shaping/physical tactics.
- A Guardian may use augmentation, barriers, native Transduction, equipment, and other appropriate methods. The examination tests demonstrated role performance, not a mandated class, profession or spell list.
- Guardian is the author's **working role title**; final in-world nomenclature remains OPEN.

## Thirteen consecutive Guardian tests

### Category I — Six individual ELDRIS sustainment trials
1. **Red ELDRIS:** Sustain/occupy a single projected Red ELDRIS's attacks for the prescribed interval.
2. **Orange ELDRIS:** Sustain/occupy a single projected Orange ELDRIS.
3. **Yellow ELDRIS:** Sustain/occupy a single projected Yellow ELDRIS.
4. **Green ELDRIS:** Sustain/occupy a single projected Green ELDRIS.
5. **Blue ELDRIS:** Sustain/occupy a single projected Blue ELDRIS.
6. **Violet ELDRIS:** Sustain/occupy a single projected Violet ELDRIS.

### Category II — Four escalating ELDRIS group trials
7. **Group Configuration I:** Sustain and control attacks from a relatively simple group of projected ELDRIS.
8. **Group Configuration II:** Sustain against a more demanding mixed-composition group.
9. **Group Configuration III:** Sustain against escalating multi-ELDRIS attacks and pressure.
10. **Group Configuration IV:** Sustain against a complex coordinated group configuration.

The author has selected **four escalating group compositions**; precise ranks, numbers, motion paths and compositions are not fixed. Do not silently import the Offensive Specialist's 'two static, two dynamic' substructure as a locked Guardian rule.

### Category III — Three allied-protection trials
11. **Allied Defense I:** Control hostile ELDRIS and protect a projected ally from attacks.
12. **Allied Defense II:** Protect multiple allied manifolds while controlling several ELDRIS.
13. **Complex Battlefield Protection:** Sustain threat control and preserve allied manifolds through a complex engagement.

The Guardian is not graded solely on self-survival in Category III. A candidate who remains unharmed while allies are destroyed has not fulfilled the role objective.

## Activation, timing, fatigue and rating
- Instructor activates each of the 13 chamber configurations individually, in order.
- Guardian scenarios involve **timed sustainment intervals**; author examples include **five or fifteen minutes**, but no universal duration is selected.
- All thirteen trials are consecutive and **Genesis fatigue carries forward**. Candidate cannot treat each trial as a fresh reserve.
- The shared Accord Registry station scale is **Failed=0; Red=1; Orange=2; Yellow=3; Green=4; Blue=5; Violet=6; White=7**.
- A completed thirteen-trial examination's overall rating uses `floor(sum(station_scores)/13)`; mean below one is Failed. White overall requires all thirteen White; Paragon is a working label.
- **Incomplete exam: no certification rating**, even if earlier stations scored well.
- Exact Guardian scoring weights/thresholds for injury, survival, control, allied preservation, elapsed time, and role performance remain OPEN. Do not assume that a Guardian is graded on destroying ELDRIS.

## Shared institutional context
- The Accord Registry is a **Crown-owned institution**, not a guild. It maintains individual qualifications, vaelum recognition, contracts and recruitment, and analytical reporting to the Crown on registered adventuring capacity.
- **vaelum** are independent professional organizations; **aithren** are participating individuals. A vaelum is not synonymous with a temporary expedition party.
- The Guardian examination is one of four role-specific 13-station certifications; the other three are Helkir, Offensive Specialist, and Support.
