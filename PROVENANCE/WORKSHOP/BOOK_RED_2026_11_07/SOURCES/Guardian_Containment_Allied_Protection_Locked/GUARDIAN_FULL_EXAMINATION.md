# Accord Registry — Guardian Certification: Complete Current Workshop Specification

## Chamber and apparatus
- Shared Accord Registry circular examination chamber: radius 100 ft; diameter 200 ft; approximately 31,416 sq ft floor.
- The full floor is a sigil array; instructor activates one of 13 scenario configurations in sequence.
- The ELDRIS are **interactive projected recursive-topology manifestations**, behaving as controlled simulations of actual ELDRIS. They lack the full capacities of natural ELDRIS but **can physically injure or kill** the Guardian.
- Candidates may use native Transduction, Bindings, equipment, augmentation, and other admissible methods; there is no artificial MMO 'aggro' mechanism.
- The Guardian's function is **occupying and controlling the battlefield**, preventing hostile manifolds from breaking containment or damaging allies. Eliminating enemies is not the primary assessment objective.

## Category II — Four group configurations
7. ELDRIS group composition I — containment of several hostiles.
8. ELDRIS group composition II — more demanding mixed group containment.
9. ELDRIS group composition III — escalating/moving group pressure.
10. ELDRIS group composition IV — complex group containment.

The exact membership and behavior of each group are OPEN; above difficulty descriptors are workshop characterizations, not fixed numeric encounters.

## Category III — Three allied-protection configurations
11. Hostile ELDRIS with projected ally/ies — contain hostiles and protect allies.
12. More complex hostile/allied engagement — contain hostiles and protect allies.
13. Complex mixed moving engagement — contain hostiles and protect allies.

All 13 configurations are consecutive; the trial's instructor controls activation and timing. Fatigue persists across the sequence. Exact trial duration is OPEN (five or fifteen minutes were examples, not canon).

## Locked chromatic spatial containment measurement
- The circular arena has **seven concentric containment bands**, from the center outward:
  **White (7) → Violet (6) → Blue (5) → Green (4) → Yellow (3) → Orange (2) → Red (1)**.
- All projected hostile ELDRIS begin in the central containment region.
- During each trial, the apparatus tracks **the outermost ring reached by ANY hostile ELDRIS manifold**. This is the **maximum radial displacement/breach over the active trial**, not the average or final location.
- For tests **1–10**, this worst outward breach determines the station score.
- Examples: all hostiles remain in White => White; a single hostile reaches Violet => Violet; one reaches Green => Green, regardless of where the others remain.
- If a hostile escapes beyond the outer Red boundary, or essential completion requirements fail, station outcome is **Failed (0)**.
- Exact radial widths of the bands are OPEN. Do not assume equal band widths or assign feet-per-color.
- The candidate can be injured yet score White if the required containment is maintained; personal injury does not automatically reduce the score.
- The earlier proposed time-threshold Guardian score is **superseded**. Trial intervals still establish the period over which containment must be maintained.

## Category III — Locked combined protection measurement
- Tests **11–13** measure **BOTH** containment (the farthest chromatic ring breached by any ELDRIS) **AND** damage to allied projected manifolds.
- The Guardian's spatial containment score provides a ceiling; allied damage **reduces the station rating further** as appropriate.
- Example: hostiles contained no farther than Green would yield Green on containment alone, but severe trauma to a projected ally lowers the score **below Green**.
- **If ANY protected allied manifold dies/is destroyed, the entire station is Failed (0)**, regardless of how well other ELDRIS were contained.
- An injured ally does not automatically fail the station; severity matters and is reflected as a score reduction.
- Exact chromatic penalty amounts and how simulated ally trauma maps to them are OPEN; do not invent a fixed one-rank or multi-rank deduction, percentages, or averaging rule.
- An all-White result in Category III requires both White containment and no relevant allied injury.
- The station's score is still a single integer 0–7, used in the same 13-score average as all other tests.

## Guardian medical protocol — locked
- A suitably rated **Accord Registry-designated helkir** is available to provide treatment between Guardian trials and to intervene after emergency deactivation.
- **No healing is permitted during an active Guardian trial.**
- A Guardian may sustain major trauma, including loss of a limb, and complete a trial; they may be healed afterward before proceeding.
- The instructor can deactivate a trial for emergency medical treatment; this does **not** retroactively count as a successfully completed trial.
- The trials carry real danger. Severe injury or death is possible; emergency response is not guaranteed to save a candidate.
- Treatment between trials does **not** automatically reset accumulated Genesis fatigue.

## Overall rating
- Station outcomes: Failed=0; Red=1; Orange=2; Yellow=3; Green=4; Blue=5; Violet=6; White=7.
- All 13 stations must be completed for certification. Incomplete examination => **no overall rating**, even if earlier scores were high.
- If completed: `overall = floor(sum(station scores)/13)`. A mean below 1 => Failed.
- Overall White requires all 13 White station scores. 'Paragon' is the working title for an all-White candidate.
- Do not conflate station color with ELDRIS chromatic rank: Red-ranked ELDRIS can be contained at White score.
