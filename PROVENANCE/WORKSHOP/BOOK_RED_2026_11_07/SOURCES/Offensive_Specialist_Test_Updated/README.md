# Offensive Specialist Test — Scoring Update

**Status:** Author-selected scoring amendment to the 13-test Offensive Specialist examination.

**Cumulative preservation:** This archive embeds `Guardian_Test_Locked.zip` byte-for-byte, which embeds Guardian_Test.zip, Offensive_Specialist_Test.zip, Healthcare_Test.zip, and earlier MK163 LIVE_MODEL archives. The original Offensive Specialist test and Guardian medical protocol remain intact. This is an additive supersession, not a rewrite of earlier historical files.

## New rule — Offensive Specialist rating is time-based
Each of the 13 instructor-activated test configurations measures how quickly the candidate eliminates the designated projected ELDRIS targets. The quicker the successful elimination, the higher the chromatic station score: White (7) fastest qualifying bracket, then Violet (6), Blue (5), Green (4), Yellow (3), Orange (2), Red (1), Failed (0) if the objective is not completed within the allowed limit or fails essential conditions. **Exact time brackets are OPEN and may vary by test.** Do not invent universal seconds or minutes.

Category I: six individual ELDRIS, Red to Violet. Category II: four group configurations, first two static, remaining two dynamic. Category III: three moving engagements with ELDRIS and allied projected manifolds. For Category III, elimination speed alone is insufficient: the candidate must meet allied-preservation constraints. Exact damage tolerance/penalties remain OPEN.

All 13 trials remain consecutive with cumulative Genesis fatigue; failure to finish the full sequence means no certification rating. On completion, overall rank = floor(sum of 13 station scores / 13), 0=Failed, 1–7 Red–White. White overall requires 13 White station scores; Paragon is a working title.

## Guardian contrast — discussion, not yet an independently finalized Guardian scoring amendment
The author wants Guardian Categories I and II (tests 1–10) scored by an objective way to sustain/control individual and grouped ELDRIS, and Category III (tests 11–13) scored by damage to projected allied manifolds. The assistant proposed **duration of successful containment** for Categories I–II, with injuries not inherently deducting points, and **amount of allied manifold damage** for Category III. This proposal has not yet been explicitly author-locked. Time brackets, duration limits, containment boundaries, and injury/death contingencies remain OPEN. A prior illustrative example inconsistently called a seven-minute scale a five-minute trial; that example is NOT canon.

## Existing Guardian medical protocol preserved
A designated rated Registry Helkir is available between trials and for emergency deactivation. No healing during active Guardian trials. Candidate may be injured or killed by interactive ELDRIS projections; intervention is not guaranteed to prevent death. Treating injury between trials does not automatically reset accumulated Genesis fatigue.
