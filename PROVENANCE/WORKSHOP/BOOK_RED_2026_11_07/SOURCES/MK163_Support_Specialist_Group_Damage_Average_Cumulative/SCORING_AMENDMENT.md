# Author-Locked Scoring Amendment — Accumulated Average Group Damage

**Supersedes:** The Support Specialist's prior worst-injured-ally scoring rule in the preceding cumulative checkpoint. The old archive is retained unchanged for provenance.

**Current rule:** Each of the 13 choreographed combat stations grades the Support candidate on the **accumulated average damage sustained by the protected allied group**. All protected allies contribute to the aggregate, and the score reflects group-wide protection rather than the single most injured survivor. Lower group damage earns higher chromatic grades, White (7) down to Red (1).

**Absolute survival condition:** If **any** protected allied manifold dies, the station is **Failed (0)**, irrespective of how low the group's average damage was. Failure to complete the required engagement also fails the station.

**Examples:** In a seven-person group, one severely injured but surviving ally and six uninjured allies do not automatically force a Red score; the group average governs. If that severely injured ally dies, the station is Failed regardless of the other six. In a one-ally station, the group average equals that ally's damage.

**Overall certificate:** Each completed station yields a single 0–7 chromatic score. All 13 stations must be completed to receive an overall rating. For a completed exam, `floor(sum(station scores)/13)` determines overall rank; White overall requires thirteen White stations. The group average is **within each station**; the established average of station scores remains the overall certification calculation. Do not conflate these two levels.

**Still open:** Exact injury/damage measurement, normalization, whether 'accumulated' refers to cumulative damage events versus end-state trauma, mapping from mean damage to color, and any unequal ally weighting. Do not invent numerical thresholds.

**Other doctrine preserved:** Thirteen randomized choreographed engagements, full combat completion, any ally death fails the station, cumulative Genesis fatigue, same 100-ft-radius chamber, Kaira's intended combat-support role, no mandated single technique.
