# Kaira, Black Sprite, Nyx — Book Red Original Trio

## Kaira

- Canonical name **Kaira**; dictated Kira is corrected on integration.
- **23 local years** at opening (verified in `MK163/WORLD_CLOCK/L3_CHARACTERS_EVENTS/CHARACTERS/KAIRA.md`). Born Afflicted, extraordinary White-heavy Awakening; healthy white hair/skin, distinctive eyes/scars. Her appearance attracts inaccurate noble-birth rumors, not a true noble ancestry.
- Early Orange: independent armorsmith, inventor of chainmail shirt and five-layer Chainmail Robe, future face of movement eventually named Age of Knowledge (the historical name is retrospective, not Day Zero's contemporary label).
- Green Arc: Alchemist-sponsored formal **Cardsmith** education at Blackstone, conceptual mathematics and technical practice; not yet as skilled in native Genesis expression as princess.
- Black Singularity: exceptional preexisting affinity, growing through independent bonds and reciprocal amplification. Her bond to Sprite is not a Card or a conventional Binding installation.
- Initial dungeon contribution: **technical support** and conceptual experimentation, not immediate conventional frontline combat or full Vaelith-style healer. Native aen competence develops gradually. Potential later versatility does not imply present mastery.

## Black Sprite (they/them)

- First Black bond in Book Red, Yellow Arc. Green Realm; exceptional Black recursion; very young and innately stronger potential than ordinary Sprites. No assigned gender, use **they/them** consistently.
- Original form: little dark recursive wisp with internal full-spectrum neon and intermittent chromatic leakage. Not a literal hot flame.
- Selected ability: assume larger humanoid-shaped **recursive** body; visibly nonhuman, no full human biology or disguise. Favors it for physical interaction and early melee. Robust, not invulnerable.
- Native telepathy: mind-to-mind with Kaira, occasional translation of Nyx, perception of other people's intentions/thoughts. This is a natural Sprite ability and can be relatively easy despite immature advanced magic. Interpretation is not perfect; exact range/privacy/resistance open.
- Early magic: disrupt permitted ELDRIS energy configurations (possible loss of Genesis dust), reinforce admissible energy configurations, amplify effects; far-future unusual manifestations remain open.
- Learns from Kaira's concept and mathematics, while Kaira learns from Sprite's native possibility intuition. Reciprocal growth, not omniscient tutor or installed spell engine.

## Nyx (male in conversation)

- Ordinary species of **Green Realm-native feline**, exceptional individual, **not Sprite, not Black bond**. The name is Nyx, dictated Nix corrected.
- Rescued on Kaira's **third return journey** from the new Violet dungeon settlement, in orange-flowered cave near dead mother; repository account specifies parents' deaths, native flower sustenance and rapid growth. Do not move rescue to first settlement visit.
- Species sustains through natural Genesis-energy/light-collecting flowers; not meat predation. Nyx's flowers are an example of his native Green Realm expression; not his complete repertoire.
- By Kaira's entry to Blackstone Nyx is **rideable**. Large, agile, hyper-mobile, frontline/pseudo-tank. Exceptional within species but immature and not omnipotent.
- Sprite can sometimes translate his thoughts/intentions, not necessarily literal human-language sentences.

## Initial party interaction

`Black Sprite: robust humanoid melee + immature unconventional Green energy realization`  
`Nyx: mobile feline frontline/tank + developing native Green expression`  
`Kaira: Cardsmith/armorsmith technical support + learning native Transduction`

The party is initially more physical/hack-and-slash than magical, not because Green is weak, but because all three are young and untrained relative to their exceptional capacities. Their independent identities and consent/agency must be preserved.
