# Blue / Violet Arc Handoff — Deliberately Open

## What the author wants to do next

Workshop Kaira's entry into the **multifaceted adventuring community** and her gradual progression toward King's Tournament. **Do not** start with "Kaira becomes a combatant" or automatically assign Blue and Violet to fixed training/tournament-prep arcs.

### Dungeon community

- Research parties study native environmental `ru’ne` and natural Genesis structures across generations.
- Harvest expeditions seek Genesis dust, Genesis crystals, `vaen` and other materials.
- Hired guards and combat specialists protect researchers/harvesters.
- Healers, sustainers and other specialists mitigate dangerous environmental pressures, particularly deeper Green/Blue/Violet strata.
- Technical workers, armorers, Cardsmiths and expedition support also belong to the community.
- Dedicated combat delvers are one possible occupation, **not the definition of adventurer**.
- Social culture: accepting, playful and welcoming to outsiders including Afflicted; serious about dangers and livelihoods. Kaira has historical affinity for this culture through work in an earlier dungeon village hub.

### Kaira's present assets

- Formal Blackstone Cardsmith education; master chainmail craftsmanship and fashion/knowledge movement identity.
- Young Black Sprite bond, exceptional but immature; early telepathy, robust humanoid melee, limited energetic disruption/reinforcement and magical amplification.
- Nyx rideable at Blackstone, high-mobility Green-native feline with flower energy sustenance, physical frontline capability and emerging native expression.
- Princess friend (native Genesis prodigy, royal social standing) and Alchemist older-sister friend (social/business access and mentorship).
- Kaira has not yet gained Black Vaelith healing specialization; that occurs in **Book Orange**. Sprite energy reinforcement may have healing-like results by distinct Resolution geometry.
- The trio is capable but novice as an expedition group. They may defeat an ELDRIS through Sprite disruption and upset resource harvesters by reducing recoverable dust. This is an **illustrative encounter premise**, not a written scene.

### Decisions not yet made

- Does Kaira begin as researcher, resource specialist, armorer, technical support, guard-adjacent support, or another hybrid? No forced class yet.
- What exact Sprite manifestations are reliable, and under what conditions?
- What are the concrete protective/healing-like effects of Resolution-level energy reinforcement?
- How is Sprite telepathy limited, and how does it affect ethical/social relationships?
- What are the first paid dungeon engagements, where do they occur, and what goes wrong?
- How does the three-being party integrate with other adventurers?
- What events belong specifically in Blue versus Violet?
- Who joins the eventual tournament team, and when?
- What is the precise tournament calendar, qualifying structure and event sequence?

### Design principle

Start with **community and capability**, then choose **story events**. Do not infer a conventional class, combat proficiency, full healing, unlimited Sprite powers, new bond, or Dimensional Ring before White.
