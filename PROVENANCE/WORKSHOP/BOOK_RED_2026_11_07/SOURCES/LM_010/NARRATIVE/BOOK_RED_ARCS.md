## Red Arc 1

Genesis delivery disaster and collapse; rare White-heavy Awakening potion at Stone Hollow; departure/caravan to a large city in **Green royal domain**. Source `BOOKS/RED/ARCS/ARC_001/ARC.md`. ## Orange Arc 2

Leases modest apartment in Orange district of Green-domain city (110 eln/month). Plays raeon, buys ordinary General Metal Manipulation Genesis Card, befriends talented mailwright, contracts 300,000 Genesis-infused self-closing iron rings for 1000 eln. Creates mass-reduced Offset-Lattice chainmail demonstration shirt (~23–25k rings) over eight days from pickup; develops five-layer Chainmail Robe, completed/revealed at arc's end. First trip to **new** Violet dungeon settlement ~five miles NW, with shirt in backpack; first collision with arrogant Blue-ranked nobleman's son outside armorsmith shop. Kaira is NOT already known to customers there. Recurring visits for armor commissions and gathering botanical materials/`vaen`; **Nyx rescued on third RETURN**. Kaira's early dark dermal Red `aen` dot is unstable, not a mastered ring. The full Orange Arc outline is not completed here.

## Yellow Arc 3

Chainmail Robe movement expands, imitations appear, cultural transition toward later-named Age of Knowledge through deeper conceptual understanding. Alchemist (globally respected botanical genius/vintner) arrives at Kaira's door insisting on an **original, uniquely commissioned robe**. Their relationship deepens into best friendship/older-sister mentorship. Kaira forms her first **exceptional Black Sprite identity bond** from hidden crystal (not an installed Genesis Card). Exact scene sequencing and Black Sprite's first manifestations OPEN. At arc close, Alchemist arranges/sponsors Kaira's Blackstone admission, tuition and campus housing. The Age of Knowledge need not be called that at Day Zero.

## Green Arc 4

Kaira attends premier Blackstone for formal Cardsmith education. Princess (youngest daughter, 27, non-governing People's Princess) is concurrently an advanced-track Transduction/mathematics student after elite tutoring. Alchemist facilitates introduction. Kaira and princess quickly become extremely close; Alchemist/Kaira bond continues. Sprite and Nyx grow alongside Kaira; **Nyx already rideable by Blackstone entry**. Kaira leaves with substantial Cardsmith expertise but not yet princess's native-Genesis proficiency. Duration/curriculum/qualifications OPEN.
