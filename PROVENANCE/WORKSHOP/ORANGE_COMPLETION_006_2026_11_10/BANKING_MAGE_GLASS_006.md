# MK163 — Banking, Fixed-Manifold Mage Glass Cards and Paper Ledgers

**Author-established system; first account opened early Orange, working Day -1.** Banking uses Genesis Card/sigil infrastructure for **identification/verification** and wholly **handwritten records** for balances and custody. No computers, electronic databases, government-issued photo IDs, Crown identity cards, credit-card network or magic financial ledger.

1. A bank clerk creates a numbered **paper account record**. It tracks available **eln**, stored **Genesis crystals** (inventory/quality and custody), stored **Genesis Dust**, optional assigned lockbox and handwritten transaction history. Crystals in custody remain the customer's property unless separately exchanged/sold; deposits need not be automatically converted into eln. Exact fees/interest/transfer rules remain open.
2. A blank **Mage Glass banking card**, effectively a Genesis Card with a fixed local manifold, is activated/assigned to that account through a bank sigil. It carries no balance or valuables. On placement/activation at a bank counter sigil, a **luminous numerical account reference** projects above/near the sigil (e.g. `0113872` is illustrative ONLY). The bank checks the registered card verification, not merely publicly readable digits. The projection is a local mathematical realization, not a computer display.
3. The clerk says they'll retrieve the record, goes into the archive/back room, fetches the indexed binder/notebook and reads the customer's account status. Transactions are performed at the counter and immediately recorded by hand. Banking technology does not itself maintain or synchronize ledgers.
4. Account creation includes a **private recovery phrase** and optionally a customer-held account certificate. If the card is lost, bank records and phrase allow verification, revocation of old card and issue of a replacement fixed-manifold card referencing the **same account**. If card, certificate and phrase are all lost, bank uses independent witnesses, corroborated transaction details, further bank review, and may deny access until identity is adequately established. This is an exceptional/manual procedure, not guaranteed instant recovery. Account number alone is never sufficient proof.
5. Cross-branch availability, exact uniqueness/security math of manifold, tamper resistance, physical archive protection, ledger replication, privacy and transaction approval policy remain open. Do not imply that two manifolds yielding same visible number are automatically authenticated as the same registered credential.
