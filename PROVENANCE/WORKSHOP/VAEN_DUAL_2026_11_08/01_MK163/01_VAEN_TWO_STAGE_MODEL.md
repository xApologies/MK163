# MK163 — vaen: two successive Genesis evolutions
**Author workshop decision:** November 8, 2026. **Status:** accepted conceptual model; realm-stage mechanics remain an interpretive proposal, not new L0 mathematics. **Target:** `WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/TECHNOLOGY/GENESIS_CRYSTALS_DUST_AND_VAEN.md` and `WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/ENVIRONMENT/GENESIS_FLORA_FAUNA_AND_VAEN.md`; add a focused owner only if needed, and cross-link from Book Red arcs.

## Definition and progression
Use protected lower-case **vaen** (dictation: vein, vane, Vane). A vaen is a naturally occurring item or a specific part of an item which, following an initial Genesis expression, undergoes a **secondary Genesis evolutionary event** realizing a **specialized identity-bearing geometry**. This is not equivalent to high energy concentration, a generic glowing object, a Genesis crystal, Genesis Dust, a newly discovered chemical element, or a standard alchemical Type 2–5 material. The secondary identity is distinct from the first-stage expression. Genesis 'evolution' is a structural/environmental development and need not mean Darwinian speciation or inheritance across generations.

**Stage 0 — native material existence in White:** fungus, flower, tree, seed, animal, mineral, shell, resin, naturally formed glass, fossil/mineralized wood, etc. Ordinary matter participates in the White Realm's superposed existence.

**Stage 1 — Genesis expression:** localized environmental geometry/pressure and field interaction alter an item's organization or properties. Genesis-expressive organisms can be adapted or dependent under the inherited biological classification. A whole fungal patch may express Genesis, yet none of its mushrooms need be vaen. Glow is neither required nor sufficient.

**Stage 2 — vaen realization:** a second Genesis-field interaction/evolution, within admissible environmental geometry, yields a particular identity-bearing structure in an entire item or a localized part. One or two mushrooms in a larger expressive patch may acquire vaen identities. The acquired geometry is specialized to the item, but comparable identities can recur under comparable conditions; not every vaen is unique worldwide, rare, or valuable.

The framework does **not** require a person to perform a Binding or a sigil operation to create natural vaen. Binding Circles may later select compatible identities from suitable vaen, but are not the universal creation mechanism.

## Examples — selected vs candidate
- **Red Arc fungus:** special mushroom resource in Kaira's Awakening Potion. Model: fungal Genesis expression followed by identity-bearing vaen formation; do not invent a new potion recipe or assert all mushrooms in a patch share the same identity.
- **Flowers:** established natural vaen category; particular blossoms may undergo second-stage identity realization.
- **Orange Arc:** Kaira's first return from the Violet Dungeon staging settlement, distinct from later Nyx adoption. Moss is under Genesis expression; translucent green mineral nodules embedded in/beneath it are **mineral vaen**, not Genesis crystals. The moss and mineral conditions interact in a localized ecology; exact causal microscopic field mechanics remain open. Her warning-color frog is Genesis-expressive fauna and **not itself vaen**.
- **Specialized seeds:** environment-grown or environment-realized seeds can become vaen after the second event; not all seeds.
- **Tree:** an entire Genesis-expressive tree can itself become vaen; alternatively bark, leaves, sap/resin, or hardened amber-like products can acquire the second-stage identity. This supersedes an earlier suggestion to categorically exclude entire trees. Ordinary roots/nodules were initially rejected as a preferred example, not established as a universal impossibility.
- **Fauna:** a deer may undergo Genesis expression, and its **antlers** can separately acquire identity-bearing vaen geometry. This supersedes the earlier broad dismissal of scales/feathers/antlers; a naturally shed or retained component may qualify where the second event is established. Do not assume the whole deer becomes a harvestable item or that the animal must be killed.
- **Shells:** tentative permissible example, not a guaranteed category.
- **Mineral concretions, deposits, nodules, stones:** permitted vaen after second-stage identity formation, including Orange Arc stones.
- **Natural glass:** a lightning–sand event with Genesis environmental interaction may produce glass that subsequently bears a vaen identity; no claim that all lightning strikes make vaen.
- **Mineralized wood/fossils:** re-admitted as conditional possibilities after earlier 50/50 discussion and provisional rejection. They may capture a Genesis interaction and later an identity-bearing geometry; not all fossils qualify.
- **Lichen:** more naturally classified as Genesis expression, not selected as a vaen example in this workshop. This is preference, not a physical prohibition.

## Identity, use and economy
A vaen can bear one or multiple compatible identities. Material substrate and acquired identity are separate descriptive dimensions. Applications include alchemy and optional Binding identity selection; don't invent universal prices, a catalog of powers, mass-to-energy conversion, or guaranteed recipes. Some resources are common and environmentally renewable, others exceptional. Identity geometry may be distributed across an entire organism or concentrated in a component. Do not conflate the Green appearance of Orange Arc mineral nodules with a compulsory Green-basin provenance.
