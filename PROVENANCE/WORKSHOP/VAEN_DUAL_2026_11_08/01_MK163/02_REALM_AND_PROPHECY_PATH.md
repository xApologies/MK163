# MK163 — interpretive realm composition for two-stage vaen formation
**Status:** author agreed to the explanatory two-stage realm pathway, **not** to a mathematically proven six-step Genesis operator. **Target:** MK163's existing vaen owners and realm/prophecy crosswalk, with references to AERA rather than copying its whole framework.

## White remains native geometry throughout
White is the material realization of six chromatic structures in superposition, **not a separate seventh ordinary chromatic basin**. A natural fungus, tree, animal or stone begins in White and stays materially realized in White during both transformations. Chromatic emphases are contributions to a compatible composition, **not physical travel through six realms or six compulsory successive spells**.

## First evolution — Genesis expression (interpretive emphasis)
- **Violet — Genesis Field:** interaction with the environment's Genesis field.
- **Green — Resolution:** an admissible Genesis-expressive configuration becomes realized.
- **Blue — Bandwidth:** capacity for additional complexity, coupling and sustained interaction.
- **Yellow — Persistence:** the expressive organization endures over an interval.
- Red Chirality and Orange Topology are **not absent**; all six structures participate in White, but their roles are less distinctive in this explanatory first-stage emphasis.
- Biological examples have especially relevant prophecy concepts **V Covenant Gardens** (relationship of magic with living existence) and **VI Living Art** (living-system expression and evolution). Nonbiological minerals do not become biological organisms because of these analogies.

**Outcome:** Genesis-expressive fungus/tree/deer/moss/mineral, which is not automatically vaen.

## Second evolution — vaen identity realization (interpretive emphasis)
- **Violet — Genesis Field:** further interaction under particular local environmental geometry.
- **Green — Resolution:** a particular admissible identity becomes realized.
- **Red — Chirality:** organization, orientation and admissibility of identity-bearing relations.
- **Orange — Topology:** coherent geometric/structural organization of the specialized identity. Its formal position relative to the existing five-node Genesis Field→Resolution→Bandwidth→Persistence→Chirality chain is **OPEN**; do not invent a new L0 node.
- **Yellow — Persistence:** the realized identity remains associated with the material or component over time.
- **Blue — Bandwidth:** constrains complexity and sustained interaction, even when not shown as a distinct sequential second-stage event.
- Relevant prophetic readings include **II The Unwritten** (meaningful/admissible expression), **IV Thousand Threads** (relations between existing identities), and **I Seven Realms** (compatibility of chromatic structures in White). **VII Cartographers** concerns spatial adjacency, not generic geometry alone; don't claim it is mandatory for every vaen.

**Outcome:** a naturally occurring item or part with a specialized, persistent, identity-bearing Genesis geometry: vaen.

## Explicit firewalls
AERA's **seven foundational prophecy concepts**, **six chromatic energy basins**, **six operational Transduction domains**, and **six-stage shaping lifecycle** are different classifications; never map them one-to-one. The Black Prophecy is singular and is not an eighth realm, ordinary vaen source, or required event. The optional denizen-prophecy crosswalk (Red Dragon→Thousand Threads, Orange Vaelith→Covenant Gardens, Yellow Primordial→Living Art, Green Sprite→Unwritten, Blue Leviathan→Borrowed Hours [weak], Violet Unicorn→Cartographers) is interpretive, not the vaen formula. Avoid reassigning these correspondences.
