First-order native form and first Genesis-expressive evolution are possible within a species. A second distinct evolution may produce identity-bearing `vaen` in whole organisms or components; it is not an ordinary mandatory third plant form. Names are working selections from author-reviewed plates; exact taxonomy, individual Genesis status, and biological mechanics remain open. 01–12, 13–24, 25–36 correspond to three plates.

## Design rules

Colossal canopy species 01–04 and 25–30 are ten distinct architectural organisms. Maintain varied silhouettes and upper/lower land growth. Genesis expression appears selectively as internal veins/channels, not blanket glow. Nytha Spire is a biological design and not necessarily mineral crystals.
