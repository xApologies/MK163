# Kaira's master-smith relationship and ring commission

## First encounter (ESTABLISHED)
Kaira visits multiple smiths: one too slow, one too expensive to haggle, one ring quality not acceptable. Row artisans may first see her as wealthy Awakened despite afflicted signs. Chosen unnamed mailwright's rings are exceptional; Kaira scrutinizes Genesis-infused iron, elastic self-closing geometry, tolerances, durability, shape recovery and weave suitability. During **first** haggling exchange she challenges him on material, process and pricing; her ability to articulate mailworking knowledge **floors him immediately**. They connect as craft peers before signing. He is Kaira's first genuine friend, not merely supplier; later becomes business mentor and confidant, teaching pricing, customer acquisition, commissions, deposits, margins. He learns from her inventive geometry too. Relationship grows closer across arcs but begins immediately, not after repeated tests. His name, rank and precise shop remain OPEN.

## Ring contract and costs
Kaira orders **300,000** Genesis-infused iron self-closing rings. Approx planning allocation: 25,000 demonstration shirt, 250,000 five-layer robe, 25,000 extra. Exact robe requirement OPEN. Existing baseline 80 eln for 23,000 rings → 300,000×80/23,000 = **1043.47826 eln**. She haggles base contract to **1000 eln** (ESTABLISHED). Assistant proposed **100 eln** additional mass-reduction enchanting and 1100 total, but author had not explicitly locked that extra price. Mark OPEN/PROPOSED. The *function* of ring enchantment is only mass reduction; no resistance to fire, magic or impacts from enchantment. Genesis-infused iron's durable/rust-resistant/self-closing properties are material characteristics, not proof of any further protective enchantment. Customers may seek separate enchanters.

## Production and pickup
Master takes personal interest, works on order himself, delegates some to a colleague rather than sending to industrial offsite shop. **11-day total** accepted; approximate pickups every 3 days, days 3,6,9 and final day 11. Exact batch sizes OPEN. Working first batch ~80,000 rings; storage chest about 50×40×30 cm (20×16×12 inches), deliberately generous; actual volume depends on ring diameter, wire and packing. Mass reduction lets Kaira carry box, bulk still inconvenient; no Dimensional Ring until Book Red end. Do not imply all 300k rings fit this one box.

## Revisit
After eight days constructing demonstration shirt (counting from first pickup), Kaira brings it to smith on her next visit, solicits opinion, discusses business and receives genuine recognition. The 'day nine' label refers to ninth day of shirt process; it is not necessarily ninth day since commission. At night she starts robe work; following day heads northwest to Violet dungeon aithren staging community with the **shirt**, not completed robe.
