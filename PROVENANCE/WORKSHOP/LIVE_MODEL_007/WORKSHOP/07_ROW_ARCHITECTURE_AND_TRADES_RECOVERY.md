# Blacksmith Row — full workshop reasoning, not a new institution

## Zoning and prestige
Three Rows exclusively in Yellow District; proposed north/west/east geographic distribution not yet pixel-mapped. Red/Orange have smaller independent smithies, sometimes conventional forges; under newest author clarification no standalone unrelated Yellow smithies. Green/Blue/Violet prohibit smithy disturbance/noise/traffic, but wealthy residents travel to Yellow for commissions. Row is analogous to Rodeo Drive: scarce high-rent commercial address, visible master craft, prestige, expensive commissions. Rank is distinct from district. Recessed upper promenade, NO overhang; large ground-floor suites, working ~300m plaza length not final. Smiths are conductors of matter through Metal/Thermal Transduction, not continuously burning furnace workers.

## Specializations — reasoning
First proposed mail, plate, swords, axes/mauls, precision, jewelers, sigil-substrate, metallurgy. Author rejected dedicated metallurgy: alchemists synthesize advanced metals; elite smiths know metallurgy and stock Genesis-infused alloys. Rejected dedicated sigil-substrate: any smith can carve ru’ne for an object; enchanters configure effects. Conventional locksmith family uncertain because sigil doors can be locked by placing sigil-engraved cube on an anchor. Jewelers likely elsewhere in city, but ceremonial/artistic metalwork appropriate. Basic hinges, nails, agriculture tools, mass machinery generally made outside prestige Row. Precision smiths accepted as one-off intricate work, not mass fasteners. Fine bespoke hinges/doors/chest hardware fit high-end commissions.

## Workshop families, not numerical tenant quotas
Mailwrights: chainmail rings, shirts, exotic weaves.
Armorers: plate, articulated, composite armor.
Weaponsmiths: swords, massive swords, axes, mauls, polearms.
Architectural metalworkers: intricate wrought-iron gates, fences, balconies, railings, structural ornaments; larger shops for storage.
Furniture/domestic smiths: custom metal dining tables/chairs, four-poster beds, frames, chandeliers/fittings; wood scarcity makes metal standard.
Artistic/ceremonial smiths: sculpture, monuments, ceremonial items, premium decoration.
Precision/instrument smiths: high-end specialized mechanical or scientific/alchemical apparatus, final role and allocation open.
Categories overlap. Prior proposed shop-count distributions (16/16/16/12/4 or 8/10/10/8/10/8/6/4 or 5/6/6/4/4/4/3) are assistant brainstorming, NOT approved distribution. No compulsory smithy counts by category.
