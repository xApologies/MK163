# Kaira's weave, Metal Manipulation and construction timeline

## Proprietary shirt weave
Author approved **Offset-Lattice Weave** as Kaira's signature mailwork, developed through years of smithy labor and mental design without paper/drawings. Compared with European 4-in-1 and 6-in-1, intended benefits: dense coverage, controlled flexibility, alternative load paths, close fit; tradeoffs include tighter tolerances, complexity, more labor/material and difficult repair. **Do not assert empirically verified superiority or universal armor protection**. Mathematical concept: primary P(i,j)=(i,j), secondary Q(i,j)=(i+1/2,j+1/2), each interior Q connects to P(i,j),P(i+1,j),P(i,j+1),P(i+1,j+1). This is only an abstract connectivity graph: physical ring crossings, interference, over-under order, diameter and elasticity are UNVALIDATED. Distinguish author approval of design *concept* from proof of physical realization.

## Five-layer robe

Opaque, gray-black, athletic body-hugging silhouette, prominent hood, no flared skirt; garment-first, not primarily protection-first. Flexibility, thickness, opacity and drape are separate properties. Mass reduction mitigates weight, not automatically stiffness or drape.
