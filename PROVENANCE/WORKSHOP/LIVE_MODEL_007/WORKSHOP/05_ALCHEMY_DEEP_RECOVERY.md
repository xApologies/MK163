## Physical setup
The alchemist's rectangular workbench is a binding circuit with six chromatic crystal anchors feeding a central vessel/target through an orthogonal sigil. The circuit need not literally be circular. A beaker containing water is one possible receiving medium. **Ru’ne are the individual carved corridors that make up the sigil**, not arbitrary block art, decorative glyphs or dissolved ingredients. Crystals alone may drive a circuit. Dust is **optional**, generally sprinkled into the corresponding ru’ne channels to stabilize energy transfer, especially at high throughput, toggling or near-complete discharge. A red channel may be reinforced with red dust. Dust is NOT normally put directly into the beaker. In some special recipes dust enters the material or liquid. White Genesis Dust is a special superposition-structuring case and can be directly added. Do not turn optional dust into a mandatory ingredient.

## Identity and material science
Vaen are naturally occurring identity-bearing specialty resources (AERA/MK147 authority); binding circuits can select and resolve compatible inheritable identities. Vaen are not necessarily chemical elements or obligatory reagents. Type 1 ordinary chemistry; Types 2–5 Genesis-mediated engineered material categories. Illustration: a field isolates helium-4 from atmosphere and cools it into superfluid conditions (under appropriate conditions below ~2.17 K); iron and green dust participate in a fictional Type 2 stable liquid-iron synthesis. Helium is a controlled intermediate, not necessarily a constituent of the finished material. Another example: R/O/G crystals infuse iron to improve durability, hardness, corrosion resistance. Exact recipe and classification not finalized. The author wants materials and phase control, not an additional invented universal operator or a new physics textbook.

## Awakening
