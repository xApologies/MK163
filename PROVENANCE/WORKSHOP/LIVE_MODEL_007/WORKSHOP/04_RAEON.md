# Raeon narrative and formats (MK163 delta; _raeon authoritative for game mechanics)
Community ethos: strategic ability, tactics, creativity and sportsmanship take precedence over birthright/nobility/wealth at table, though inequality not magically erased. This is major Arc Two emotional thread.
APPROVED workshop format distinction: STANDARD current published cycle only for sanctioned tournament; OPEN cards from any published cycle, casual/organized. Both retain inherited 60-card deck and copy rules. Shops sell old and current inventory; example dollar card prices are illustrative, NOT eln pricing.
Tournament: six-day cadence, signups days 1–5, tournament day 6, variable ~30–50 entrants, head-to-head single elimination; seven awarded ranks Red through White (White first), other participants no prizes. STANDARD Red–Violet old-cycle card prizes, White current-cycle card; OPEN all seven older-cycle card prizes. Ranking eliminated players beyond winner needs no narrative detail; administrative tiebreak OPEN. Entry one Orange ahm and house-deck rental one Red ahm were floated as examples; do not assert fixed canon fees without confirmation.
Three house-deck ARCHETYPES (not formats): Tempo aggressive Degrade/early fields; Control Restoration/sustained emergent fields; Utility/Universal draw/selection/flexibility. House decks used for free/ordinary practice with assistant on slow day; rented for tournament. Cards durable maege glass.
