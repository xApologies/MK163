# Delta 005 — decisions, illustrative values, corrections, and open parameters

## Locked author decisions
- MK163 inherits AERA mechanics but stores its own realization, not duplicated source files.
- The user requires a repository-aligned exhaustive live model, non-destructive reverse diff, Codex handoff, and source provenance.
- A large city is 10×10 miles, with nested square Red/Orange/Yellow/Green/Blue/Violet rings and White civic center.
- The original city map key has 26 exact named layers; three additional functions were approved, total 29.
- Misc Services is intentional flexible zoning, not a requirement for a fixed miscellaneous business.
- Every scene's mapped route, travel time, stops and encountered businesses must be consistent with the persistent map.
- Kaira's eventual apartment is NW Orange, a little east of NW corner and not directly at a northern gate.
- Three principal Blacksmith Rows in Yellow; Blacksmith Row is a professional artisan plaza rather than an industrial forge district.
- Ground-level smithy suites are larger; upper suites are recessed behind promenades, without an overhang.
- Six civic artisan categories were approved, distinct from AERA's six operational Transduction domains.
- General alchemical tables use permanent sigils with reconfigurable modes and tactile ER foam controls.
- Energy transfer rate is adjusted during alchemical preparation; wrong sequencing or excessive rates can damage channels.
- Alchemical houses may protect recipes, table designs and operational methods.
- There is no need to create a crystal-specialist profession.
- Alchemical paste bonds to matter and takes on substrate identity, can restore damaged stone, and does not itself reconstruct lost sigil mathematics.
- Paste requires skilled alchemical Transduction and costly Genesis materials, with controlled professional distribution.
- The king's agricultural legacy involves velis growth fields plus enriched soil.
- The six MK163 domai exterior radii are 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17 miles and working internal linear scale ×1.7.
- ELDRIS permitted classes accumulate through the domai's chromatic class.
- Crown military involvement normally begins with Yellow domai.
- Crown dominates White material supply by expedition capability, but private trade and reserves exist.
- Awakening Potions are extraordinary in cost and primarily available to nobility, not legally exclusive to them.
- Affliction is rare genetic biology and may affect noble/royal or commoner children.
- Kaira is unmistakably Awakened with retained Afflicted pigmentation and scars.
- Orange District residents begin speculating that she is estranged from an unknown noble family.
- Rumors reach noble circles; her Chainmail Robe/fashion identity magnifies recognition; presumed noble origins recur through seven books.
- Kaira's usual breakfast is fruit at home; she generally buys lunch and dinner because she lacks cooking experience.
- Arc One's revised collapse, Stone Hollow alchemist, return and caravan sequence supersedes the older five-beat ordering.
- Arc Two begins at first apartment haggling; ends with first Chainmail Robe completion and public reveal.
- Nyx is adopted around third dungeon return, not the Black Sprite. First Black Sprite Binding is Arc Three.

## Explicitly illustrative, NOT fixed universal rules
- Blacksmith Row length around 300 meters.
- Eight to sixteen suites per side per tier, 32–64 total per Row.
- 60–65% up to 100% of smithing process via Transduction by rank/skill.
- Two or three major kingdom-level Transduction-equipment manufacturers.
- Ten to one hundred alchemists in a large proprietary house.
- Alchemical table lifespan around a century.
- Red fader 50%, Green fader about two-thirds; example of control setting.
- Alchemical process around one hour.
- Paste possession could lead to a month's jail.
- Three/five/seven harvests per season.
- Five-mile city–dungeon separation until map reference points settled.
- Dungeon hub 1×1 or 2×2 miles.
- Three to four weeks for a long spatial-corridor caravan journey; 300–500-mile corridor hops.
- Kaira's frugal savings estimate around three years without additional income.
- Red crystal sold at roughly 60% value in Stone Hollow.
- City nighttime darkening near 2100 in 31-hour day.
