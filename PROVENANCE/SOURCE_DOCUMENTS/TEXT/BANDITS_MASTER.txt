GENESIS POINT — BANDITS, WILDS, AND DOMAI SUPPRESSION SYSTEM 
MASTER OVERVIEW (CANON LOCK-IN) 
I. GEOGRAPHIC & SOCIETAL LAYOUT 
1. Sanctuaries 
   - Three primary floating cities. 
   - Each sanctuary is a large black disc city. 
   - Arranged in a deliberate triangular configuration in the sky. 
   - Contain noble tiers (upper/lower) and commoner populations beneath them on the ground. 
   - Sanctuaries do NOT individually project Domai suppression. 
2. Central Hub Disc 
   - A smaller black disc located at the exact geometric center between the three sanctuary 
discs. 
   - Approximate scale: multi-mile radius (order-of-magnitude ~5 miles). 
   - Unpopulated or minimally staffed. 
   - Exists solely to support the Sanctuary Ring. 
   - Considered critical infrastructure, not a city. 
3. The Wilds 
   - All land outside the sanctuary suppression bubble. 
   - Unclaimed, unstable, and dangerous. 
   - Primary territory for bandits, cartels, and roaming threats. 
   - Domai formations and chimera events may occur naturally here. 
   - Travel between sanctuaries necessarily passes through the Wilds. 
------------------------------------------------------------ 
II. SANCTUARY DOMAI SUPPRESSION SYSTEM 
4. Sanctuary Ring (Primary Suppression Engine) 
   - A massive circular ring suspended above the central hub disc. 
   - Orientation: perfectly horizontal (parallel to the ground). 
   - Diameter: multiple miles. 
   - Engraved continuously with runes along its surface. 
   - Slowly rotates in place (“lazy rotation”). 
   - Rotation is constant and functional, not decorative. 
   - Locked in position relative to the hub disc (does not orbit or drift). 
5. Sanctuary Suppression Bubble 
   - A single unified suppression field projected by the Sanctuary Ring. 
   - Encompasses: 
     - All three sanctuary cities. 
     - The airspace between them. 
     - The ground far below. 
   - Prevents the conditions required for Domai formation from stabilizing. 
   - Does NOT destroy Genesis energy. 
   - Does NOT react to Domai. 
   - Instead, prevents Domai nucleation entirely within the radius. 
   - Sanctuaries are collectively stabilized; none are independently protected. 
6. Functional Philosophy (Sanctuary Scale) 
   - Preventative, not reactive.

   - Harmonizing rather than resisting Genesis pressure. 
   - Creates an area where Domai “never finish forming.” 
   - Produces a calm, timeless, stable environment. 
   - The sanctuaries orbit safety; safety does not orbit them. 
------------------------------------------------------------ 
III. BANDITS OF THE WILDS 
7. Bandits (General Role) 
   - Persistent nuisance threat during travel between sanctuaries. 
   - Primary danger for caravans and long-distance movement. 
   - Force travelers to hire guards and plan logistics. 
   - Serve as narrative pressure during long journeys. 
   - Tactics include: 
     - Road obstructions (logs, collapsed bridges). 
     - Ambushes. 
     - Night raids. 
     - Kidnapping for ransom (especially nobles). 
     - Theft of cargo and supplies. 
8. Bandit Settlements 
   - Small, hidden towns or camps located in the Wilds. 
   - Designed to be temporary and relocatable. 
   - Camouflaged and difficult to locate. 
   - Not permanent cities. 
   - Rotate locations depending on Domai activity and external pressure. 
   - Abandoned settlements may remain intact for years. 
------------------------------------------------------------ 
IV. BANDIT DOMAI SUPPRESSION SYSTEM 
9. Portable Suppression Cubes (Bandit-Class Constructs) 
   - Cube-shaped rune constructs. 
   - Portable (wagon-transportable). 
   - Each cube has six rune-etched faces. 
   - Smaller, cruder, and less elegant than sanctuary systems. 
   - High value, difficult to replace. 
   - Illegal or restricted outside sanctuary authority. 
10. Cube Function 
   - Each cube prevents FUTURE Domai formation within a limited local area. 
   - Cubes do NOT: 
     - Cause Domai to form. 
     - Increase Genesis pressure. 
     - Trigger immediate events when destroyed. 
   - Cubes act as preventative exclusion devices, not suppression engines. 
11. Triangulated Protection Zones 
   - Bandits typically deploy 2–6 cubes around a settlement. 
   - Overlapping fields create a protected town zone. 
   - Precise placement matters. 
   - Protection exists only while cubes are active and aligned.

12. Cube Removal or Destruction 
   - Removal/destruction does NOT cause immediate Domai formation. 
   - Area simply reverts to baseline Wilds conditions. 
   - Domai may or may not form there in the future. 
   - Risk is probabilistic, not guaranteed. 
   - Allows: 
     - Safe dismantling by hunters or militaries. 
     - Abandonment without instant catastrophe. 
     - Long-term escalation arcs instead of instant events. 
------------------------------------------------------------ 
V. DOMAI BEHAVIOR (ESTABLISHED RULES) 
13. Domai Characteristics 
   - Structured, not random. 
   - Sporadic in occurrence. 
   - Can be small or large. 
   - Require specific conditions to stabilize. 
   - Do not appear everywhere all the time. 
14. Domai Escalation 
   - Small Domai may appear independently. 
   - Larger Domai may expand over time. 
   - Separate Domai can merge. 
   - Merged Domai create large regional danger zones. 
   - The largest Domai become multi-year campaign-scale events. 
15. Relationship to Suppression Systems 
   - Sanctuary Ring: prevents Domai from ever forming locally. 
   - Bandit Cubes: prevent future Domai from initiating locally. 
   - Removal of protection does not create Domai; it only removes safety. 
------------------------------------------------------------ 
VI. BANDIT HUNTERS / ADVENTURERS 
16. Bandit Hunters 
   - Specialized adventurers focused on hunting bandits. 
   - Always-open bounties issued by: 
     - Sanctuaries. 
     - Merchant coalitions. 
     - Noble houses. 
   - High-risk, high-reward profession. 
   - Targets include: 
     - Bandit leaders. 
     - Camps. 
     - Suppression cubes. 
     - Supply routes. 
17. Narrative Role 
   - Maintain road viability without eliminating danger. 
   - Create recurring rivalries.

VII. DESIGN & THEMATIC CONTRAST 
18. Sanctuary System 
   - Centralized. 
   - Permanent. 
   - Elegant. 
   - Continuous. 
   - Preventative. 
   - Harmonized with Genesis. 
19. Bandit System 
   - Distributed. 
   - Temporary. 
   - Utilitarian. 
   - Discrete. 
   - Conditional. 
   - Resistant to Genesis. 
------------------------------------------------------------ 
VIII. CANON STATUS 
20. Canon Lock Notes 
   - Sanctuary Ring is horizontal, not vertical. 
   - Ring rotates slowly and continuously. 
   - Ring + hub disc create a shared suppression bubble. 
   - Bandit cubes do not cause instant Domai formation when destroyed. 
   - Domai escalation is slow, structured, and contextual. 
   - Wilds danger is systemic, not scripted.
