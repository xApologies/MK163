══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
═════════ 
PURPOSE 
The King's Tournament exists for three reasons: 
1. Provide a kingdom-wide competitive framework. 
2. Move characters naturally between cities. 
3. Establish regional and kingdom recognition. 
The tournament is NOT: 
- Arena combat. 
- PvP elimination. 
- A standard fantasy tournament. 
- A dungeon-clearing competition. 
The tournament is effectively: 
    "A very dangerous dungeon racing circuit." 
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
═════════ 
DUNGEON MODEL 
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
═════════ 
Dungeons are NOT levels. 
Dungeons are NOT cleared. 
Dungeons are NOT destroyed. 
Dungeons are persistent environmental structures. 
Properties: 
- Massive in scale. 
- Oscillating. 
- Regenerating. 
- Resource producing. 
- Continuously inhabited by monsters. 
- Continuously changing. 
A dungeon may extend: 
- Miles vertically. 
- Tens of miles vertically. 
- 17+ miles deep or greater. 
The dungeon remains after the tournament. 
The dungeon remains after adventurers leave. 
The dungeon remains after centuries. 
The tournament merely operates inside the dungeon.

PHASE 2 
KING'S TOURNAMENT 
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
═════════ 
When qualification season ends: 
Each of the 9 qualification dungeons contributes: 
    6 qualified teams 
Result: 
    9 × 6 
    = 54 Teams 
The Blue Region Tournament begins.

TOURNAMENT STRUCTURE 
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
═════════ 
The tournament is NOT elimination. 
The tournament is a points circuit. 
All 54 teams remain active. 
The field does not collapse each stage. 
Instead: 
Teams accumulate points.
