MK117 — BOOK 1 STRUCTURAL ARC OUTLINE
ARC 1 — THE VILLAGE HUB
(~Chapters 1–8)
Core Identity:
Confinement, labor, survival, awakening, escape.
This arc establishes:
- Enix’s life as an indentured servant to the blacksmith
- the village hub structure
- the wilds and civilization boundary
- eldren danger
- the Genesis field awakening
- Enix’s decision to leave her old life behind
CHAPTER 1 — THE SALVE
Enix travels through the village hub to the apothecary to obtain salve for her damaged fingertips from years of
chainmail labor. The chapter establishes her daily life, emotional isolation, and how the village quietly reacts to
her unusual appearance.
CHAPTER 2 — THE SMITHY
The reality of Enix’s labor is shown. She works endlessly producing chainmail rings and armor components. Her
mastery is already visible, but she is treated more as a tool than a person.
CHAPTER 3 — THE DELIVERY ROUTE
Enix and the apprentice leave the village on a delivery route. This is one of the few times Enix experiences
freedom outside the smithy. The chapter introduces the outer roads and the feeling of leaving civilization behind.
CHAPTER 4 — BEYOND THE BOUNDARY
The story introduces the wilds, stabilized territory, and the environmental danger surrounding civilization. Enix
experiences fascination with the wilderness and the unknown outside the protected zones.
CHAPTER 5 — THE CRITICAL RESOLUTION EVENT
A Genesis field collapse occurs. Eldren manifest during the event. Chaos erupts and Enix awakens to the
Genesis field during the crisis.


=== PDF PAGE 2 ===

CHAPTER 6 — AFTERMATH
Enix struggles with the consequences of awakening and her changing perception of the world. She realizes she
cannot remain in the village forever.
CHAPTER 7 — THE ROAD TO THE CITY
Enix leaves the village and travels alone toward the city. During the journey she begins unconsciously
interacting with the Genesis field, repeatedly forming and collapsing simple ring structures.
CHAPTER 8 — ARRIVAL
Enix arrives in the city, opens a bank account, rents her first apartment, and experiences true freedom for the
first time in her life.
ARC 2 — THE ORANGE DISTRICT
(~Chapters 8–25)
Core Identity:
Freedom, routine, exploration, belonging.
This arc establishes:
- Enix’s routine in the city
- the Orange District culture
- the abeca house
- the RAEON card game
- Genesis card collecting culture
- artifact shops
- blacksmith row
- Enix’s local mystique
- the beginning of her friendships
The city itself becomes the primary focus of the arc.
CHAPTER 9 — THE FIRST MORNING
Enix wakes up in complete silence with nobody controlling her schedule for the first time in her life.
CHAPTER 10 — THE DISTRICT
Enix explores the Orange District and experiences city life, public shaping, commerce, food stalls, and the
layered social structure of urban civilization.


=== PDF PAGE 3 ===

CHAPTER 11 — THE ABECA HOUSE
Enix discovers the local abeca house and forms her first emotional connection in the city through the barista who
remembers her order.
CHAPTER 12 — THE RESTAURANT
Enix realizes she does not know how to cook and develops a routine of eating at the same local restaurant while
unconsciously maintaining survival habits from her old life.
CHAPTER 13 — THE CARD SHOP
Enix discovers RAEON and becomes fascinated with the strategy game. Her unusual strategic thinking
immediately begins attracting attention.
CHAPTER 14 — THE GENESIS GALLERY
Enix explores collectible Genesis cards and becomes obsessed with the idea of owning a fireball card despite
the extreme cost.
CHAPTER 15 — THE ARTIFICER
Enix visits the local artificer shop and begins theorycrafting artifact combinations she cannot yet afford.
CHAPTER 16 — ROUTINE
Enix settles into a repeating routine between the abeca house, card shop, Genesis gallery, artificer, and
restaurant.
CHAPTER 17 — GOSSIP
Rumors spread throughout the district about the strange pale noble girl living alone in the Orange District.
CHAPTER 18 — BLACKSMITH ROW
Enix visits Blacksmith Row and shocks local smiths with her understanding of chainmail craftsmanship and
pricing.
CHAPTER 19 — THE RINGS
Enix orders specialized chainmail rings and begins discussing robe concepts with the blacksmith.
CHAPTER 20 — THE CARD PLAYER
Enix’s reputation in the local RAEON scene rapidly grows due to her unusual strategic playstyle.
CHAPTER 21 — THE SHOPKEEPERS
The district shopkeepers quietly begin discussing Enix among themselves as her reputation spreads through the
local community.
CHAPTER 22 — THE OUTSIDER
Despite her growing familiarity in the district, Enix realizes people still keep an emotional distance from her due
to her appearance and assumed noble status.


=== PDF PAGE 4 ===

CHAPTER 23 — THE DESIGN
Enix begins constructing the first chainmail robe.
CHAPTER 24 — THE HOOD
Enix completes major sections of the robe and becomes emotionally attached to its design and silhouette.
CHAPTER 25 — THE FIRST ROBE
Enix publicly wears the chainmail robe for the first time, beginning the cultural reaction that will eventually spread
throughout the city.
ARC 3 — THE ROBE MOVEMENT
(~Chapters 25–50)
Core Identity:
Recognition, expression, cultural emergence.
This arc establishes:
- the rise of the Enix robe
- the beginning of Enix’s business
- the robe as a fashion and cultural icon
- Enix becoming socially visible
- the spread of robe culture
- the “original Enix robe” identity
- the spectral robe event
- the arrival of the Alchemist
## Major Arc 3 Developments
- Blacksmith Row becomes obsessed with the robe craftsmanship.
- Enix receives her first commissions.
- The robe spreads throughout the district and eventually the city.
- Enix establishes a workshop in the Yellow District.
- Original Enix robes become status symbols.
- Knockoff robes begin appearing.
- The mage class becomes irritated by the cultural movement.


=== PDF PAGE 5 ===

- Enix purchases her first Genesis fireball card.
- Enix’s red RAEON deck identity becomes established.
- Enix’s emotional breakdown during a late-night work session causes a Genesis-field outburst that alters a robe
with spectral Genesis-infused striations.
- The spectral robe creates a second wave of cultural fascination.
- Painted robes and colored-ring variants begin appearing throughout the city.
- Enix’s nervous ring-creation habit becomes a recurring subconscious shaping behavior.
- Enix begins experimenting with manifold seam coupling and robe articulation.
- The Alchemist arrives as a mysterious wealthy customer seeking an original robe.
ARC 3 ENDPOINT
The Alchemist notices Enix’s eyes and immediately realizes she is awakened — but does not match any known
awakening profile.
This begins the Alchemist’s quiet obsession with understanding what Enix truly is.
ARC 4 — THE ALCHEMIST
(~Chapters 50–70)
Core Identity:
Friendship, social ascent, emotional opening, hidden protection.
This arc establishes:
- Enix’s relationship with the Alchemist
- the Alchemist’s fascination with Enix
- noble social structures
- the Alchemist’s quiet political protection of Enix
- the transition toward academy life
## Major Arc 4 Developments
- The Alchemist repeatedly visits Enix’s workshop during the crafting of her custom robe.
- Enix slowly opens up emotionally for the first time in her life.
- The Alchemist initially believes Enix’s village story is fabricated.
- Over time, the Alchemist realizes Enix is telling the truth.


=== PDF PAGE 6 ===

- The Alchemist becomes quietly protective of Enix while also recognizing her immense social and intellectual
value.
- The Alchemist shields Enix from noble attention by socially aligning her under her own influence.
- Enix becomes friends with the Princess through the Alchemist.
- The Princess introduces Enix to the King’s RAEON nights.
- The King becomes obsessed with understanding Enix’s strategic thinking.
- The Alchemist offers Enix sponsorship to the Royal Healing Academy after learning Enix originally dreamed of
becoming an apothecary like her mother.
ARC 4 ENDPOINT
Enix leaves for the capital city to begin formal healing education.
ARC 5 — THE ACADEMY
(~Chapters 70–95)
Core Identity:
Education, struggle, formalization, conceptual understanding.
This arc establishes:
- Enix’s healing education
- mathematics
- anatomy
- biology
- chemistry
- organic chemistry
- topology
- MK43 Ultra mathematics
- conceptual shaping foundations
## Major Arc 5 Developments
- Enix struggles heavily with formal mathematics despite her intuitive field understanding.
- Professors become frustrated because Enix often arrives at correct conclusions through impossible logic.
- The Princess helps bridge the gap between Enix’s intuition and formal academic structure.
- Enix studies anatomy, biology, chemistry, and organic chemistry to understand healing structurally.


=== PDF PAGE 7 ===

- Healing is established as conceptual reconstruction rather than generic healing magic.
- Enix’s shaping style becomes highly effective due to her understanding of manifolds, continuity, and structural
systems.
- Enix gradually learns to formally manipulate AERA.
ARC 5 ENDPOINT
Enix graduates from the academy but remains largely unrecognized professionally.
ARC 6 — THE HOSPITAL
(~Chapters 95–105)
Core Identity:
Consequence, practical healing, exhaustion, proving competence.
This arc establishes:
- real-world healing pressure
- hospital dynamics
- triage
- emotional burden
- Enix’s unconventional medical effectiveness
## Major Arc 6 Developments
- The Alchemist secures Enix an entry-level hospital position.
- Enix struggles socially and professionally at first.
- Enix develops a reputation for unconventional recoveries and impossible saves.
- Senior healers slowly begin recognizing her unusual talent.
ARC 6 ENDPOINT
An Adventurer Guild takes notice of Enix’s healing ability.
ARC 7 — THE OFFER
(~Chapters 105–110)
Core Identity:


=== PDF PAGE 8 ===

Recognition, transition, expansion into the wider world.
This arc establishes:
- Enix’s transition from civilization into expeditionary operations
- the beginning of the wider world story
- the next phase of her life
## Major Arc 7 Developments
- The Adventurer Guild approaches Enix to serve as a healer for a DOMAI expedition.
- Enix is recognized not because of fame, but because she became useful where it mattered.
- The offer represents the first step into the larger world beyond the cities.
# BOOK 1 ENDPOINT
Book 1 ends with Enix accepting a position as a field healer attached to a DOMAI assault expedition.
The story transitions from:
civilization and identity formation
into:
the frontier, the wilds, eldren, and expeditionary life.
