# World Clock constitution

- L0_MATHEMATICS: foundational mathematical descriptions and provenance.
- L1_FANTASY: Genesis Field, physics, biology, Transduction and related fantasy framework.
- L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION: starscape, geography, ecology, infrastructure, society and institutions.
- L3_CHARACTERS_EVENTS: identities, relationships, events, world-state changes.
- L4_NARRATIVE_REALIZATION: cross-series realization rules and story interpretation, **not duplicate manuscript prose**.

Each downstream layer inherits constraints of upstream layers. Store MK163-specific deltas, indexed upstream references, and verified snapshots only when necessary; do not mirror the entirety of AERA. Explicitly label inherited / author-established / proposed / unresolved.
